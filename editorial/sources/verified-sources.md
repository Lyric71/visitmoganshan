# Verified source ledger

Every figure used in a published piece, with the citation that goes with it.

**Read this before researching anything.** If a figure is here, within its
shelf life and verified twice, reuse the exact citation below. That is what
keeps the same number from appearing three different ways across 174 pieces.

**Append to this file before you finish a draft.** A figure used and not
logged will be researched again in three days.

## How to log an entry

```
### <the figure, in plain words>
* Value: <number and unit, RMB for money>
* As of: <month year the source states, not the date you found it>
* Source: <publisher in English (中文)>
* Tier: <1 to 4, per source-tiers.md>
* URL: <link>
* Verified 1: <date of check 1: page fetched, figure, unit, period and date confirmed>
* Verified 2: <date of check 2: page fetched again before the draft was finished>
* Shelf life: <volatile: 3 months | semi-stable: 6 months | evergreen: 12 months>
* Used in: <slug>, <slug>
* Notes: <anything that limits how it can be used; where sources disagree, log each and say so>
```

## Rules

1. **A source with no date does not go in this file.** Find the date or drop
   the figure.
2. **A source with one check does not go in this file.** Every entry carries
   two verification dates.
3. **An OTA is never the source for a price, a closure or a policy.** Tier 4
   entries are logged only as "announced" and say so in Notes.
4. **Disagreement is logged, not resolved.** Where two tier 1 or tier 2
   sources give different figures, both are entries, cross referenced in
   Notes, and the piece publishes the disagreement.
5. **Our own work is logged separately**, in the section below, with the
   method, the date and who did it. It is cited as ours, never as a
   published source.
6. **Recheck anything past its shelf life** before reusing it. Move stale
   entries to the retired section rather than deleting them, so a reader who
   asks where a published number came from can still be answered.
