### Chain deviation, decided and published

`sources/source-tiers.md` routes visitor numbers to the Huzhou or Zhejiang
bureau only, on the ground that county and marketing sources inflate. Both
holiday counts this piece uses are county figures relayed by the press. No
bureau release carries the county and scenic area split the article turns on.
A search for a Huzhou municipal cross check produced one candidate, 潮新闻
3435962, which on fetch turned out to be a Hangzhou figure, not Huzhou, and
was discarded rather than used.

Cutting the claim under SPEC condition 1 would have removed the spine of the
piece, and the piece is not blocked under condition 2 because its central
figures, the holiday dates and the crowd calendar, are tier 1 and tier 2 and
hold. So the figures are published as what they are: the article names them
as county figures in a municipal paper rather than a bureau release, says
county numbers run high, and states the direction of the bias, which runs in
the argument's favour since inflating the county total only shrinks the
scenic area's share of it. Both ledger entries carry the deviation in full
and say the numbers must not be reused as bureau counts.
