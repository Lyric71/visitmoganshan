The tea item was read against its source again (22 September Deqing News,
fetched through curl and iconv from GB18030).

* The draft said one of the four new grades is "picked in autumn". The source
  lists the grades as 屋脊头, 知春, 梅笺 and 秋白 and never says any of them
  is an autumn pick; the claim was an inference from the name 秋白 (autumn
  white). Cut, under the no fabricated figures rule, and replaced with two of
  the grade names: "Four yellow teas beyond the single bud are now sold,
  among them Zhichun (知春) and Qiubai (秋白)."
* The visitor line said our tea page "still treats the pick as a spring crop
  ... and that needs correcting". The page is corrected in this same commit,
  so the line now says it "has been corrected today".

target. Rescanned: no em dash, no en dash, no spaced hyphen, no `/go/` link.
