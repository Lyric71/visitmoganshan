Registration is open for the UTO trail training race in Moganshan on 6
October 2026. The Zuicool (最酷) entry page gives a 07:30 start from Yucun
Square (庾村广场), at the foot of the mountain.

There is one division. The stated route is 22.65 km with 1,379 metres of
climbing. Runners have six hours, with the course closing at 13:30. The page
describes it as a training event for runners attempting their first 100 km
trail race rather than a mass programme with several distances.

Entry costs RMB 218 until 10:00 on 15 September, then RMB 258. The platform
says registration can close as soon as places are filled, so the later price
is not a guarantee that entries will remain available. Zuicool is the selling
platform and is authoritative for the date, entry status and fee.

The event page bills it as a National Day holiday race. It does not
publish a road closure notice or a spectator plan. A visitor entering the race
can save RMB 40 by acting before the early rate ends. Anyone staying around
Yucun should expect the square to be busy before the 07:30 start, but should
wait for a police or organiser notice before assuming any road will close.
