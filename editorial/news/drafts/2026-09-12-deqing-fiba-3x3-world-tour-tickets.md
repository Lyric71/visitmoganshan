Tickets are now on sale for the Deqing stop of the FIBA 3x3 World Tour,
which will run on 26 and 27 September 2026. Deqing News (德清新闻网)
reported the sale on 9 September. FIBA’s own calendar confirms the two dates.

The tournament will be held at the three on three basketball arena in Deqing
Geographic Information Town (德清地理信息小镇). Deqing News says 14 teams and
56 players are due to compete. The published field includes the Serbian team
Ub, the 2025 World Tour champion, and the host team from Deqing.

The county has tied the event to a ticket stub offer. Spectators will be able
to show the stub for offers at Deqing Intime City and at four visitor areas:
Xinshi Ancient Town, the Xiazhu Lake wetland park, Xiazhu Lake Fantasy Valley
and the Moganshan International Tourism Resort. Hotel and restaurant offers
are also promised. The report does not give the value, conditions or expiry
of any offer, so those details need checking in the ticket channel before a
booking is made.

Deqing News says tickets are available through the official booking channel,
but its article does not link to that channel or publish a price. A visitor
who wants to attend can now book the tournament and use the stub locally, but
should confirm the exact offer attached to the selected ticket before planning
the rest of the trip.
