The 2026 Kailas Moganshan Skyrace (凯乐石莫干山跑山赛) is set for 23 to 25
October. Letour Sports (朗途体育), the organiser, now marks every entry route
on its event page as closed.

The page lists adult distances of 10 km, 20 km, 30 km, 50 km, 70 km and 100
km, plus a youth 10 km category. It also gives different experience
requirements for the longer courses. Those details confirm a full race
weekend rather than a single short trail start.

The listing does not publish a start map, spectator plan or road restrictions.
Those operational notices usually appear closer to the race and need an
organiser or police source before they can be treated as fact. Registration
being closed also means the page should not be read as an invitation to buy a
late place through an unofficial seller.

For a visitor who is not already entered, the useful fact is the weekend. A
large multi distance trail event can put pressure on village rooms, pickups
and the narrow roads around trail access points. Anyone booking Moganshan for
23 to 25 October should ask the property where runners will gather and how its
pickup will operate once the official traffic notice appears.
