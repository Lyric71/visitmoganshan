## Rain is forecast for all three race days

The national forecast for Deqing, issued on Wednesday 21 October, has rain on
Friday, Saturday and Sunday. Thursday stays cloudy at 15 to 21 °C. Friday 23
October starts with light rain and turns moderate, 16 to 21 °C. Saturday is
moderate rain easing to light, 18 to 22 °C. Sunday is light rain, 18 to 22 °C.
Monday 26 October is the first dry day, cloudy then sunny.

The National Meteorological Centre (中央气象台) gives the same wet days, with lows
up to two degrees higher. Both forecasts are for the county town. The mountain
is higher and usually cooler.

**What this means for a visitor:** the stone paths will be wet from Friday to
Sunday, so pack a rain layer and shoes with grip, and move any long walk to
Monday if you can.

> October 2026, tier 1. https://www.weather.com.cn/weather/101210204.shtml

> Source: 中央气象台 (National Meteorological Centre), Deqing forecast issued
> 21 October 2026, tier 1. http://www.nmc.cn/publish/forecast/AZJ/deqing.html

## The Skyrace starts on Friday evening in Yucun

The [Kailas Moganshan Skyrace](/things-to-do/kailas-moganshan-skyrace/)
(凯乐石莫干山跑山赛) starts and finishes on Yuxin North Street (庾信北街) in
[Yucun](/moganshan/villages/yucun/). The 100 km field leaves at 18:00 on
Friday and the 70 km at 19:00, so runners climb through the night. The 50 km
starts at 06:00 on Saturday and the 20 km at 08:30. The 30 km goes at 06:00 on
Sunday, the two 10 km races at 09:00 and 09:30, and the last cut off is 16:00
on Sunday.

On 21 October every race shuttle on the organiser's page was marked closed,
including the RMB 15 seats from Deqing station and Deqing West. Mogan Yujie
(莫干庾街), the shopping street in Yucun, is holding a street party over the same
three days, with a market, workshops, a music night and an outdoor fashion
show on Saturday. The street's post, carried by the county paper, puts the
field at 15,000 runners. That figure is the street's; the organiser has
published none.

> Source: 朗途体育 (Letour Sports), the organiser, 2026 race rules, 9
> February 2026, tier 3. https://moganshan.saihuitong.com/article?id=69954&mid=57201

> Source: 朗途体育 (Letour Sports), event page, every shuttle marked 报名已截止,
> read 21 October 2026, tier 3. https://moganshan.saihuitong.com/events?mid=72300

> Source: 德清新闻网 (Deqing News), carrying a post by 莫干庾街 (Mogan Yujie),
> 19 October 2026, tier 4, as announced. https://dqnews.zjol.com.cn/dqnews/system/2026/10/19/035314153.shtml

We found no traffic notice for this year's race by Wednesday evening. Last
year's was issued on 17 October, a week before the start. It put Yuxin North
Street under traffic control for all three days and shut Zhuyuan Road (竹源路),
Huangfu Road (黄郛路), the Yulan road (于烂线) and Cuiying Road (翠莹路) to traffic in
phases.

> Source: 德清交警 (Deqing traffic police), notice for the 2025 race,
> reprinted by 德清108生活社区 (the Deqing 108 community), 17 October 2025,
> tier 4. https://m.108sq.cn/deqing/shuo/detail/69640851

**What this means for a visitor:** if you are not running, keep your car out
of Yucun from Friday afternoon to Sunday afternoon, and expect headtorches and
runners on the paths above the villages through Friday night.

## Spartan Race entries close on 31 October

The Spartan Race (斯巴达勇士赛) at Moganshan is still listed for Saturday 7 and
Sunday 8 November. Its registration page, read on 21 October, closes entries
at 23:59 on Saturday 31 October. The news desk has already reported a [women
only trail race on 13
December](/journal/news/2026/10/women-only-winter-trail-race-on-13-december/),
with entries open until 12 November.

**What this means for a visitor:** if you want to race in November, enter by
31 October; if you don't, treat 7 and 8 November as the next busy weekend when
you book a room.

> Source: 最酷 (Zuicool), the Spartan Race registration page, read 21 October
> 2026, tier 3. https://reg.zuicool.com/11858

## Three new places at Laoling and on Yujie

An autumn round up in Deqing News names two places that are open now. At
Laoling (劳岭村), a team from Hangzhou has turned an underused fruit picking farm
of about 200 mu (13 hectares) into 麓禾村 (Luhe Village), with kayaks, off road
karts, camping and farm activities. The paper says it took more than 1,000
visitors a day at its peak. On Yujie, the Danish outdoor brand Nordisk has
opened a brand centre with a camping area, a gear shop and a café.

> Source: 德清新闻网 (Deqing News), 17 October 2026, tier 2.
> https://dqnews.zjol.com.cn/dqnews/system/2026/10/17/035312915.shtml

Also on Yujie, 钝行酿造所 (Dunxing Brewing) has opened, making a dozen or so low
alcohol sparkling drinks brewed from tea, plus kombucha and alcohol free
versions. Its founders are working on one made with Mogan yellow tea (莫干黄茶).
The [Laoling tea farm
café](/journal/news/2026/10/laoling-tea-farm-cafe-lets-visitors-make-tea/) the
news desk reported on Monday is in the same village. None of the reports gives
opening hours or prices.

> Source: 德清新闻网 (Deqing News), 21 October 2026, tier 2.
> https://dqnews.zjol.com.cn/dqnews/system/2026/10/21/035315719.shtml

**What this means for a visitor:** if the rain keeps you off the paths, the
two Yujie places are indoors in Yucun, while Luhe Village is mostly outdoors;
ask your guesthouse to check opening hours before you set off.

## Weddings are filling autumn weekends at the castle

[Naked Castle](/where-to-stay/hotels/naked-castle/) (裸心堡) told the county
paper it has more than ten weddings in October. Every weekend date is taken,
so some couples have moved to weekdays. It has held 42 weddings so far this
year. Mantian (缦田度假酒店), a resort guesthouse, has four in October, and an
official of Moganshan town says it has hosted more than 80 weddings and
wedding events in 2026.

> Source: 德清新闻网 (Deqing News), 21 October 2026, tier 2.
> https://dqnews.zjol.com.cn/dqnews/system/2026/10/21/035315709.shtml

**What this means for a visitor:** if you are booking an autumn weekend at the
castle or a small guesthouse, ask whether a wedding is on that night. Couples
usually book the rooms and the meals along with the ceremony, the Mantian
manager told the paper.
