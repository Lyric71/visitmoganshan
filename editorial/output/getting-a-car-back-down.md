# Getting a car back down: Didi, taxis and the Moganshan pickup problem

Most advice on Didi for Moganshan, and on taxis, is about getting there. That
part rarely goes wrong. Deqing station has a taxi rank, with the coach bay
next door. Getting a car back down is the trip that fails. You are at a
guesthouse in a bamboo valley and the last bus has gone. The car that does
turn up may want more than the fare.

| The question | The answer, and its date |
|---|---|
| Does Didi work on Moganshan? | Yes, but drivers often ask a premium, say Chinese guides of 2019 and 2020 |
| A car down to Deqing station | About RMB 100 in our August 2026 check; RMB 50 to 100 in 2019 |
| Can a car reach a bed inside the scenic area? | Not from 08:00 to 17:30, without a booking and a parking reservation |
| Last bus off the top | 17:00 at Houwu, December 2025 |
| Last Y1 bus back to Deqing | 17:00, August 2026 |

Moganshan (莫干山) is a mountain in Deqing County (德清县), Huzhou (湖州), Zhejiang
Province, about 60 km north of Hangzhou and 200 to 240 km from Shanghai. The
nearest high speed station is [Deqing](/getting-here/deqing-station/) (德清站),
about 30 minutes away by road. Moganshan Road in Shanghai, the M50 art
district, is a different place 200 km away, and a ride hailing app will
happily send a car there.

## Why the trip down is harder than the trip up

On the way up you meet the cars at the station. Going down, a car has to find
you, in one of several valleys, on a mountain that keeps cars out for most of
the day. And not every driver working it is licensed. Nobody here has waited
on the mountain for one; this page rests on dated written accounts and our own
desk research.

That last point comes from the police, not from us. In June 2025 the deputy
head of the Moganshan police station (莫干山派出所) told a provincial paper that
black cars (黑车, unlicensed taxis) had troubled the scenic area for years,
along with villagers flagging down visitors' cars to charge them.

> Black cars and paid flagging down a long standing problem, Ping An Times
> (平安时报) on Ping An Zhejiang (平安浙江网), 24 June 2025, tier 2.
> https://www.pazjw.gov.cn/gongchuangpingan/202506/t20250624_31073840.shtml

A Youxiake (游侠客) guide said as much in June 2020: the ride hailing apps were
"not very effective" here, and asked for a premium. Ask a guesthouse or
restaurant owner to call a car, it added, and you usually get a local taxi or
a black car: pricier, and not always there.

> Ride hailing apps "not very effective" and asking a premium, Youxiake (游侠客),
> guide by 金二思, 22 June 2020, tier 3.
> https://www.youxiake.com/gonglue/view?id=4306

## Didi on Moganshan: what the app can and cannot do

Didi (滴滴) works here. The question is on what terms.

The app isn't the obstacle any more. Set it to English, register with your own
phone number and pay by international card, or use the Didi mini programme
inside Alipay or WeChat. Chat with the driver is translated both ways. [Money
and payments](/plan/money-and-payments/) covers the foreign card.

> Didi in English, overseas numbers, foreign cards, the mini programme and
> translated chat, Guangzhou Foreign Affairs Office (广州外事), 7 April 2025,
> tier 2.
> https://news.qq.com/rain/a/20250407A08XI100

It can't take a car past the barrier. From 08:00 to 17:30 every vehicle stops
at a transfer centre unless its passenger has a booking inside and a parking
reservation, and a Didi is a vehicle like any other. Before 08:00 and after
17:30 the road is open. The [private car ban
page](/plan/private-car-ban-on-moganshan/) has the detail.

> Vehicles change at a transfer centre from 08:00 to 17:30 and enter freely
> outside those hours, Tide News (潮新闻), 4 December 2025, tier 2.
> https://tidenews.com.cn/news.html?id=3315585

Type the destination in Chinese and check it. Deqing station is 德清站. Deqing
West (德清西站) is a different station.

No county tariff for this trip is published anywhere we can reach. Each figure
is one fare, on one day, from one source.

| Deqing station to your accommodation, taxi | RMB 50 to 100, 30 to 40 min | SmartShanghai | 3 | August 2019 |

> Taxi or van from Deqing station to Moganshan town, about RMB 100 and about

> A taxi from Deqing to your accommodation at RMB 50 to 100, SmartShanghai,

All three were paid going up; the one downhill fare in print is in the next

anything less as a saving.

## Black cars, and the driver who finds you

Most of the trouble in print comes from cars you did not order. A Youxiake
community writer found in 2019 that ride hailing trips to the scenic area
mostly cost RMB 20 to 40 extra. On the way up, the driver named the surcharge
once the bags were in the boot, and the writer paid. On the way back they
agreed RMB 30 to Deqing West before getting in, and paid nothing more. That
driver was touting at the transfer centre, and had been reported before for
surcharges.

> Surcharges of RMB 20 to 40, a RMB 30 fare agreed first and a RMB 60 taxi
> from Deqing station, August 2019 visit, Youxiake community (游侠客社区), trip
> report by 喵喵酱旅行, tier 3, undated page, dated by its own account.
> https://bbs.youxiake.com/y/1022265.html

The Moganshan station, with the transport and enforcement departments, has
moved against black cars, unlicensed guides and roadside flagging in 27
operations, and counts visitor calls and complaints down 67.3 percent year on
year since 2024. That is the station's own count.

> 27 operations and complaints down 67.3 percent since 2024, the station's
> count, Ping An Times (平安时报) on Ping An Zhejiang (平安浙江网), 24 June 2025,
> tier 2.
> https://www.pazjw.gov.cn/gongchuangpingan/202506/t20250624_31073840.shtml

So agree the fare before the bags go in. And prefer a car booked in the app,
which has an English support line, to one offered at the roadside.

## The order that works on the day you leave

Ask at check in, not at check out. Give the guesthouse your train time and ask
what a car to the station costs. It may be a local taxi or a black car, so get
the price in a message.

Inside the scenic area, trust the coach. Be on a bus down by 17:00, the last
departure on the only board in print, then find your car at the transfer
centre, where in 2019 drivers came looking for fares. The [shuttle system
page](/plan/moganshan-shuttle-system/) has the fares and hours.

> Houwu buses 08:30 to 17:00, last down 17:00, Tide News (潮新闻),
> 4 December 2025, tier 2.
> https://tidenews.com.cn/news.html?id=3315585

From the town, the Y1 bus to Deqing costs RMB 10, and its last return in our
August 2026 check left at 17:00. Miss both and the order is Didi with the
destination in Chinese, then the guesthouse's car at a price agreed in
writing.

> Y1 fare of RMB 10 and return runs from 08:40 to 17:00, BeyondBorder Group
> Ltd desk research, 1 to 5 August 2026.

![People with bags seen from behind boarding a small coach at a roadside stop under a bamboo slope on a grey late afternoon](/images/guide/getting-a-car-back-down-4.webp 'At Houwu in December 2025 the bus left when full, otherwise every 30 minutes. Queue early on a busy afternoon.')

An early train is the hardest case. Before 08:30 neither the coach nor the Y1
runs, so the only way down is a car. One thing helps: before 08:00 the barrier
is open, so a car can come to a door inside the scenic area. Book it the night
before, or sleep that night in Wukang, the county seat about 20 km away.
[Getting around Moganshan](/getting-here/getting-around/) covers the rest.
