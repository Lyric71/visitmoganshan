# Houwu: streams, a thousand year ginkgo, and the second transfer centre

Our own villages page gave Houwu one line: quiet, on the west side of
Moganshan, about 30 minutes from Yucun (庾村). That undersells it. For many
drivers it is where the visit starts. Between 08:00 and 17:30, signs on the
mountain road send cars back down to the Houwu transfer centre.

Chinese travel sites add a 1,500 year old ginkgo. The oldest account we found
says more than 1,400, and that figure came from a former village head, not a
survey.

Moganshan (莫干山) is a bamboo mountain and former hill station in Deqing County
(德清县), Huzhou (湖州), Zhejiang, about 60 km north of Hangzhou and 200 to 240 km
from Shanghai. It is not Moganshan Road in Shanghai, the M50 art district.
Houwu (后坞村) is one of the villages at its foot.

## Houwu village, Moganshan: what it is and where

Houwu lies on the western flank, in what was Fatou township (筏头乡), about 30 km
west of the county centre. It has 470 households, and its old market town,
Jimiaowu (计庙坞), keeps about a kilometre of shops. The village sits in the core
of a protected landscape belt along two streams, which limits building.

> About 30 km west of the county centre, China Sports Daily (中国体育报),
> 29 April 2015, tier 2.
> https://www.sport.gov.cn/n20001280/n20745751/n20767239/c21821628/content.html

> 470 households, the Jimiaowu shops and the twin streams (双溪) landscape
> belt, Huzhou Daily (湖州日报), 28 May 2025, tier 2.
> https://finance.sina.com.cn/roll/2025-05-28/doc-ineyakcf9435506.shtml

Houwu is also a rated scenic area in its own right. The Huzhou roll of June
2023 listed Deqing Houwu village scenic area at 3A, separately from the core
of the mountain.

> Deqing Houwu village scenic area, rated 3A, Zhejiang Online (浙江在线),
> 9 June 2023, tier 2.
> https://zjnews.zjol.com.cn/zjnews/202306/t20230609_25838009.shtml

### The transfer centre

Private cars have been barred from the scenic area road from 08:00 to 17:30
since 30 November 2018. Guests booked inside the scenic area who reserve
parking may drive in at any hour. When Tide News (潮新闻) drove up in December
2025, the reporters found signs telling drivers to turn round for the Houwu
transfer centre (后坞换乘中心), and staff spoke of two transfer points. The written
record has named three since 2019: Yucun, Fatou and Houwu.

## The ginkgos at the temple gate

No source we can cite prints an admission charge for the village.

Its one sight is a pair of ginkgos at the gate of Tianquan Chan Temple (天泉禅寺),
in the part of the village called Kanshang (坎上). An account published in 2018
says the monk Weizheng (惟正) founded the temple between 535 and 546, and that
Japanese troops burned it in 1941. The gate and the two trees survived.

The account reprints a 2015 report from the county paper, Jinri Deqing (今日德清).
A former village head, Li Jianli (李建立), told it the south tree is male and the
north one female: the husband and wife trees (夫妻树). He put the male at over 40
metres tall and more than 1,400 years old. It takes four adults, hand in hand,
to circle it. When the leaves drop, in autumn and winter, the ground goes
gold. We found no tree survey that dates the tree.

> The temple's history and the two ginkgos as Jinri Deqing reported them on
> 28 January 2015, Sohu (搜狐, 章家 account), 21 December 2018, tier 4, as
> published. https://www.sohu.com/a/283511134_120056432

Look, but leave the nuts. In September 2026 a county hospital doctor warned
that raw ginkgo nuts (白果) are toxic in quantity and the fresh flesh can bring
a rash within two days.

> The ginkgo nut warning, Deqing News (德清新闻网), 24 September 2026,
> tier 2. https://dqnews.zjol.com.cn/dqnews/system/2026/09/24/035297964.shtml

![Two very large ginkgo trees with yellowing leaves beside an old stone gateway in a village, a concrete lane and a parked scooter in front, overcast sky](/images/guide/houwu-2.webp 'The 2015 report has the trees flowering in April and May and fruiting in October. Some older villagers still dye the nuts for weddings.')

## Eating in Houwu

No dated source we found names a restaurant here. You'll mostly eat where you
sleep: of the 107 Houwu listings we hold, 91 list a restaurant.

> Facilities on 107 captured Trip.com listings with a Houwu address,
> BeyondBorder Group Ltd primary research, captured 8 August 2026, compiled
> 13 October 2026.

The village's own food event is the New Year pig feast (年猪饭), held five times
by 2025, and the village plans a banquet hall for it.

> The pig feast and the banquet hall, Huzhou Daily (湖州日报), 28 May 2025,
> tier 2. https://finance.sina.com.cn/roll/2025-05-28/doc-ineyakcf9435506.shtml

When you book, ask whether the house cooks dinner.

## Staying in Houwu

Houwu's first guesthouse, the Shangri La (香格里拉), opened in 2005. By 2025 more
than 150 of the 470 households were in the trade, and a hotel costing RMB 30
million had opened at Jimiaowu. The same report has an art hotel due in
October 2025; no listing yet shows it trading, so it is not below.

> The first guesthouse, the households in the trade and the two hotels,
> Huzhou Daily (湖州日报), 28 May 2025, tier 2. https://finance.sina.com.cn/roll/2025-05-28/doc-ineyakcf9435506.shtml

**The Muke Cooperative** (木可合作社) is the reason to stop in Jimiaowu. The pink
four storey building at Jimiaowu was the village credit cooperative. It opened
in November 2023 for digital nomads: 16 rooms, each with a bathroom, and long
work tables by the door. In September 2024 the writer Jiang Kan (蒋瞰) reported
RMB 400 to 500 a night, RMB 2,023 a week or about RMB 4,000 a month, breakfast
included. No children under 15.

> The cooperative's building, rooms, rates and rules, NetEase (网易,
> 文旅进行时), by Jiang Kan, 23 September 2024, tier 3.

## The walk from Houwu

This is a desk route; nobody on this desk has walked it.

Moganshan's national mountain fitness trail (莫干山国家登山健身步道) opened on 9 October
2014: 45.1 km in four loops, most of it in Houwu.

| Loop | Length | What the county paper said in 2014 |
|---|---|---|
| Dayangli (大洋里) | 9.6 km | The hardest, about 400 m of climb to a camp hut at 635 m |
| Yaowu (瑶坞) | 10.2 km | Mostly road and stone paving, past the Houwu culture hall |
| Yangshulin (杨树林) | 3.2 km | The shortest, gravel and steps |
| Tianquanshan (天泉山) | 22.1 km | The longest, from the culture hall over Tianquanshan |

> The trail's opening and its 45.1 km, mostly in Houwu, China Sports Daily
> (中国体育报), 29 April 2015, tier 2.
> https://www.sport.gov.cn/n20001280/n20745751/n20767239/c21821628/content.html

> The four loops, Deqing News (德清新闻网), 20 November 2014, tier 2.
> https://dqnews.zjol.com.cn/dqnews/system/2014/11/20/018710329.shtml

In 2019 the county also printed a 15 km walk from Houwu over Tianquanshan to
Yucun square by way of Sword Pond (剑池).

> The 15 km route to Yucun square, Zhejiang Online (浙江在线), 5 April 2019,
> tier 2. https://huzhou.zjol.com.cn/qy/dqx18223/201904/t20190405_9837182.shtml

## Who should base in Houwu

Drivers from the west: the road delivers you to the transfer centre.

Walkers, since the longest loop starts at the culture hall.

Remote workers in for a week or a month, at the cooperative's weekly rate.
Families with children under 15 cannot stay there.

It does not suit anyone without a car who wants a choice of dinner. Yucun is
half an hour away. [Yucun on its own](/moganshan/villages/yucun/) makes the
case for the town.

## The honest downside

Houwu has stalled before. The village head told Huzhou Daily that the
protection rules held back building, and that after 2020 visitors to its
outdoor park and lavender farm fell sharply. The bet now is on young people
coming home to open businesses on the Jimiaowu street.

> Building limits and the fall in visitors after 2020, Huzhou Daily (湖州日报),
> 28 May 2025, tier 2.
> https://finance.sina.com.cn/roll/2025-05-28/doc-ineyakcf9435506.shtml

The cooperative met the same wall. Jiang Kan reported its biggest problem:
prices here are not low, so the "geographic arbitrage" that draws nomads to
Southeast Asia is hard to pull off. On its pay what you want trial, one guest
stayed six nights for RMB 72.

> The arbitrage problem and the trial, NetEase (网易, 文旅进行时), by Jiang
> Kan, 23 September 2024, tier 3.
> https://m.163.com/dy/article/JCPD5J150524BDFD.html

And the clock: the last bus down at Houwu left at 17:00 on the December 2025
board.

alternatives.
