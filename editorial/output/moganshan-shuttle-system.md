# The Moganshan shuttle system: both transfer centres, both fares, last bus down

The Moganshan shuttle bus is not one service. It is three, paid for
separately, and the one that ends your day ends it earlier than the gate
does. Your car stops at a transfer centre at the foot of the mountain. A
coach takes you up. A smaller bus loops the sights at the top. Two weekend
lines link the villages. Each has a fare and a last departure, and no English
page we could find prints them together.

| The question | The published answer, and how old it is |
|---|---|
| Where a private car stops | Yucun, Fatou or Houwu transfer centre |
| Coach up and back down | RMB 35 return, last in print in 2020 |
| Bus between the sights at the top | RMB 15 that day, last in print in 2020 |
| Two themed weekend lines | RMB 5 a trip, opened June 2025 |
| Last bus down, Houwu, in winter | 17:00, reported December 2025 |

Moganshan (莫干山) is in Deqing County (德清县), Huzhou (湖州), Zhejiang
Province (浙江省), about 60 km north of Hangzhou and 200 to 240 km from
Shanghai. Moganshan Road in Shanghai, home of the M50 art district, is a
different place with no gate and no shuttle.

## Two transfer centres, or three

Ask the scenic area and you may be told two. Read the paperwork and you get
three. When Tide News (潮新闻) drove up in December 2025, a member of staff
told the reporters that two transfer points were set up when the road closed
to cars in 2018.

> A staff member's account of two transfer points set up in 2018, Tide News
> (潮新闻), 4 December 2025, tier 2.
> https://tidenews.com.cn/news.html?id=3315585

The written record says three, and has since a 2019 travel report: the main
transfer centre at Yucun (庾村), Fatou (筏头) on the provincial road S304,
and Houwu (后坞) off the same road. Our
[getting around page](/getting-here/getting-around/) says three, and the
[private car ban page](/plan/private-car-ban-on-moganshan/) prints the route
each one feeds and what parking costs.

> Three transfer points, their locations and the routes they serve, Sohu
> Travel (搜狐旅游, 权哥探店), 2 July 2019, tier 3.
> https://www.sohu.com/a/324235488_357973

Which one matters. Houwu is what a driver from the west meets first, and the
only one with a recent timetable in print. Yucun is the one every taxi knows.
Fatou is where you get sent when the Yucun car park fills.

## What the Moganshan shuttle bus costs

Every figure below is dated and printed by a named source. None of them is
current, and no official page we can reach confirms any of them.

| Item | Figure | Source | Tier | Checked |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Coach, up and back down | RMB 35 | Deqing Culture and Tourism, December 2018, and Youxiake, June 2020 | 4 and 3 | 16 September 2026 |
| Coach, single fare down | RMB 18 | Deqing Culture and Tourism, December 2018 | 4 | 16 September 2026 |
| Bus between the sights at the top | RMB 15, valid that day | Youxiake, June 2020 | 3 | 16 September 2026 |
| Themed weekend lines | RMB 5 a trip | Deqing News, June 2025 | 2 | 16 September 2026 |

> Transfer centre opened with 500 parking spaces, a RMB 35 return coach fare
> and a RMB 18 single fare down, as announced by Deqing Culture and Tourism
> (德清文旅), 7 December 2018, tier 4, reproduced on 163.com.
> https://m.163.com/dy/article/E2EU8TUV0518SHA7.html

> Entry at RMB 80, coach up and down at RMB 35 and the loop bus at RMB 15,
> RMB 130 in total, Youxiake (游侠客), guide by 金二思, 22 June 2020, tier 3.
> https://www.youxiake.com/gonglue/view?id=4306

The Youxiake guide is headlined for 2026, sits near the top of the Chinese
results and has been read 74,000 times. The date in its own page metadata is
22 June 2020. So the stack of numbers everybody quotes is six years old, and
RMB 130 is an order of magnitude rather than a bill.

![A ticket window with a hatch and a handwritten price board in Chinese, seen at an angle with a queue of people from behind](/images/guide/moganshan-shuttle-system-2.webp 'Fares are paid at the centre, in cash or by Chinese QR code. Budget RMB 130 a head for entry plus both buses.')

## The last bus down is the number that matters

A ticket that expires costs money. A last bus that has gone costs the
evening. The only recent timetable in print is the board at Houwu, read off
the sign by reporters in December 2025.

| What the Houwu board said | Time |
|---|---|
| Shuttle running window | 08:30 to 17:00 |
| Last bus up | 16:30 |
| Last bus down | 17:00 |
| Frequency | When full, otherwise every 30 minutes |

> Houwu transfer point timetable, departing when full and otherwise every 30
> minutes, Tide News (潮新闻), 4 December 2025, tier 2.
> https://tidenews.com.cn/news.html?id=3315585

A trip report from an August 2019 visit gives the same last departure from
the top, 17:00 from Yinshan Street (荫山街), in high summer. Our own desk check in
August 2026 gave the centres a longer day, 08:00 to 18:00, and that figure
sits on two of our pages. Three readings from three different dates, and the
seasonal calendar behind them is not published where a visitor can reach
it.

> Last coach down from Yinshan Street at 17:00 in an August 2019 visit,
> Youxiake community (游侠客社区), trip report by 喵喵酱旅行, tier 3, undated
> page, dated by its own account of the visit.
> https://bbs.youxiake.com/y/1022265.html

> Transfer centre hours of 08:00 to 18:00, BeyondBorder Group Ltd desk
> research, 1 to 5 August 2026.

Plan against 17:00 and you are never caught out. Ask the driver on the way up
what time he comes back down, which is what the Chinese guides have been
telling their own readers since 2019.

## Two lines opened in 2025 that no English page carries

On 21 June 2025 the resort opened two themed bus lines, named after the two
races the mountain hosts. Both charge RMB 5 a trip, which makes them the
cheapest way to move between villages that are otherwise a taxi apart.

| Line | Runs between | Calling at | Hours |
|---|---|---|---|
| Kailas (凯乐石号) | Biwu Dragon Pool (碧坞龙潭) at Xiantan and the Wusi (五四村) pool resort | Nanlu, the Sihe fish scale weir, Jun An Li (郡安里), Yucun | 08:00 to 17:40 |
| Spartan (斯巴达号) | The transfer centre and Jiangnan Yaowu (江南瑶坞) | Laoling reservoir, naked Stables (裸心谷), Ziling, Houwu | 08:00 to 16:20 |

Both ran May to October, on Saturdays, Sundays and public holidays.

> Two themed tourism lines opened on 21 June 2025, both at RMB 5 a trip, with
> their routes, stops and hours, Deqing News (德清新闻网), 24 June 2025,
> tier 2. https://dqnews.zjol.com.cn/dqnews/system/2025/06/24/034991600.shtml

The stop lists say what they are for. The Spartan line threads the western
guesthouse belt, where moving between villages has always meant calling a
car. The Kailas line links the northern villages to Yucun.

One caution. That report covers the 2025 season. No dated source confirms
either line ran in 2026, and a seasonal weekend service is the kind of thing
that quietly does not come back, so ask at the centre before you count on
it.

![A small bus stop sign on a village lane with a hand written route list, bamboo behind it and a wet road](/images/guide/moganshan-shuttle-system-3.webp 'The themed lines ran weekends and holidays, May to October 2025, at RMB 5 a trip. Whether they returned for 2026 is not published anywhere we can reach.')

## Luggage and dogs, where the record contradicts itself

The transfer is the worst moment of a Moganshan trip with bags. Everything
comes off one vehicle and onto another, outdoors.

One Chinese trip report, describing an August 2019 visit, says the service
desk at the main centre stores luggage free, keeps free cages for the large
dogs that cannot go up the mountain, and lends small carriers against a
returnable deposit. No other account says so.

> Free luggage storage at the service desk and free dog cages at the main
> centre during an August 2019 visit, Youxiake community (游侠客社区), trip
> report by 喵喵酱旅行, tier 3, undated page, dated by its own account.
> https://bbs.youxiake.com/y/1022265.html

Our own check in August 2026 found no left luggage service, and that is what
the getting around page says today. Both cannot be right. Either the service
closed in the seven years between, or our check missed a desk that does not
advertise itself in English. Nobody here has rung the scenic area to settle
it.

> No left luggage service at the transfer centres, BeyondBorder Group Ltd
> desk research, 1 to 5 August 2026.

So carry your bags up, or leave them at the property. A free store, if it is
still there, is a bonus. On dogs both accounts agree: a large one does not go
up the mountain.

## Who does not need any of this

Most first visits never touch the coach. The guesthouses and the restaurants
sit below the gate, in Yucun, Xiantan, Laoling and Houwu, and a taxi reaches
all of them. What the transfer centre protects is the old hill
station at the top, and that needs a ticket as well as a fare.

![Two people with wheeled cases seen from behind on a paved forecourt, a coach pulling away, bamboo hills in cloud beyond](/images/guide/moganshan-shuttle-system-4.webp 'The transfer is where the bags change vehicle. Allow more than the timetable suggests if you are carrying more than a daypack.')

The people who do need it are day trippers and anyone sleeping inside the
boundary without a car registered to their property. Their chain is fixed:
train to [Deqing station](/getting-here/deqing-station/), Y1 bus or taxi to
the transfer centre, coach up, loop bus at the top, then all of it reversed
before the last departure. Work backwards from 17:00, not forwards from
breakfast. The [tickets and entry page](/plan/tickets-and-entry/) has what
the gate costs, and the [planning FAQ](/plan/faq/) answers what is left.
