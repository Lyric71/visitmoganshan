# Laoling and Sanjiuwu: where the foreign farmstay began

The farmhouses where Moganshan's foreign farmstay trade began are still on the
hillside, but you cannot book them under their own name. In 2007 Grant
Horsfield, a South African working in Shanghai, rented them in Sanjiuwu (三九坞).
Sanjiuwu is a hamlet of Laoling (劳岭村), a Moganshan village on the south side.
He called the place naked Home Village (裸心乡), and in 2017 the farmhouses were
remade as part of naked Castle, a resort on the same slope. Laoling carries on
as a village of guesthouses with no centre to speak of.

## Laoling, Moganshan: what it is and where

Moganshan (莫干山) is a bamboo mountain and former hill station in Deqing County
(德清县), Huzhou (湖州), Zhejiang, about 60 km north of Hangzhou and 200 to 240 km
from Shanghai. It is not Moganshan Road in Shanghai, the M50 art district. The
villages sit below the ridge, and Laoling is on the south side, reached
through the Laoling tunnel (劳岭隧道).

Sanjiuwu is not a separate village, though some guides list it as one (our own
villages page did until now). People's Daily placed it in Laoling in 2017.
Zhejiang Online's reporters, coming in through the tunnel in 2016, found more
than ten guesthouses in the part called Lingkengli (岭坑里) and put Sanjiuwu
further into the hills.

> Sanjiuwu in Laoling village, People's Daily Online (人民网), carrying People's
> Daily Overseas Edition, 7 July 2017, tier 2.
> http://travel.people.com.cn/n1/2017/0707/c41570-29389273.html

> The tunnel, Lingkengli and Sanjiuwu beyond it, Zhejiang Online (浙江在线),
> 23 July 2016, tier 2.
> https://zjnews.zjol.com.cn/zjnews/hznews/201607/t20160723_1789530.shtml

By March 2016 Laoling had 45 guesthouses. They gave more than 200 jobs, the
same report said, which was a seventh of the village's people.

> 45 guesthouses and more than 200 jobs, Zhejiang Online (浙江在线), 23 July
> 2016, tier 2.
> https://zjnews.zjol.com.cn/zjnews/hznews/201607/t20160723_1789530.shtml

## Sanjiuwu and the first yangjiale

Guangming Daily told the story in 2013. Horsfield went cycling in the summer
of 2007, got lost and came down into Sanjiuwu. He rented several empty
farmhouses and did them up himself, with tables made from tree stumps, no air
conditioning, no gas and a wood stove for winter. The rooms had names such as
Ziling Residence (紫岭居) and Green Bamboo Cottage (翠竹小筑).

> The 2007 summer visit, the farmhouses, the room names and the furnishings,
> Guangming Daily (光明日报), 21 February 2013, tier 2.
> https://epaper.gmw.cn/gmrb/html/2013-02/21/nw.D110000gmrb_20130221_1-16.htm

The county gave the idea a name in 2009: yangjiale (洋家乐), a farmstay in
foreign hands. That makes Sanjiuwu the birthplace of the foreign run farmstay,
not of the Moganshan guesthouse. Zhejiang Online calls Yiyuan (颐园), opened in
2002, the first guesthouse in the modern sense.

> Deqing County names the yangjiale in 2009, Zhejiang Online (浙江在线), 23 July
> 2016, tier 2.
> https://zjnews.zjol.com.cn/zjnews/hznews/201607/t20160723_1789530.shtml

## What costs nothing in Laoling

Nothing we read prints an entry charge for the village.

The free things are outdoors. The best known is the metasequoia avenue (水杉大道),
the line of tall trees the county paper uses as a landmark in the village. A
2023 post on The Paper's platform added the reservoir and the rice fields
beside it, and said the flat lanes suit cycling and an early run.

> The avenue as a landmark, Deqing News (德清新闻网), 17 October 2026,
> tier 2. https://dqnews.zjol.com.cn/dqnews/system/2026/10/17/035312925.shtml

> The avenue, the reservoir and the rice fields, The Paper (澎湃新闻), a
> 澎湃号 media account, 28 September 2023, tier 3.
> https://www.thepaper.cn/newsDetail_forward_24783701

The Laoling reservoir (劳岭水库) was also a stop on a weekend shuttle in 2025. The
Spartan (斯巴达号) line ran from the transfer centre past the reservoir, naked
Stables and Ziling to Jiangnan Yaowu, 08:00 to 16:20, for RMB 5, on weekends
and holidays from May to October. We found no 2026 timetable.

> The Spartan line, its stops, hours and fare, Deqing News (德清新闻网), 24
> June 2025, tier 2.
> https://dqnews.zjol.com.cn/dqnews/system/2025/06/24/034991600.shtml

## Eating in Laoling

No dated source we found names a restaurant in the village. Most people eat
where they sleep: of the 62 Laoling listings we hold, 56 list a restaurant.

> Facilities on 62 captured Trip.com listings with a Laoling address,
> BeyondBorder Group Ltd primary research, captured 8 August 2026, compiled
> 21 October 2026.

Two places made the county paper in October 2026. Chashanli (茶杉里), near the
metasequoia avenue, is a tea and coffee house on a tea farm whose 200 mu (13
hectares) of bushes date from the 1950s and 1960s. The owner, a recognised
maker of Mogan Huangya (莫干黄芽) tea, lets customers pick leaf and fire it; our
news desk reported it on 19 October. Luhe Village (麓禾村), a converted fruit
picking farm of about 200 mu, has kayaks and karts, and room to camp. Neither
report gives hours or prices.

> Chashanli, the tea farm and its opening, Deqing News (德清新闻网), 17
> October 2026, tier 2.
> https://dqnews.zjol.com.cn/dqnews/system/2026/10/17/035312925.shtml

> Luhe Village, Deqing News (德清新闻网), 17 October 2026, tier 2.
> https://dqnews.zjol.com.cn/dqnews/system/2026/10/17/035312915.shtml

When you book, ask whether the house cooks dinner, and what time it stops.

## The walk from Laoling to Ziling

This is a desk route; nobody on this desk has walked it.

GreenTour Asia (绿色旅行网) lists the Laoling to Ziling old path (劳岭紫岭古道) as 3.5
km. It starts from the Yucun side on stone paving, passes the Laoling
reservoir and about 100 mu of tea, and ends in Ziling village, where Le
Passage began in 2008. The guide calls the gradient gentle and the walk free.

> The route, its length, surface and gradient, GreenTour Asia (绿色旅行网),
> updated 3 July 2026, tier 3.
> https://www.greentourasia.com/article/moganshantubugonglve

We found no measured climb and no official waymarking for it.

![A walker seen from behind on a worn stone path between rows of tea bushes on a gentle slope, bamboo at the edge of the frame, mist low over the hill and puddles on the stones](/images/guide/laoling-4.webp 'The old path is stone paving through tea. After rain the stones are slippery, so wear shoes with grip.')

## Who should base in Laoling

People with a car who want a pool and do not need a street outside the door:
28 of the 62 listings have an outdoor pool.

If you want to sleep where the guesthouse trade began, it has to be the
castle: it is the only listing in Sanjiuwu.

Cyclists and early runners will like the flat lanes by the reservoir.

It does not suit a first visit without a car, or anyone who wants a choice of
dinner. [Yucun](/moganshan/villages/yucun/) has the restaurants and a transfer
centre.

## The honest downside

The thing the village is famous for has gone inside a resort. You can drive to
Sanjiuwu, but naked Home Village is now part of naked Castle, and its
farmhouses are seen as a guest there or not at all.

The rest is distance. Laoling has no square and no high street. Nor is it one
of the three transfer centres the county has named since 2019, which are
Yucun, Fatou and Houwu.

> The three transfer centres, Deqing News (德清新闻网), 30 September 2019,
> tier 2. https://dqnews.zjol.com.cn/dqnews/system/2019/09/30/031939856.shtml

The 2025 shuttle ran on weekends only, and no 2026 timetable had appeared by
21 October 2026. Without a car, every meal out and every trip to the scenic
area gate is a taxi or a lift the guesthouse arranges.

[The villages below Moganshan](/moganshan/villages/) sets out the
alternatives.
