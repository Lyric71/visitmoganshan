# Biwu Dragon Pool: a short waterfall walk the guides call free

Chinese walking guides list Biwu Longtan (碧坞龙潭), the Biwu Dragon Pool, among
Moganshan's free walks. The county paper describes something else: a small
scenic area that has traded since 2003, whose operator set out to rebuild its
ticket entrance in 2023 and now sells tickets through booking apps. Plan to
pay. The walk itself is short: about 3 km up a stream valley through bamboo to
a waterfall and a glass viewing platform, then back down, in an hour or two.

Moganshan (莫干山) is a bamboo mountain and former hill station in Deqing County
(德清县), Huzhou (湖州), Zhejiang, about 60 km north of Hangzhou and 200 to 240 km
from Shanghai. It isn't Moganshan Road in Shanghai, the M50 art district. The
pool sits on the mountain's northern foot, up the slope behind the Biwu (碧坞)
end of Xiantan village (仙潭村), with Tianquanshan (天泉山) above it. The county
paper counts it part of the national scenic area, but it has its own operator
and its own ticket, separate from the gate on the road up the mountain.

## The ticket the guides leave out

The case for paying comes from Deqing News (德清新闻网), the county paper. On 3
November 2023 it reported that the scenic area, about 100,000 m² and trading
since 2003, was being rebuilt: old equipment replaced, the ticket entrance
rebuilt, a café put into the old power station. During the works the ticket
fell to RMB 20. When they finished it was to go back to RMB 60. Deqing
residents with a local ID would get in free on weekdays, which doesn't help a
visitor.

> Ticket RMB 20 during the works and RMB 60 afterwards, trading since 2003,
> about 100,000 m², Deqing News (德清新闻网), 3 November 2023, tier 2.
> http://dqnews.zjol.com.cn/dqnews/system/2023/11/03/034421443.shtml

The works ran into 2024. On 6 May that year the paper put phase one at RMB 8
million in all, due to finish by July. By March 2025 the operator, a company
under the county supply and marketing cooperative, was selling tickets as an
agent through Douyin, Kuaishou, Meituan and Ctrip. By its own count it drew
about 85,000 visitors in 2024. Over 60 percent came from Hangzhou and
Shanghai.

> Phase one at RMB 8 million, due by July 2024, Deqing News (德清新闻网),

## Stage by stage: the Biwu Longtan walk

The stages follow the loop a 2021 account suggests and the layout a 2024
visitor describes. The names are in Chinese too, for a map app.

### 1. Jiangnan Biwu gate to the entrance (江南碧坞 to 景区入口)

The 2024 visitor left the car at the Jiangnan Biwu gate, where there is a car
park, and walked about five minutes through the village to the entrance.

### 2. Up the left bank (溪流步道)

Take the stream path on the left. It climbs past the old sights. The county
paper names the gate tower (城楼) and the rain terrace (应雨台); the August 2024
traveller noticed a Nine Dragon Wall (九龙壁), climbed steps from the entrance
and passed a café part way up. The 2021 account names a butterfly valley along
the stream.

### 3. The fall and the pool (瀑布 and 龙潭)

The fall drops five zhang (五丈), about 17 m, into the pool. The 2021 account
ties the pool to the legend of Ganjiang and Moye (干将莫邪), the sword makers
Moganshan is named for; the [Sword Pond page](/things-to-do/sword-pond/) tells
the story.

### 4. The glass platform, and down the right side (玻璃观景台)

The glass viewing platform comes after the fall, at the end of the climb. From
there the 2021 account goes down a hill path on the right side of the valley,
back into the village.

> A five zhang fall, a butterfly valley and a glass walkway; up the stream on
> the left, down the hill path on the right; no ticket and no parking fee;
> about three hours with the village, Tencent News (腾讯新闻), account
> 江南实时分享, 25 June 2021, tier 3.
> https://news.qq.com/rain/a/20210625A0BLFD00

![A thin waterfall dropping into a dark green pool between mossy rocks, a wet wooden handrail cutting across the bottom corner, mist softening the bamboo above](/images/guide/biwu-dragon-pool-3.webp 'The 2021 account puts the fall at five zhang, about 17 m. Go up by the stream on the left and come down the hill path on the right.')

## How long it takes

| Who | What | Time |
|---|---|---|
| A traveller, August 2024 | The entrance to the top and back | About an hour |
| A Tencent account, June 2021 | The pool and Xiantan village together | About three hours |
| GreenTour Asia, July 2026 | Any of its half day routes for beginners, not this one alone | Two to three hours |

No source gives a climb in metres. Plan on two hours: enough for the walk and
the platform, with a stop at the café.

## Water, and why to pack a change of clothes

In summer the stream is the point. GreenTour Asia runs a separate 5 km stream
walk (溯溪) that starts in Biwu village and goes up the valley in the water,
with shallows for paddling and catching fish. It says to wear stream shoes
because the stones are slippery, and calls it a summer route. The operator
lists stream play among its activities, Deqing News reported in March 2025.
Expect children to come out wet.

In winter the same guide sends walkers to short routes such as this one, for
the icicles, with grip and poles, and says to avoid rain and snow.

![Two walkers seen from behind wading up a shallow, stony stream in hazy summer light, trousers rolled, shoes in hand, a bag and towels left on a rock](/images/guide/biwu-dragon-pool-4.webp 'GreenTour Asia calls its 5 km stream walk a summer route and says to wear stream shoes: the stones in the water are slippery.')

## Getting there

Xiantan is on the north side of the mountain, and that traveller drove about
20 minutes from a guesthouse beside Yucun (庾村). In 2025 a weekend shuttle, the
Kailas (凯乐石号), ran from Biwu Dragon Pool through Yucun to Wusi (五四村), 08:00 to
17:40 on Saturdays, Sundays and holidays from May to October, at RMB 5. No
source we can reach confirms it ran in 2026.

> The Kailas shuttle's route, hours and fare in 2025, Deqing News
> (德清新闻网), 24 June 2025, tier 2.
> https://dqnews.zjol.com.cn/dqnews/system/2025/06/24/034991600.shtml

Without it, plan on a car or a ride hailing car both ways. No dated source
gives the opening hours, so go in the middle of the day. [Getting around
Moganshan](/getting-here/getting-around/) covers the options, and the [Xiantan
village page](/moganshan/villages/xiantan/) covers where to sleep and eat at
the bottom.

## What to carry

* **Grip.** Wet stone steps are the hazard, as everywhere on the mountain;
  the [walking page](/things-to-do/hiking/) covers footwear.
* **A change of clothes and a towel** in summer, and stream shoes if you will
  walk in the water.
* **Payment.** A phone wallet, and some cash in case the gate wants it.
* **The stage names in Chinese.** The entrance, the fall and the platform
  are all above.

## When not to go

**In or just after heavy rain.** GreenTour Asia warns that the stream stones
are slippery, and tells winter walkers to stay away in rain and snow.

**If you want a long walk.** This is an hour's outing. The [Chiang Kai shek
path](/things-to-do/chiang-kai-shek-path/) is a full day, and [walking on
Moganshan](/things-to-do/hiking/) sets out the rest.

**If you will not pay to walk.** The county paper describes a ticketed
site.
