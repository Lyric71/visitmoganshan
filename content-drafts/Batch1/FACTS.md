## 1. Where it is, what it is

- Moganshan (莫干山), also written Mount Mogan, is a mountain in **Deqing County, Huzhou prefecture, Zhejiang Province, China**.
- About **60 km north of Hangzhou**, about **200 to 240 km from Shanghai**.
- Main peak is **Tashan**, elevation **719 to 720 m**. (Some sources say 758 m. Do not use that figure.)
- Core scenic area is about **20 km²**. Wider designated area sometimes given as 43 km². If you use a size, use 20 km² and say "core scenic area".
- Forest cover is **over 92 percent**, mostly **moso bamboo**. It is a working commercial bamboo crop, not landscaping.
- Designations: **national scenic area**, **national AAAA tourist attraction**, and a **national-level tourism resort** named by the Ministry of Culture and Tourism on **15 December 2020** ("Deqing Moganshan International Tourism Resort").
- Traditionally listed as one of **China's four great summer resorts**, with Beidaihe, Lushan and Jigongshan.
- Named sights: **Sword Pond (Jianchi)**, Luhuadang Park, Dakeng, the Xuguang sunrise platform, Chiang Kai-shek's villa, the Bai and Yun Art Museum.

### THREE ENTITY TRAPS — mention where relevant, never confuse
1. **Moganshan Road** (莫干山路) is the **M50 art district in Putuo District, Shanghai**. A completely different place, about 200 km away.
2. **Moganshan Veneer** is an industrial plywood and veneer brand. Unrelated.
3. **Moganshan railway station** (on the Shangqiu–Hefei–Hangzhou line) **does NOT serve the mountain**. It serves Deqing/Wukang town. **Deqing station is the correct station.**
4. Moganshan's village **Yucun (庾村)** is NOT Anji's **Yucun (余村)**, which won a UN Tourism Best Tourism Village award in 2021. Different characters, different county. Moganshan has never won that award.

## 3. Getting there — MEASURED

### Rail
- **Shanghai Hongqiao to Deqing: direct, 63 to 80 minutes. 13 direct services a day.** Second class fare roughly RMB 61 to 110. Examples: G1419 17:39→18:42 (63 min), G687 12:12→13:21 (69 min), D377 08:11→09:19 (68 min).
- **Hangzhou East to Deqing: 13 to 17 minutes** (35 km), fastest 13 min. **16 daily services.** Fares roughly RMB 16 to 67.
- The **Shanghai–Suzhou–Huzhou high-speed line opened 26 December 2024**, cutting Hongqiao to Huzhou to 55 minutes at fastest. It improved the northern approach.
- Deqing station sits on the **Nanjing–Hangzhou high-speed line, open since 2013**. The Hangzhou leg does NOT depend on the 2024 line.
- **Huzhou station** exists but is farther from Moganshan than Deqing.

### Last mile from Deqing station
- **Y1 tourist shuttle** ("Republican-era tram") from Deqing Coach Centre, next to the station, to the Moganshan tourist distribution centre. **About 30 to 40 minutes, RMB 10, 7 departures a day**: 07:30, 08:40, 09:40, 10:40, 13:00, 15:00, 16:00. Return runs 08:40 to 17:00. **It terminates in Moganshan town, not inside the scenic area.**
- **Taxi or van: about RMB 100, about 30 minutes** to the town.

### The rule almost nobody publishes in English
- **Private vehicles cannot drive inside the scenic area.** Visitors transfer at one of **three transfer centres: Yucun, Fatou, Houwu**, operating **08:00 to 18:00**.

### Road
- Shanghai to Moganshan: about **200 to 240 km, 2 to 3 hours** driving.
- Hangzhou to Moganshan: about **60 to 63 km, about 1.5 hours**.
- Direct coach Shanghai to Deqing: about 2h40 to 4h, from about RMB 69.

### Airports
- **Shanghai Hongqiao (SHA):** walk to the high-speed rail concourse, 63 to 80 min to Deqing, then 30 to 40 min transfer.
- **Shanghai Pudong (PVG):** add the transfer to Hongqiao (maglev plus metro, or 50 to 70 minutes by car).
- **Hangzhou Xiaoshan (HGH):** either rail/taxi to Hangzhou East then high-speed rail to Deqing, or an airport coach to Deqing bus station.

## 4. Weather and seasons — MEASURED

- **July to August mean temperature: 24.1 °C.**
- **6 to 7 °C cooler than Shanghai and Hangzhou in summer.** (One source says about 5 °C. Use 6 to 7.)
- **Annual mean 13.3 °C. January mean 1.4 °C.** (One source gives about 3.5 °C for January; prefer 1.4 but you may say "close to freezing".)
- Winter scenic-area tickets are **half price**.
- Summer (July to August) is the historic and current core season. The founding reason was missionaries escaping Shanghai's summer heat, and it is the same proposition today.
- Golden Weeks (May Day, National Day) spike hard: **high-end guesthouse occupancy hit 94 percent over the May 2024 holiday**. National Day 2025 daily footfall in Yucun rose 30 to 40 percent over normal.
- Spring (bamboo shoots, meadows) and clear late autumn are both promoted shoulder seasons.
- Weekday and weekend split is severe. Weekends and holidays drive volume. Mondays are notably quiet.
- **Ticket prices conflict across sources**: one gives RMB 120 online / RMB 130 at the gate, half price in winter; another gives RMB 50 off-season / RMB 80 peak. **Say prices vary and should be checked at booking. Do not state one price as fact.**

## 5. History — the hill station

- American Northern Baptist missionary **E. N. Fletcher** identified Moganshan as a summer retreat, dated variously **1891 to 1894**.
- **First Western villas date to 1896 to 1898.** (Sources conflict on who was first: George A. Hudson, American Presbyterian, 1898; or an American named W. W. White, or an English merchant, 1896.) Safe phrasing: "Founded by American Protestant missionaries in the 1890s; the first Western villas date to 1896 to 1898."
- **The Moganshan Summer Resort Association (莫干山避暑会) was formally constituted in 1898.** Its annual meeting was the first Thursday of mid-August.
- **By 1910: about 300 foreigners, mostly American and British**, held summer homes there. Missionaries, businessmen, customs officials and their families. Churches and public halls were built.
- **1920s: 154 Western-style villas, of which 118 were foreign-owned.**
- **1929: foreign-owned villas had fallen to 78.**
- **1928: the Moganshan Management Bureau was established**, returning Chinese administrative control.
- **1928 to 1949: Chinese owners built 300+ further villas.**
- **About 250 historic villas survive today** (some sources say "200+"). Local mountain stone, in British, American, French, German and Chinese-eclectic styles. Chinese sources call it an "architecture of all nations" collection.
- **First tennis court: 1901.** A tennis association followed; by **1909** the Moganshan open championship was a significant Sino-foreign social fixture. Public swimming pools proliferated in the 1930s.
- A **Scottish physician-missionary named Maitland built an English-castle-style villa in 1910**, later restored as naked Castle.
- Academic source: **Hayley Keon, "A Peaceful Place: Raising Imperial Children at China's Moganshan Hill Station," Journal of Imperial and Commonwealth History, published online 9 January 2026, DOI 10.1080/03086534.2025.2609226.**

### Political history
| Figure | Site | Detail |
|---|---|---|
| Chiang Kai-shek | Songyue Lodge, Wuling village | Honeymooned there with Soong Mei-ling, **December 1927**. Returned in **1948** as President, holding currency-reform meetings. |

Write only that he stayed there in 1954, in the year the first constitution was adopted. Do not claim he wrote or confirmed it there.** |
| Du Yuesheng and Zhang Xiaolin | — | The two Green Gang bosses built an ornate Chinese-style villa in **1935**. |

- **1940:** Moganshan became headquarters of the CCP's West Zhejiang Special Committee.
- **1949:** foreigners departed. Villas were redistributed to work units or requisitioned.
- **1984:** the "Moganshan Conference", a landmark gathering of young reform economists, was held in the assembly hall.
- **2003: Mark Kitto**, a British former magazine publisher and author of *China Cuckoo*, took a lease and opened **Moganshan Lodge**. Described as the first foreigner to live on the mountain in modern times.
- **2007: Grant Horsfield**, South African, founded **naked Xiang / naked Home Village** at Sanjiuwu, the first "yangjiale" (洋家乐, "foreigner's farmstay").
- **2011: naked Stables Private Reserve** opened, **the first LEED Platinum certified resort in mainland China**.
- Operators from France, the UK, Belgium, Denmark, the Netherlands, South Korea and Russia followed. Investors in Moganshan guesthouses come from **18 countries**.
- **moganshanlodge.com lapsed and now hosts an unrelated gambling site.** Do not link to it.

## 6. Accommodation — the minsu economy

- "Minsu" (民宿) is the Chinese term for a small, owner-run guesthouse or boutique guesthouse. "Yangjiale" (洋家乐) is the local term for the foreign-founded farmstays.
- Growth: 1 property in 2002 → 70 (2013) → about 200 (2014) → 400+ (2015) → 550+ (2016) → 700+ with about 10,000 beds (2018) → 800+ (2024) → 850+ with 10,000+ beds (March 2025) → **about 1,000 (September 2025)**.
- Local government capped bed capacity at about **10,000** in late 2017 and tightened approvals. Actual stock has drifted above it.
- **Occupancy: about 70 percent before 2021, about 55 percent in 2024. High-end segment 68 percent in 2025.**
- **Payback periods lengthened from 3 to 5 years out to 5 to 8 years.** Labour is 30 to 40 percent of revenue. About 80 percent of properties are individually owned.
- Rates: mid-range off-season **RMB 300 to 500**; mid-range peak **RMB 1,000+**; high-end threshold **RMB 1,500+**. Units priced above RMB 1,500 grew **42 percent year on year**. naked Stables and naked Castle band roughly **USD 291 to 524**.
- Deqing minsu sector hosts **over 7 million guests a year**, revenue **over RMB 3 billion**.
- Chinese reporting notes the villages **lack an evening economy**: no night market, little late-night food.
- **Four Seasons announced a Moganshan resort on 12 September 2025: 90 keys on a 23-hectare site in Yu Village, opening 2030.** Developer is Zhejiang Shangyin Cultural Tourism Development Co., a subsidiary of the Deqing County Cultural and Tourism Development Group. Its press release describes it as "Moganshan's first international luxury resort with direct access to natural hot springs".
- Trip.com acquired **67.04 percent of Dalezhiye** in March 2025 (28 properties, 556 rooms, average rate about RMB 1,300).

## 8. China entry rules (for the visa page)

- **50 countries** hold unilateral visa-free access to mainland China as of February 2026. The trial expansion began **1 December 2023** with six countries; before that only Singapore and Brunei held unilateral exemptions.
- Permitted stay was **doubled from 15 to 30 days in November 2024**.

## 11. The sword legend (added 5 August 2026)

The name Moganshan comes from **Mo Ye (莫邪) and Gan Jiang (干将)**, a swordsmith couple of the Spring and Autumn period who, in legend, forged blades for the King of Wu. **Sword Pond (Jianchi, 剑池)** is said to be where the blades were quenched.

Source status: this appears in the Visit Moganshan brand direction document of 4 August 2026 and in general Chinese cultural reference. It is **legend, not documented history**. Always write it as a legend, never as fact. Do not attach dates to it beyond "the Spring and Autumn period". Do not claim archaeological evidence.
