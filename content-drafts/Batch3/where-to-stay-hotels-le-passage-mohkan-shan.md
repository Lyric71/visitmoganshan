# Le Passage Mohkan Shan

Le Passage Mohkan Shan is a hotel on Moganshan. Place the mountain first: Zhejiang Province, Huzhou prefecture, Deqing County. Hangzhou lies 60 km south of it, Shanghai 200 to 240 km away. The M50 art district on Shanghai's Moganshan Road is a different thing entirely, and it soaks up most of the search traffic for the name.

Before anything else. We have not stayed here or photographed it. This site awards no stars or scores and holds no sponsored placements. We do not repeat other people's review numbers either. What this page does is describe. A rating would need a stay behind it.

For this one, the record is three facts long, so this is a short page.

## The spelling, which decides what you find

The hotel writes its name as Mohkan Shan. The mountain is Moganshan. Search engines treat those as different words.

So try both. "Le Passage Mohkan Shan" is the property's own form. "Le Passage Moganshan" is what most people type. If one gives you nothing, run the other.

## Getting there, and the question we cannot answer

You still have to get there, and the route in is the same for everyone.

| Leg | Detail |
|---|---|
| Rail | Deqing is your station. From Shanghai Hongqiao the run is 63 to 80 minutes, 13 direct trains daily. From Hangzhou East, 13 to 17 minutes on 16 services |
| Last stretch | About 30 to 40 minutes from Deqing station to Moganshan town. Van or taxi about RMB 100, Y1 shuttle RMB 10 |
| The car rule | No private vehicle may pass the scenic-area boundary. You change vehicles at Yucun, Fatou or Houwu, whichever serves your road in |
| The window | All three transfer centers open at 08:00 and close at 18:00 |

> Rail times, transfer times and scenic-area vehicle rules, BeyondBorder Group Ltd primary research, 1 to 5 August 2026.

That 18:00 close is what wrecks evening arrivals. Whether it wrecks yours depends on which side of that boundary your hotel stands, and we do not know. Ask two things when you book: whether the hotel sits inside the scenic area, and which transfer center serves it.

One last trap. A railway station named Moganshan exists, on a different line, and it drops you in Wukang, the county town. [Getting around](/getting-here/getting-around/) has the shuttle timetable and the transfer centers in full.

## What to do with this page

Use it as a checklist, not a review. Search both spellings, confirm on a platform that rooms are on sale, get a rate in writing, and ask whether you will be sleeping inside the scenic area.
