# Moganshan destination fact sheet

This is the fact sheet behind [the trade section](/trade/) and [why Moganshan](/trade/why-moganshan/). **Measured** means a figure from a source, **estimate** a range we derived. Where sources disagree, both appear.

The destination: Moganshan, a mountain in Deqing County, Huzhou prefecture, Zhejiang Province, China. Hangzhou 60 km away, Shanghai 200 to 240 km.

## Moganshan destination information: location and geography

| Item | Figure |
|---|---|
| Distances | about 60 km to Hangzhou, 200 to 240 km to Shanghai |
| Main peak | Tashan, 719 to 720 m |
| Core scenic area | about 20 km² |
| Forest cover | over 92 percent, almost entirely moso bamboo |
| Historic villas standing | about 250 (estimate) |

> Geography measured, villa count estimated. BeyondBorder Group Ltd research, 1 to 5 August 2026.

Other sources put the peak at 758 m. Two further disagreements: the wider area at 43 km², the villa count at "200+". More on [the mountain](/moganshan/).

## Access

| Leg | Time | Frequency or distance | Fare |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hongqiao to Deqing | 63 to 80 min | 13 direct a day | RMB 61 to 110 |
| Hangzhou East to Deqing | 13 to 17 min | 16 a day, 35 km | RMB 16 to 67 |
| Deqing to Moganshan town, Y1 | 30 to 40 min | 7 a day | RMB 10 |
| Deqing to Moganshan town, taxi | about 30 min | on demand | about RMB 100 |
| Shanghai to Moganshan, road | 2 to 3 h | 200 to 240 km | n/a |
| Hangzhou to Moganshan, road | about 1.5 h | 60 to 63 km | n/a |
| Shanghai to Deqing, coach | 2h40 to 4h | direct | from RMB 69 |
| Transfer centers | 08:00 to 18:00 | Yucun, Fatou, Houwu | free |

> Rail, road, shuttle and transfer-center figures, measured. BeyondBorder Group Ltd research, 1 to 5 August 2026.

Y1 departures: 07:30, 08:40, 09:40, 10:40, 13:00, 15:00, 16:00, returning until 17:00. It ends in Moganshan town, not the scenic area.

Deqing has been on the Nanjing to Hangzhou high-speed line since 2013. The Shanghai to Huzhou line of 26 December 2024 puts Huzhou 55 minutes from Hongqiao, though Deqing is closer.

That is the easy half. Private vehicles cannot enter the scenic area, so every party transfers, which is where most itineraries come apart. [Getting here](/getting-here/) explains it.

Door to door, estimates: 2h30 to 3h15 central Shanghai, 2h45 to 3h30 Hongqiao, 4h00 to 4h45 Pudong, 1h20 to 1h45 Hangzhou.

## The hill station in dates

| Date | Event |
|---|---|
| 1891 to 1894 | E. N. Fletcher, American Baptist missionary, picks the site |
| 1896 to 1898 | first Western villas. Sources differ on who built first |
| 1898 to 1910 | Summer Resort Association formed. About 300 foreigners hold homes |
| 1920s | 154 Western-style villas, 118 foreign-owned |
| 1928 to 1949 | Chinese control returns, owners add 300+ villas, foreigners leave |
| 1937 | Zhou Enlai holds secret KMT and CCP talks at Baiyun Shanguan |
| 1954 | Mao Zedong stays in the former "Empress Hotel", built 1934 |
| 2003 to 2007 | Mark Kitto opens Moganshan Lodge, Grant Horsfield founds naked Home Village |
| 2011 | naked Stables opens, mainland China's first LEED Platinum resort |

> Hill-station chronology, measured. BeyondBorder Group Ltd research, 1 to 5 August 2026.

Local sources tie Mao's 1954 stay to the first PRC constitution. Do not print that: the drafting is documented in Hangzhou.

> Hayley Keon, "A Peaceful Place: Raising Imperial Children at China's Moganshan Hill Station", Journal of Imperial and Commonwealth History, 9 January 2026.

## Accommodation stock and rates

| Measure | Figure |
|---|---|
| Guesthouses (minsu) | about 1,000, September 2025 |
| Beds | 10,000+, March 2025, against a 2017 cap of 10,000 |
| Occupancy | about 55 percent in 2024, high end 68 percent in 2025 |
| Mid-range room | RMB 300 to 500 off-season, RMB 1,000+ at peak |
| High-end threshold | RMB 1,500+. naked Stables and Castle, USD 291 to 524 |
| Deqing minsu sector | over 7 million guests, RMB 3 billion+ yearly |

> Stock and rate bands, measured, rates 2025. BeyondBorder Group Ltd research, 1 to 5 August 2026.

Behind the stock, the economics have tightened. Rooms above RMB 1,500 grew 42 percent year on year, and payback now runs 5 to 8 years, up from 3 to 5. No village has an evening economy.

Two lower readings exist: the summer gap about 5 °C, January about 3.5 °C. Ticket prices are worse: RMB 120 online and RMB 130 at the gate in one set, RMB 50 and RMB 80 in another.

## The three name traps

| Trap | The confusion |
|---|---|
| Moganshan Road | the M50 art district in Putuo, Shanghai, 200 km away |
| Moganshan Veneer | a plywood and veneer brand. No connection |
| Moganshan railway station | serves Wukang town in Deqing County, not the mountain. Book Deqing |

A fourth: Moganshan's Yucun (庾村) is not Anji's Yucun (余村), the 2021 UN Tourism winner.
