## What a cookie is

A cookie is a small text file that a website asks your browser to store. Load another page and the browser hands it back, so the site knows it is the same visitor.

Some cookies are needed for a site to work at all: they remember that you are signed in, or which language you picked. Others count visitors, or follow you across other sites so advertising can be aimed at you. The technology is the same. Purpose separates them, and purpose is what most privacy laws care about.

Other storage does the same job under other names, including local storage and tracking pixels. A real notice covers those.
