## What this site is

visitmoganshan.com is an English-language guide to Moganshan, a mountain in Deqing County, Zhejiang Province, China. The publisher is BeyondBorder Group Ltd, registered in Hong Kong. [About](/about/) covers who we are and how the site is funded.

It is an editorial publication. Use what is useful, and check the time-sensitive parts before you travel.

## It is not a booking service

We sell nothing. No booking engine, no cart.

We are not your travel agent or tour operator, and we do not act as agent for any hotel, guesthouse or transport company. Book with one and your contract is with them, under their terms, not ours.

## Accuracy, and its limits

The method is on [About](/about/). This is the part that affects you.

Every figure traces to a dated fact base. If a number is not in it, it does not reach a page. It produces three habits you will notice on any page.

| What we do | What it means for you |
|---|---|
| Date every figure that can go stale | The page says when it was checked, not that it is right today |
| Publish conflicting sources as a conflict | Two sets of scenic-area [ticket prices](/plan/tickets-and-entry/) disagree, so we print both |
| Label gaps with `TODO: verify` | A marked gap is a gap, and you should not read past it |

Prices, timetables, opening hours and entry rules move. Some of this will be out of date before you read it. Check anything that costs you money or a connection.

## Third-party information

None of the time-sensitive numbers here are ours.

| What | Who publishes it |
|---|---|
| Train and shuttle times | The railway or shuttle operator |
| Ticket prices | The scenic-area authority |

We report what those sources published, on the date shown.

We give no warranty that any of it is current or correct. If a train time here is wrong and you miss a train, that risk is yours, and we say so rather than bury it in a long clause.
