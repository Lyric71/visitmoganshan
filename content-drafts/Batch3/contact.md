# Contact

Three things bring people here. Corrections come first, because a correction is the most useful thing you can send us.

Before any of that: no address, form or response time is live yet. The last section explains where that stands.

## Corrections

This site is desk research, compiled in Hong Kong. Nobody on the writing side has walked the mountain yet, as [About](/about/) says. Some of it will be wrong, and we would rather hear it from you than leave it standing.

We take corrections from anyone. A reader caught out by a ticket price. A guesthouse owner who knows the shuttle times moved. A driver, a guide, a county official, an editor.

### What a useful correction contains

- **The page.** Give us the URL or the page title.
- **The sentence.** Quote the wording you think is wrong. Pages get rewritten, so a quote is easier to find than a description.
- **The source.** A timetable, a price board, a booking screen, an official notice. Your own experience counts too. Tell us when it happened.

### What happens next

We check the claim against our dated fact base. If your correction holds up, we change the page and move its last-checked date so you can see it moved.

Sometimes we cannot confirm either version. The claim comes out and a `TODO: verify` marker goes in. Swapping one unverified number for another is not a correction.

A property can ask for a correction like anyone else. It cannot ask for a removal.

## Travel trade and media

Operators, DMCs and editors should start at [For the travel trade and media](/trade/), which links the [destination fact sheet](/trade/fact-sheet/) and the [itinerary modules](/trade/sample-itineraries/). Most answers are there.

If you still need something, tell us your publication or company, the subject, the use and your deadline.

We do not supply property ratings or a best-hotel list. We cannot give you an inbound visitor figure for Deqing either, because nobody publishes one.
