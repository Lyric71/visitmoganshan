## Editorial rules in force

- **No FAQ sections.** Questions get answered in the body section where a reader would already be looking.
- **No invented facts.** Unverified items carry a `TODO: verify` marker. There are **244** of them across the corpus, deliberately.
- **Conflicts published as conflicts.** Ticket prices, the founding date, the first-villa builder and the January mean temperature are all given as ranges with the disagreement named.
- **Hotels described, never rated.** No property is scored, ranked or given quality adjectives, because nobody on the site has stayed anywhere. `/where-to-stay/best-hotels/` refuses to publish a ranking and explains why.
- **Legal pages are marked drafts** requiring review by a qualified lawyer before publication. Nothing about data handling is asserted.
- **No two pages open the same way.** A corpus-wide pass on 6 August removed 28 near-identical placement openings.
