description: "The place that started Moganshan's second life as a weekend destination, and still the largest thing on the mountain. Rooms are round thatched cabins on steel stilts and rammed earth cottages, spread across a wooded ridge above a reservoir at Lanshukeng, a few minutes below Moganshan town. There is an outdoor pool, a spa, three restaurants, a kids' club and a conference room that seats about forty, which tells you who books it: families, and companies running an offsite. Rates open around US$339 a night and climb steeply for the larger units. It is the least Chinese-feeling stay on this mountain, for better and worse."
note: "It is a resort, and it behaves like one. The buildings are spread far enough apart that you will use the shuttle rather than walk, meals are effectively on site because nothing else is within reach on foot, and the whole thing is priced for that captivity. Guests raising problems on Trip.com mostly raise the same two: rooms that are showing their age, and slow service when the place is full."
