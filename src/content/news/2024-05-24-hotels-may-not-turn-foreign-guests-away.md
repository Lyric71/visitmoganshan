A joint directive of 24 May 2024 bars hotels from refusing foreign guests for lack of a licence. Three bodies signed it:

- Ministry of Public Security
- Ministry of Commerce
- National Immigration Administration

Refusals are still reported. Two years on, the directive has not settled it on the ground, and the smaller the property the less likely it is to have heard of it.

What a guesthouse can and cannot ask of a foreign passport is the subject of a longer piece in the editorial calendar. Until then, [what a minsu is](/where-to-stay/minsu-explained/) explains the registration that sits behind the refusals.
