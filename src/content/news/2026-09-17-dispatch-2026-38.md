## A country park is due to open at Fatou in October

The old street at Fatou (筏头老街), the settlement that gives the Fatou
transfer centre its name, has been rebuilt as Mogan Lake Country Park
(莫干湖郊野公园). Deqing News reported on 16 September that the civil works
are finished and that the buildings, a cycling station, a ruins garden and an
ecology exhibition hall, are in their final stage. The site covers about
48,000 square metres and the county expects it to open in October. It sits
inside a drinking water protection zone, which is why the old street became a
park rather than anything with beds in it.

**What this means for a visitor:** from October there should be a free public
park with paths and viewpoints beside a transfer centre that has until now
been somewhere to leave a car and wait for a bus.

## A farm park at Laoling is open, and also not open

A site of about 200 mu at Laoling (劳岭村), roughly 13 hectares, has become
Luhe Village (麓禾村), built out of an existing fruit picking farm by a young
team from Hangzhou. Deqing News listed a pedal train, off road karting,
kayaks, catering, camping, farm work sessions and eight themed courtyards.
The same report says in its first line that the park has formally opened to
the public and then quotes the operator saying it has not formally opened for
business, so treat it as trading but unfinished. She expects 100,000 visitors
a year once it is running, and said the team is planning around the Hangzhou
to Deqing rail line, which
[began trial running on 2 September](/journal/news/2026/09/hangzhou-to-deqing-rail-line-begins-trial-running).

**What this means for a visitor:** Laoling sits on the road to the western
guesthouse villages and on one of the two themed bus lines, so a family half
day now exists there that did not in the spring. Ring ahead before you build a
day around it.

## The ninth Moganshan Conference is in its final run up

The task force preparing the ninth Moganshan Conference (莫干山会议) met on
14 September and was told that preparation has entered its sprint stage. The
conference descends from the 1984 meeting that put the mountain into Chinese
economic history. The report named the officials in the room and gave no
date, no venue and no programme.

**What this means for a visitor:** when the date does land, beds across the
county tighten for several days around it. If your trip falls in late
September or October, book the room before the announcement rather than
after.

## A restored walk at Jichoushan is finished, away from the mountain

At Shangyang (上杨村), in Xiazhuhu (下渚湖) rather than on Moganshan, the main
works are done on a walking route around Jichoushan (计筹山). Deqing News put
it at 520 metres of restored old path and more than 400 metres of new
connecting path, about one kilometre in all, for more than RMB 2 million. It
links a Yuan dynasty cliff inscription cut by Zhao Mengfu, the Baishi Dongtian
site associated with the Song poet Jiang Kui, a Taoist hall and a flat stone
long used as a chessboard.

**What this means for a visitor:** about a kilometre of restored path, with a
signed entrance, for anyone who has already done the walking on Moganshan and
wants somewhere with fewer people on it. No opening date was given.

## The county recovered RMB 61,000 from an agency that stopped answering

The enforcement team at the county culture, radio, television, tourism and
sport bureau has recovered RMB 61,000 of the more than RMB 80,000 taken by a
travel agency that went quiet. Deqing News reported on 16 September that the
company registered in January, ran a few tours, then deregistered in March.
More than 60 customers, most of them elderly, had paid by personal WeChat
transfer rather than into a company account, and the agency's mandatory
service quality deposit could not be used to cover them.

**What this means for a visitor:** if you book anything through a Chinese
agency, pay into the company account, ask to see the travel agency operating
licence as well as the business licence, and keep the written contract. A
personal transfer leaves almost nothing to recover.
