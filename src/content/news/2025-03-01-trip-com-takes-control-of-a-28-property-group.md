Trip.com acquired 67.04 percent of Dalezhiye (大乐之野) in March 2025. The platform that sells rooms here now owns some.

| The deal | Figure |
|---|---|
| Stake acquired | 67.04 percent |
| Properties | 28 |
| Rooms | 556 |
| Average rate | about RMB 1,300 |

Moganshan's beds are otherwise small and local. About 80 percent of the area's guesthouses have a single owner, and Moganshan Town counted about 1,000 of them by September 2025. Twenty eight under one platform is a different animal.

[Where to stay](/where-to-stay/) has the price bands. Trip.com is also this site's affiliate partner, which is why the deal is reported here for what it changes and not for the deal itself.
