# The Kailas Moganshan Skyrace: what a 100k trail race does to a small mountain

A 100 km trail race sounds like a niche event for a few hundred hard cases. On
Moganshan it fills the mountain. The Kailas Moganshan
Skyrace (凯乐石莫干山跑山赛), a Moganshan trail race that started with 800
runners in 2017, put more than 11,000 on the mountain in October 2025. This
year it runs from Friday 23 to Sunday 25 October, and entries are closed. If
you aren't running, the race still decides whether you find a bed and which
roads are open. If your room is on the course, it decides how much you sleep.

Moganshan (莫干山) is a bamboo mountain and former hill station in Deqing
County (德清县), Huzhou (湖州), Zhejiang, about 60 km north of Hangzhou and 200
to 240 km from Shanghai. It isn't Moganshan Road in Shanghai, the M50 art
district. The race starts and finishes on Yuxin North Street (庾信北街) in
[Yucun](/moganshan/villages/yucun/) (庾村), the market town where the road up
the mountain begins.

written sources.

## The Moganshan trail race is seven races

The name sells the 100 km. The weekend is seven races, staggered so that
somebody is on the paths from Friday evening to Sunday afternoon. The real
distances are not always the round ones.

| Category | Actual distance | Climb | Start | Cut off |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 100 km | 99 km | 5,903 m | Fri 18:00 | Sun 00:00 (30 hours) |
| 70 km | 67.2 km | 3,618 m | Fri 19:00 | Sat 14:00 (19 hours) |
| 50 km | 50.3 km | 2,615 m | Sat 06:00 | Sat 21:00 (15 hours) |
| 20 km | 23.1 km | 1,049 m | Sat 08:30 | Sat 16:30 (8 hours) |
| 30 km | 31.6 km | 1,570 m | Sun 06:00 | Sun 16:00 (10 hours) |
| 10 km and youth 10 km | 8.5 km | 460 m | Sun 09:00 and 09:30 | 3 hours |

The two longest races start in the dark on Friday, so the slowest 100 km
runners are still out on Saturday night, more than a day after they left. The
organiser sends the course file to entrants only, so this page can't print
the route. Walkers on the upper paths that weekend should expect runners at
any hour.

Entry cost RMB 350 for the 10 km and RMB 1,400 for the 100 km. The longer
races want a qualifying finish from the past year (a 50 km trail race before
the 100 km, for example). There's no late place to buy. Running on somebody
else's number gets the runner and the number's owner banned.

> Distances, climb, start and cut off times, fees, qualifying finishes and the
> ban for running on another person's number, Letour Sports (朗途体育), race
> rules, 9 February 2026, tier 3.
> https://moganshan.saihuitong.com/article?id=69954&mid=57201

![Trail runners seen from behind climbing a narrow stone path through bamboo at night, their headtorches making a broken line of light up the slope](/images/guide/kailas-moganshan-skyrace-2.webp 'The 100 km and 70 km start on Friday evening, so the paths above the villages carry headtorches through two nights.')

## From 800 runners to 11,000

The first race ran on 28 October 2017. About 800 people started, and each
finisher took home a medal shaped as a sword, after Gan Jiang and Mo Ye
(干将莫邪), the swordsmith couple in the legend behind the mountain's name.
Renamed the Moganshan Skyrace in 2018, it drew more than 3,000 runners that
year and 5,000 in 2019. It paused for the pandemic and came back with new
landmarks on the course, the Sword Testing Cliff (试剑崖) and Mogan Ridge
(莫干岭) among them.

When entries for 2025 opened that February, applications passed 10,000 on
the first day, against 9,000 places. The organiser added 2,000.

> 800 runners on 28 October 2017, 3,000 in 2018, 5,000 in 2019, the sword
> medal, the new landmarks, the 2025 applications and places, Deqing News
> (德清新闻网), 10 April 2025, tier 2.
> http://dqnews.zjol.com.cn/dqnews/system/2025/04/10/034924244.shtml

On race weekend in October 2025 more than 11,000 started, in 14 waves across
five categories. The organiser had 97 foreign nationals on the entry list.

> More than 11,000 runners, five categories, 14 waves, 97 foreign entrants,
> Zhejiang Online (浙江在线) carrying Tide News (潮新闻), 27 October 2025,
> tier 2. https://zjnews.zjol.com.cn/zjnews/202510/t20251027_31306828.shtml

The county paper and the provincial press call that field an Asian record.
Nobody publishes the list it beats, so treat the phrase as the resort's
description rather than a measured fact. The organiser bills 2026 as the
race's tenth year and hasn't said how many will start.

## What 11,000 runners do to the beds

Xinhua counted nearly 900 licensed guesthouses around Moganshan in September
2026. Set a field of 11,000 against that, then add the partners and children
many runners bring.

> Nearly 900 licensed guesthouses (近900家合规民宿), Xinhua News Agency
> (新华社), 29 September 2026, tier 2.
> http://www.zj.xinhuanet.com/20260929/2bb0c09f3dc547deb5beb818d6b25693/c.html

In 2025 rooms near the course were hard to find a week before the start, and
more than 100 guesthouses and hotels were full two nights running. A runner
friendly alliance (跑者友好联盟) of more than 100 guesthouses and restaurants
offered late checkout, cheaper breakfasts and free transfers.

> Rooms hard to find a week before, the alliance and its offers, Zhejiang
> Online (浙江在线) carrying Tide News (潮新闻), 27 October 2025, tier 2.
> https://zjnews.zjol.com.cn/zjnews/202510/t20251027_31306828.shtml

The bigger numbers come from the resort's management, not a statistics
bureau, and this site prints them as its claims: more than 30,000 overnight
visitors over the 2025 weekend, and nearly RMB 100 million spent locally, up
more than 300 percent on the period before.

> Full houses, overnight visitors and spending, the last two from the
> resort's data, Zhejiang Online (浙江在线) carrying Hangzhou Daily (杭州日报),
> 29 October 2025, tier 2.
> https://zjnews.zjol.com.cn/zjnews/202510/t20251029_31312010.shtml

If you want a quiet weekend in the bamboo, 23 to 25 October is the wrong one.
Come the week after.

![Muddy trail shoes and a race vest drying on a guesthouse terrace railing in grey morning light, a plastic bin and a small folding bicycle crowding the corner](/images/guide/kailas-moganshan-skyrace-3.webp 'Ask a guesthouse before booking whether its lane is on the course: a house on the route hears runners pass all night.')

## Roads, shuttles and the night

We found no traffic notice for the 2026 race by 15 October. Last year's came
out on 17 October, a week before the start, from the resort committee, the
county traffic police and the organiser. It put Yuxin North Street under
traffic control for three days. Zhuyuan Road (竹源路), Huangfu Road (黄郛路),
the Yulan road (于烂线) and Cuiying Road (翠莹路) shut in phases to cars,
scooters and bicycles, and part of Huangfu Road, the town's main street, ran
one way. The police set the hours on the day.

> Phased closures for 24 to 26 October 2025, Deqing traffic police (德清交警),
> notice of 17 October 2025, as reprinted by the 108 community forum
> (德清108生活社区), tier 4. https://m.108sq.cn/deqing/shuo/detail/69640851

Expect the same shape this year, and send your guesthouse your arrival time
before you travel. Private cars already stop below the scenic area; [the car
ban](/plan/private-car-ban-on-moganshan/) explains the rule.

The organiser sells race shuttle seats at RMB 15 from Deqing station to the
Moganshan transfer centre (换乘中心) on 23 and 24 October, and from Deqing
West on the 24th. The listing doesn't say whether someone without a race
number can book. [Deqing station](/getting-here/deqing-station/) covers the
ordinary way up.

> Race shuttle seats at RMB 15, Letour Sports (朗途体育), event page, read 15
> October 2026, tier 3. https://moganshan.saihuitong.com/events?mid=72300

It takes a lot of people. In 2024 the race used more than 1,100 volunteers, 207
rescuers, 77 rescue vehicles and 24 coaches across 17 stations. Villagers shut
their dogs in so they don't chase the runners.

> Volunteers, rescuers, vehicles, coaches, stations and the dogs, Deqing News
> (德清新闻网), 10 April 2025, tier 2.
> http://dqnews.zjol.com.cn/dqnews/system/2025/04/10/034924244.shtml

![A small aid table under a tarpaulin at a village junction after dark, a volunteer seen from behind ladling soup, steam lit by a bare bulb and rain on the lens](/images/guide/kailas-moganshan-skyrace-4.webp 'Village stations hand out home cooked food; a supporter may help a runner only at an official station, never on the course.')

## Watching it without a race number

The rules are strict about company. Nobody may run alongside an entrant, and
help from a friend is allowed only at an official station. Shops and stalls
along the way are fair game, and the villages make the most of it. In 2025
villagers ran 23 "energy stations" (能量加油站) with chicken, sweet potatoes
and green rice cakes, and stood at dark junctions with torches.

> No companions on the course, support only at stations, purchases allowed,
> Letour Sports (朗途体育), race rules, 9 February 2026, tier 3.
> https://moganshan.saihuitong.com/article?id=69954&mid=57201

> 23 village energy stations and torches at junctions, Zhejiang Online
> (浙江在线) carrying Hangzhou Daily (杭州日报), 29 October 2025, tier 2.
> https://zjnews.zjol.com.cn/zjnews/202510/t20251029_31312010.shtml

The easiest place to watch is the finish. In 2025 Mogan Yujie (莫干·庾街),
Yucun's riverside street, held a market of more than 60 stalls and a concert,
and drew more than 20,000 people on the opening night. The 100 km finishers
trickle in from Saturday into Sunday; the 10 km runners come in on Sunday
late morning.

> More than 60 stalls and 20,000 people on the opening night, Zhejiang Online
> (浙江在线) carrying Tide News (潮新闻), 27 October 2025, tier 2.
> https://zjnews.zjol.com.cn/zjnews/202510/t20251027_31306828.shtml

## What stays on the mountain

The county has been building for races. Deqing News reported that it
had restored 35 km of old paths and built 100 km of trails and 75 km of cycle
paths, enough for races up to 100 km. In January 2026 the resort announced
ten new routes and more than ten branded races for the year, [Ultra Trail
Mogan](/things-to-do/utmb-mogan/) among them.

> 35 km of old paths restored, 100 km of trails, 75 km of cycle paths, Deqing
> News (德清新闻网), 10 April 2025, tier 2.
> http://dqnews.zjol.com.cn/dqnews/system/2025/04/10/034924244.shtml

> Ten new routes and more than ten branded races in 2026, Deqing News
> (德清新闻网), 19 January 2026, tier 2.
> http://dqnews.zjol.com.cn/dqnews/system/2026/01/19/035134575.shtml

On litter the rules are firm. Stations hand out no disposable cups, a runner
caught dropping rubbish is disqualified, and the organiser collects its
course markers afterwards. We found no published survey of the paths after a
race weekend. The wear on the old stone steps is unmeasured.

> No disposable cups, disqualification for litter, markers collected, Letour
> Sports (朗途体育), race rules, 9 February 2026, tier 3.
> https://moganshan.saihuitong.com/article?id=69954&mid=57201

If the race weekend is the one you have, book the room first and plan the
rest after. Rooms across the resort are listed on [Trip.com](/go/stays-all/),
and [where to stay](/where-to-stay/) explains the villages.
