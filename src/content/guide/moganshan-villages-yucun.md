# Yucun: the village you can visit without a ticket

Most visitors treat Yucun as somewhere to pass through on the way to something
they have paid for. They have it backwards. Yucun sits at the foot of Moganshan,
below the scenic area gate. Its old silkworm farm, a Republic era library, a
gallery and a new riverside street carry no admission price in any source we can
reach.

Moganshan (莫干山) is a bamboo mountain and former hill station in Deqing County
(德清县), Huzhou (湖州), Zhejiang, about 60 km north of Hangzhou and 200 to 240 km
from Shanghai. It is not Moganshan Road in Shanghai, the M50 art district in
Putuo. Nor is this Yucun (庾村) the Yucun in Anji County, written 余村, a different
village that the UN World Tourism Organization named a Best Tourism Village in
2021. Search results mix the two up constantly.

> Anji's 余村 among the first Best Tourism Villages named on 2 December 2021,
> Ministry of Culture and Tourism (文化和旅游部), 2 December 2021, tier 1.
> https://www.mct.gov.cn/whzx/whyw/202112/t20211202_929544.htm

## Yucun, Moganshan: a town, not a village

Yucun is the market town where the road up the mountain begins. The
administrative village, the unit a Chinese postal address uses, is Liaoyuan
(燎原村), and that is the name a booking confirmation will usually print. Of the
809 Moganshan listings we hold, 73 give a Liaoyuan address and 24 give Huangfu
East or West Road (黄郛东路, 黄郛西路), the town's main streets.

> Addresses on 809 captured Trip.com listings, BeyondBorder Group Ltd primary
> research, captured 8 August 2026, compiled 25 September 2026.

The headline says village because that is what English listings and searches
call it.

The town is also a 4A rated scenic area in its own right. That is the second
highest grade on China's A to 5A scale for tourist sites, and it sounds like a
gate. The only official document we can find that pairs Yucun with a ticket is a
June 2023 Huzhou roll of scenic areas waiving "the first gate ticket" for exam
candidates. The roll prints no price.

> Deqing Yucun scenic area (德清庾村景区), rated 4A, in Liaoyuan village,
> Moganshan town, on the June 2023 roll of Huzhou scenic areas free to exam
> candidates, Zhejiang Online (浙江在线), 9 June 2023, tier 2.
> https://zjnews.zjol.com.cn/zjnews/202306/t20230609_25838009.shtml

The ticket that matters is the scenic area's, up the hill. Yucun is where people
leave their cars for it. It's one of three transfer centres (换乘中心), with Fatou
and Houwu, and the coach up leaves from here.

> Visitors entering the scenic area through transfer centres at Yucun, Fatou
> and Houwu, Deqing News (德清新闻网), 30 September 2019, tier 2.
> https://dqnews.zjol.com.cn/dqnews/system/2019/09/30/031939856.shtml

## What costs nothing in Yucun

Most of the old town comes out of one man's project. Huang Fu (黄郛), who had run
the Republican government's foreign affairs and served as mayor of Shanghai,
retired to Moganshan in 1931 and set about remaking the village below it: trees,
water storage, a school, a library, a livestock farm and a silkworm egg farm. He
died in 1936. His widow, Shen Yiyun (沈亦云), kept the work going until 1949, and
he is buried in Yucun.

> Huang Fu's retirement in 1931, the reform, its end in 1949 and the reuse of
> the silkworm farm, The Paper (澎湃新闻), 16 January 2015, tier 2.
> https://m.thepaper.cn/newsDetail_forward_1294947

| Place | What it is | Entry and hours, as published | Latest dated source |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1932 park (庾村1932文创园) | Eleven buildings of the silkworm farm, now shops, cafés and rooms | Open since October 2013. No price printed anywhere | The Paper, January 2015 |
| Republic library (莫干山民国图书馆) | A hall built in 1932; Republic era books and periodicals | 09:00 to 16:30, closed Mondays, in 2023. Run by a poetry bookshop since June 2026 | Deqing County Library, June 2026 |
| White Cloud art museum (白云美术馆) | Ground floor gallery in the farm buildings, five rooms to let upstairs | Opened 2017. No hours or price published | The Paper, December 2022 |
| The old bus station (莫干山老车站) | An old bus station, now an exhibit on a century of getting to Moganshan | No hours or price published | Zhejiang Online, November 2019 |
| Mogan Yujie (莫干·庾街) | A 1.6 km street along the Fuxi stream, with the Moganshan Art Museum | Open street. Museum price not published | Zhejiang Online, November 2025 |

Start with the park. The designer Zhu Shengxuan (朱胜萱) leased the ground in 2011,
kept the farm's layout, repaired its eleven buildings and opened the first phase
in October 2013. The architect Zhuang Shen (庄慎) strung bamboo awnings between
the yards on a budget of RMB 100,000, and the paths were laid with pebbles from
the stream bed.

> The 2011 lease, the RMB 100,000 awnings and the October 2013 opening, The
> Paper (澎湃新闻), 16 January 2015, tier 2.
> https://m.thepaper.cn/newsDetail_forward_1294947

The White Cloud art museum occupies part of the same buildings. It opened in
2017 with a public gallery on the ground floor and five loft rooms upstairs that
you can book.

> White Cloud art museum, opened 2017, gallery below and five rooms above,
> The Paper (澎湃新闻), reporter 钱雪儿, 12 December 2022, tier 2.
> http://m.cyol.com/gb/articles/2022-12/12/content_X5WbnlCp2d.html

The library is what has changed most. A hall built in 1932 opened as a branch of
the county library in 2016, with more than 600 Republic era periodical titles
running to 20,000 issues.

> The library's 2016 opening and its periodical holdings, Tencent News
> (腾讯新闻), 16 August 2020, tier 3.
> https://news.qq.com/rain/a/20200816A0AL8D00

In June 2026 the county library said a poetry bookshop, Paofuyun (泡芙云), had
taken over running it. It added a bookshop, a small exhibition room, a crafts
corner and a counter for coffee and tea, and kept the Republic collection.

> The 1932 hall now run by 泡芙云诗歌书店, Deqing County Library (德清县图书馆),
> 15 June 2026, tier 1. http://www.dqlib.com.cn/news/newsdetail/8241

The last published hours are 09:00 to 16:30, closed on Mondays. They date from
January 2023. The library's notice for the 2026 Mid Autumn holiday lists its
main building and three city reading rooms and does not mention the Moganshan
branch at all, so treat the old hours as a guide rather than a promise.

> Branch hours of 09:00 to 16:30, Mondays closed, Deqing County Library
> (德清县图书馆), 8 January 2023, tier 1.
> http://www.dqlib.com.cn/news/newsdetail/5266

> Mid Autumn 2026 opening arrangements, Deqing County Library (德清县图书馆),
> 23 September 2026, tier 1. http://www.dqlib.com.cn/news/newsdetail/8428

![Low whitewashed farm buildings with deep tiled eaves around a pebbled yard, a bamboo awning over one side and a bicycle leaning on a wall](/images/guide/yucun-2.webp 'The pebble paths in the park are hard going for wheelchairs and pushchairs, and there is no paved route through the yards in any source we have.')

The old bus station stands where Huangfu East Road meets Huangfu Road, on a
stretch rebuilt as a Republic style street with a photo studio, a cloth shoe
shop and cafés, next to the town square. In 1916, the same report notes,
Shanghai to Moganshan took 14 hours by train and steamboat.

> The old station, the 1916 journey, the street and the square, Zhejiang
> Online (浙江在线), reporter 甘居鹏, 21 November 2019, tier 2.
> https://town.zjol.com.cn/czyc/201911/t20191121_11385215.shtml

In 2020 it held an old car at the door and the station's records inside.

> The old station as an exhibit, Tencent News (腾讯新闻), 16 August 2020,
> tier 3. https://news.qq.com/rain/a/20200816A0AL8D00

Mogan Yujie is the newest part: RMB 1.17 billion of rebuilt factories and public
space along the Fuxi (阜溪). Phase one opened on 1 May 2024 and phase two on 1
October 2025. Its Moganshan Art Museum, in a 1980s metalwork factory, opened on
28 September 2024 at No. 150.

> Mogan Yujie's cost, length, phases and the museum building, Zhejiang Online
> (浙江在线), 3 November 2025, tier 2.
> https://zjnews.zjol.com.cn/zjnews/202511/t20251103_31322043.shtml

> Moganshan Art Museum opened on 28 September 2024, Southern Metropolis Daily
> (南方都市报), 1 October 2024, tier 2.
> https://m.mp.oeeee.com/a/BAAFRD0000202409301005536.html

If your trip falls in either window, Yujie runs an autumn festival from 25 to 27
September and from 1 to 7 October 2026: a produce and crafts market, an
exhibition and a concert at dusk.

> Mogan Yujie autumn festival dates and programme, Deqing News (德清新闻网),
> 24 September 2026, tier 2.
> https://dqnews.zjol.com.cn/dqnews/system/2026/09/24/035297909.shtml

## Eating in Yucun: dishes, not addresses

That's the free half of the town. Lunch is where you start paying.

The dated record names dishes, not restaurants. Before the street was rebuilt, a
2018 account named pig liver noodles, sesame cakes and cloth shoes as the town's
three treasures. A 2020 one sent visitors to bamboo shoots braised with pork and
to wild greens.

> Pig liver noodles, sesame cakes and cloth shoes as Yucun's three treasures,
> The Paper (澎湃新闻), 湃客 contributor column, 9 October 2018, tier 3.
> https://www.thepaper.cn/newsDetail_forward_2502086

> Bamboo shoots braised with pork and wild greens, Tencent News (腾讯新闻),
> 16 August 2020, tier 3. https://news.qq.com/rain/a/20200816A0AL8D00

Neither names a restaurant we could confirm is still trading, so we haven't
repeated either as an address. On a weekday out of season, ask your host what is
open that evening before you walk down.

What has changed is the supply. Phase two of Yujie added a waterside shopping
street, a market and a canteen for the young people working there, and the
street counted more than 60 brands by November 2025. For dinner, that means a
walk rather than a message to your host.

> Phase two's waterside street and market, the canteen and more than 60
> brands, Zhejiang Online (浙江在线), 3 November 2025, tier 2.
> https://zjnews.zjol.com.cn/zjnews/202511/t20251103_31322043.shtml

Choose by the address. The name can mislead: twenty listings put "Yucun Square",
"Yucun Plaza" or a variant in their names. Here is where those twenty say they
are:

## A walk through Yucun

The Fuxi rises at Sword Pond on the mountain and runs down through the Mogaowu
valley into Yucun, so walking up Yujie is walking towards the summit.

> The Fuxi from Sword Pond through Mogaowu to Yucun, Tide News (潮新闻),
> 27 December 2024, tier 2. https://tidenews.com.cn/news.html?id=3000331

This is a desk route: nobody on this desk has walked it. Start at the old bus
station on Huangfu East Road, with the square beside it. Cross to the 1932 park,
which the sources place near the square, and look into the White Cloud gallery
inside it. Then take the stream path up Yujie, 1.6 km to its far end, past the
art museum.

Budget a morning.

![A paved path beside a shallow rocky stream in a hill town, concrete footbridges crossing it and bamboo on the slope above, the light flat and grey](/images/guide/yucun-3.webp 'The footbridges on this stretch came out of an international design competition for seven bridges over the Fuxi.')

[Sword Pond](/things-to-do/sword-pond/), where the stream starts, is a proper
hike from here. [Hiking on Moganshan](/things-to-do/hiking/) sets it out.

## Who should base here

Anyone without a car. The transfer centre is in the town, the coach up leaves
from it, and the timetable has been printed as a first coach up at 08:00 and a
last coach down at 17:00. The fare last printed, in 2023, was RMB 35 return.
After that you're wherever you're sleeping, and in Yucun that means a street
with food on it.

> First coach up at 08:00, last coach down at 17:00 and RMB 35 return,
> Huzhou Bendibao (湖州本地宝), 21 September 2023, tier 3. http://huzhou.bendibao.com/tour/2021123/3560.shtm

A first visit of one or two nights is the other obvious case. [The shuttle
system](/plan/moganshan-shuttle-system/) has the coach times and fares, and [the
ticket piece](/plan/moganshan-tickets-in-2026/) explains why nobody agrees on
what the gate charges.

And if the question is whether Moganshan without a ticket is worth the trip,
start here. You can spend a full day in Yucun without going near the gate.

## The honest downside

Yucun is a town, and since 2024 a busy one. Over the 2025 National Day and Mid
Autumn holiday, Mogan Yujie counted 250,000 visits, up 280 percent on the year
before. Zhejiang Online printed that figure without naming its source, and it is
not a statistics bureau count, so read it as a local tally. Either way, this is
not a quiet village over a holiday.

> 250,000 visits to Mogan Yujie over the 2025 National Day and Mid Autumn
> holiday, up 280 percent, Zhejiang Online (浙江在线), 3 November 2025, tier 2.
> https://zjnews.zjol.com.cn/zjnews/202511/t20251103_31322043.shtml

It can be loud. The street's operator told Tide News that event music drew
complaints from older residents at first.

> Residents' complaints about event music on Mogan Yujie, Tide News (潮新闻),
> 27 December 2024, tier 2. https://tidenews.com.cn/news.html?id=3000331

Building work has not finished either. A youth hostel of about 2,000 m² on Yujie
was under construction in November 2025, due to open in 2026.

> The youth hostel under construction, Zhejiang Online (浙江在线), 3 November
> 2025, tier 2. https://zjnews.zjol.com.cn/zjnews/202511/t20251103_31322043.shtml

![A crowded stream side street at dusk during a holiday, visitors seen from behind under strings of lights, a food stall steaming in the corner](/images/guide/yucun-4.webp 'Venues on this street include a live music room from Nanjing and a poetry bookshop ten times the size of the Shanghai shop that closed.')

None of that describes an ordinary weekday: the 280 percent is a holiday count,
and we found none for a Tuesday in March. If the reason you came to Moganshan is
bamboo and silence, sleep in one of the villages further out and come down to
Yucun for dinner. [The villages below Moganshan](/moganshan/villages/) sets out
what each one offers.
