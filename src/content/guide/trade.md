# For the travel trade and media

This page is for China product managers at tour operators and DMCs, and for travel editors and picture desks. Nothing here sits behind a login or fee.

Moganshan is a mountain in Deqing County, which sits in Huzhou prefecture, Zhejiang Province, China. It stands 60 km north of Hangzhou, and Shanghai is 200 to 240 km off. The main peak, Tashan, is 719 to 720 meters. Two naming traps: Moganshan Road is an art district in Putuo, Shanghai, 200 km away, and the railway station called Moganshan does not serve the mountain. Deqing does.

## Who publishes this

visitmoganshan.com is published by BeyondBorder Group Ltd, Hong Kong.

The site is independent. There are no featured or sponsored placements, and every property is covered on the same terms, from the state-owned Moganshan Hotel to naked Stables to the Four Seasons announced in September 2025 for 2030. We do not rate or review them.

None of that comes from a hosted stay or a press trip. It is desk research, compiled in Hong Kong. Where sources disagree, we publish the disagreement. [About](/about/) has the rest.

## Where Moganshan sits in the trade right now

Our read, and it is a read rather than a measured figure: in the trade, Moganshan is an add-on.

It turns up in the standard inbound-agency catalog as a half day or a single night on a Shanghai and Hangzhou circuit, not as an anchor stay. Closing that gap is why this site exists, and saying so plainly is worth more than a pitch.

There are reasons, worth pricing in before you cost anything.

- Private vehicles cannot enter the scenic area. Every group transfers at one of three centers: Yucun, Fatou, Houwu.
- The villages have no evening economy. No night market, little food late.
- Ticket prices disagree across sources: RMB 120 online and RMB 130 at the gate in one set, RMB 50 off-season and RMB 80 at peak in another. A per-head cost line is hard to fix.
- Neither Huzhou nor Deqing publishes an inbound or foreign-visitor figure. Nobody can size the international market here, and we will not invent a number.

The other side of the ledger is old. A July and August mean of 24.1 °C, 6 to 7 °C under Shanghai, is why American missionaries built a summer town here in the 1890s. About 250 of their stone villas still stand, on a mountain over 92 percent moso bamboo. Deqing County took RMB 39.68 billion in tourism revenue in 2025. The volume is there. How much of it is international, nobody publishes. [Why Moganshan](/trade/why-moganshan/) makes the case.

## Bed stock, occupancy and rates

Access is the easy half. Supply is less tidy.

|---|---|---|

| Beds | 10,000+ | March 2025 |
| Average occupancy | about 55 percent | 2024 |
| High-end occupancy | 68 percent | 2025 |
| Mid-range room, off-season | RMB 300 to 500 | 2025 |
| Mid-range room, peak | RMB 1,000+ | 2025 |
| High-end threshold | RMB 1,500+ | 2025 |

> Bed stock, occupancy and rate bands, BeyondBorder Group Ltd primary research, 1 to 5 August 2026.

The occupancy line rewards a second look. About 55 percent across the area means real midweek availability, unusual this close to Shanghai. Golden Weeks run the other way: high-end occupancy hit 94 percent over the May 2024 holiday, and Yucun footfall ran 30 to 40 percent above normal at National Day 2025.

Entry rules do more work here than they look. The 240-hour visa-free transit in force since 17 December 2024 covers 55 nationalities, the United States among them, and covers Shanghai and Zhejiang, so clients stay inside the permitted provinces. Note the sunset: the wider 30-day scheme is legislated only to 31 December 2026.
