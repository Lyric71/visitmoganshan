# Where to leave your bags: luggage storage at Deqing station and the transfer centre

It's easy to assume that Deqing station, or the transfer centre below the
mountain, will take a suitcase for the day. The written record says
otherwise. No source dated after 2019 confirms any Moganshan luggage storage
on the way up, and the one that does describes a service our own desk check
could not find in August 2026. Plan to keep your bags with you or at your
guesthouse.

Moganshan (莫干山) is a mountain in Deqing County (德清县), Huzhou, Zhejiang,
about 60 km north of Hangzhou and 200 to 240 km from Shanghai. Not Moganshan
Road in Shanghai, the M50 art district. The bag question comes up at Deqing
station and at the transfer centre (换乘中心) below the mountain.

## Moganshan luggage storage, place by place

| Place | What the record says | Date and tier | Any use on arrival? |
|---|---|---|---|
| Deqing station | Lockers inside the waiting hall, behind security | 2018, tier 2 | Only with a ticket out |
| Coach centre beside the station | Opening report names no luggage store | 2018, tier 2 | Unknown |
| Transfer centre, Yucun | A free desk in 2019; none at launch; none found in 2026 | 2018 to 2026, tiers 3 and 4, plus ours | Unconfirmed |
| Your guesthouse | Nothing published | Not published | Ask in writing |
| Hangzhou metro | Lockers at 39 stations, RMB 2 to 5 an hour | 2024, tier 2 | Yes, before the train |

## Deqing station: lockers behind the security line

In July 2018 China Railway Shanghai Group (中国铁路上海局集团) put self service
lockers into the waiting areas of 18 stations, Deqing (德清站) among them. The
large compartment takes a wheeled case, and you pay by scanning a code with
WeChat or Alipay.

> Lockers in the waiting area at 18 stations including Deqing, usable only
> after security, Zhejiang Online (浙江在线), 9 July 2018, tier 2.
> https://zjnews.zjol.com.cn/zjnews/201807/t20180709_7732143.shtml

The catch is where they stand. A second report on the same scheme notes that
by the time you reach them, your ID and your ticket have been checked. They're
for people waiting for a train. If you've just got off one, you walk out the
other side.

> Bags reach the lockers after security, with the passenger's ID and ticket
> already checked, Hangzhou Daily (杭州日报), 3 August 2018, tier 2.
> https://hznews.hangzhou.com.cn/chengshi/content/2018-08/03/content_7046403.htm

Neither report gives a Deqing price, and nothing dated since 2018 says the
lockers are still there.

Next door, joined by an 80 m covered walkway, is the coach centre (客运中心)
where the Y1 bus leaves. Its opening report lists everything from ticketing to
shops. Left luggage isn't on it.

> Coach centre joined to the station by an 80 m covered walkway, Zhejiang
> Online, Living in Hangzhou (浙江在线·住在杭州网), 22 August 2018, tier 2.
> https://zzhz.zjol.com.cn/hhpd/hzls/201808/t20180822_8073148.shtml

![A row of grey self service lockers along the wall of a station waiting hall, a wheeled suitcase standing by an open locker door](/images/guide/where-to-leave-your-bags-2.webp 'On the way home a locker can help, because the ticket that gets you through security also gets you to it.')

## The transfer centre: one free desk, three records

At Yucun (庾村) everything you carry comes off the Y1 or a taxi and onto the
coach up the mountain. Three records disagree about whether any of it can
stay behind.

At its launch in December 2018 the county tourism account listed the
centre's services: ticketing, enquiries, waiting, dining, meeting rooms,
shopping.

> Transfer centre services at launch, with no luggage store among them,
> Deqing Culture and Tourism (德清文旅), 7 December 2018, tier 4, cited as what
> was announced. https://m.163.com/dy/article/E2EU8TUV0518SHA7.html

A Chinese trip report from August 2019 says the service desk would keep bags
free of charge. Its writer's advice: leave your phone number with the staff,
and take theirs.

> Free luggage storage at the service desk during an August 2019 visit,
> Youxiake community (游侠客社区), trip report by 喵喵酱旅行, tier 3, undated
> page, dated by its own account. https://bbs.youxiake.com/y/1022265.html

Our own desk research in early August 2026 found no left luggage service
there. The [getting around page](/getting-here/getting-around/) prints both
readings.

> No left luggage service at the transfer centres, BeyondBorder Group Ltd
> desk research, 1 to 5 August 2026.

The desk may still take bags and simply not advertise it. Or it stopped some
time in the last seven years. Nobody here has rung the scenic area, no county
notice we can reach settles it, and the [shuttle
page](/plan/moganshan-shuttle-system/) prints the same split. Ask at the
service desk (服务台) before the coach leaves: show staff the words 行李寄存, left
luggage, and have a fallback if the answer is no.

![A plain service counter in a large concrete transfer hall, two travellers with a big suitcase waiting in front of it, seen from behind](/images/guide/where-to-leave-your-bags-3.webp 'If the desk will keep a bag, ask what time it closes before you board. You collect it after the coach comes down, not before.')

## Leave the big case in Hangzhou

On a round trip that starts and ends in Hangzhou, that's where the only
priced, dated option we found sits. By October 2024 Hangzhou Metro had self
service lockers at 39 stations, including the one at Hangzhou East.

> Hangzhou Metro lockers at 39 stations, first 30 minutes free, then RMB 2,
> 4 and 5 an hour for small, medium and large, capped at RMB 12, 24 and 30
> per 24 hours, Zhejiang Online (浙江在线), 1 October 2024, tier 2.
> https://zjnews.zjol.com.cn/yc/qmt/202410/t20241001_30560309.shtml

The report carries two warnings. The six large lockers at that station
were full by about 09:00 on the first day of the National Day holiday. It
sets no limit on how long a bag may stay, so ask before leaving one
overnight. The [Hangzhou page](/getting-here/from-hangzhou/) covers the
train on to Deqing.

![A metro station concourse with a bank of luggage lockers along one wall, travellers with suitcases walking past, seen from behind](/images/guide/where-to-leave-your-bags-4.webp 'At the daily cap, two nights in a large locker comes to RMB 60, if the station will keep a bag that long.')

## What to do with your bags instead

Pack for carrying. Every change on the way up is made with the bag in your
hand, and the last stretch to a guesthouse can be steps.

Before you arrive, tell your guesthouse how many bags you have and how big
they are, and ask whether they will hold them on the day you check out. No
published policy turned up for any property, so get the answer in writing.

Then work backwards. A bag held below the mountain is collected after the
last coach down, which Tide News read off the Houwu board as 17:00.

> Last bus down 17:00 at the Houwu transfer point, Tide News (潮新闻),
> 4 December 2025, tier 2. https://tidenews.com.cn/news.html?id=3315585

Heavy cases on a first trip to China? The
[Deqing station page](/getting-here/deqing-station/) suggests a first
night in Wukang. More practical questions are on the
[plan FAQ](/plan/faq/) and across [Plan your trip](/plan/).
