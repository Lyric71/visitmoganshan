# Paying for things on Moganshan: Alipay, WeChat Pay, and whether cash still works

Visitors arrive believing two things about paying in China. Both are wrong.
The first is that cash is finished. It isn't. The central bank checked
about 94,000 businesses and reported in July 2024 that 99.3 percent took
cash.

> Cash taken at 99.3 percent of about 94,000 businesses, People's Bank of
> China (中国人民银行) figures, China National Radio (央广网), 27 July 2024,
> tier 2.
> https://china.cnr.cn/gdgg/20240727/t20240727_526818534.shtml

The second is that an Alipay (支付宝) foreign card either works in China or it
doesn't. Mostly it does. The catch is the price, which changes at
RMB 200. On Moganshan the gate ticket and the shuttle sit under that
line. The room usually sits above it.

Moganshan (莫干山) is a mountain in Deqing County (德清县), Huzhou (湖州),
Zhejiang (浙江), about 60 km north of Hangzhou and 200 to 240 km from
Shanghai. Moganshan Road in Shanghai, the M50 art district, is a different
place. Setting up the apps before you fly is in
[money and payments](/plan/money-and-payments/). This page is about spending
once you are here.

Foreign entries to China reached 22.914 million in the first half of 2026,
up 20.4 percent. In August the People's Daily overseas edition listed
Moganshan among their summer escapes.

> Foreign entries to China, first half of 2026, National Immigration
> Administration (国家移民管理局) figures in People's Daily Overseas Edition
> (人民日报海外版), via Sina Finance, 11 August 2026, tier 2.
> https://finance.sina.cn/2026-08-11/detail-inimwyqt7674173.d.html

| What | Price in the dated record | Year of the figure | Foreign card fee in the app |
|---|---|---|---|
| Gate ticket at the window | RMB 80 | 2023 | None |
| Gate ticket bought the day before | RMB 70 | 2023 | None |
| Transfer shuttle up and back | RMB 35 a person | 2023 | None |
| A room, worked example only | RMB 600 | None, illustrative | RMB 18 |

> Gate ticket and transfer shuttle prices, Huzhou Bendibao (湖州本地宝),
> 21 September 2023, tier 3.
> http://huzhou.bendibao.com/tour/2021123/3560.shtm

The ticket price itself is disputed, and
[tickets and entry](/plan/tickets-and-entry/) prints the disagreement. Every
figure in it sits under RMB 200. The shuttle is explained in
[the shuttle system](/plan/moganshan-shuttle-system/).

The only dated report on the lockers at Deqing station, from 2018, says they
took WeChat or Alipay. It gives no price.

> Deqing station lockers paid by WeChat or Alipay scan, Zhejiang Online
> (浙江在线), 9 July 2018, tier 2.
> https://zjnews.zjol.com.cn/zjnews/201807/t20180709_7732143.shtml

## The RMB 200 rule on an Alipay foreign card in China

Tenpay (财付通), which runs WeChat Pay (微信支付), set the rule out in a notice
dated 15 January 2026. A payment of RMB 200 or less on a card issued outside
China carries no foreign card fee. Above RMB 200 the fee is 3 percent of the
whole payment, not of the part above the line. A refund returns the fee in
proportion.

> Foreign card fee rule, Tenpay (财付通支付科技有限公司), 15 January 2026,
> tier 1.
> https://posts.tenpay.com/posts/9e70c66564910e6002958e997eabb18b.html

Alipay launched its foreign card service in 2023 on the same terms: free to
RMB 200, 3 percent above. No newer Alipay notice we could reach changes
that. Its cap was RMB 3,000 a payment. WeChat Pay's was
RMB 6,000, with RMB 50,000 a month and RMB 60,000 a year.

> Foreign card terms and caps at launch, Yicai
> (第一财经), 20 July 2023, tier 2.
> https://www.yicai.com/news/101814100.html

So RMB 199 costs RMB 199. At RMB 201 you pay RMB 207.03.
A room at RMB 600 (real rates are in
[where to stay](/where-to-stay/)) carries RMB 18 on top; two nights at RMB 1,000 each, paid
in one go, carry RMB 60. Your own bank may add a charge on top; check with it before you go.

The launch caps have since moved. In 2024 the foreign exchange regulator
said the caps for foreign visitors had been raised fivefold, per payment and
per year. It gave them in US currency, and no source we could reach gives
today's figure in yuan. Check the limits screen before you pay for three
nights at once.

> Caps raised fivefold, State Administration of Foreign Exchange
> (国家外汇管理局) at a central bank briefing, Red Star News (红星新闻) on
> Tencent News, 26 July 2024, tier 2.
> https://news.qq.com/rain/a/20240726A0AHJI00

![A hand holding a phone towards a small printed payment code on a wooden counter, a cup of tea and receipt slips beside it](/images/guide/paying-for-things-on-moganshan-2.webp 'Tenpay says any fee shows on the payment page, to two decimal places, before you confirm.')

## Binding the card, and why it sometimes fails

Binding doesn't always work first time. Alipay staff told Xinhua in 2024
that some cards fail because each foreign bank runs its own risk checks, and
the platform connects them one at a time. The app also asks for passport
details, which put some users off.

> Card binding failures and passport details, Xinhua (新华网), 28 February
> 2024, tier 2.
> https://app.xinhuanet.com/news/article.html?articleId=0ff4c6c0-0783-49c3-bc41-440b38e1ed44

At launch both apps took Visa, Mastercard, Diners Club and Discover, and
WeChat Pay took JCB as well. Bind the card at home, then make one small
payment. Binding only proves the app knows the card; a small payment is the
real test. If it fails at home, there's time to try another card.

Some visitors need neither app. People from nine places (Singapore,
Malaysia, Thailand, Korea and Mongolia among them) can scan with the wallet
they use at home. Since December 2024 eight UnionPay (银联) linked wallets from
abroad can also scan WeChat Pay merchant codes.

> Wallets from nine places, China National Radio (央广网), 27 July 2024,
> tier 2.
> https://china.cnr.cn/gdgg/20240727/t20240727_526818534.shtml

> UnionPay linked wallets and WeChat Pay codes, Xinhua (新华网),
> 17 December 2024, tier 2.
> https://app.xinhuanet.com/news/article.html?articleId=747534e384421407cf826f93932975f2

## Whether cash still works

Yes, by rule and in practice. In March 2024 the State Council made cash the
fallback. Businesses in tourism, lodging and catering were told to promise
in public to take cash and to keep change, and regulators were told to keep
punishing refusals of banknotes and to publish the penalties.

> Cash as the fallback, General Office of the State Council (国务院办公厅),
> 国办发〔2024〕10号, 7 March 2024, tier 1.
> https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/content/202403/content_6937623.htm

The central bank's mystery shopping shows the practice.

| Central bank check, reported July 2024 | Result |
|---|---|
| Businesses taking cash, about 94,000 checked nationwide | 99.3 percent |
| Businesses taking cash, 3,000 or so checked in 17 cities | 99.4 percent |
| Of those, holding enough change | 91.9 percent |
| Notes under RMB 20 issued, first half of 2024 | Up 30 percent on a year earlier |

Plan around the change figure. About one business in twelve was short of
change when checked.

> Cash and change, People's Bank of China figures, China
> National Radio (央广网), 27 July 2024, tier 2.
> https://china.cnr.cn/gdgg/20240727/t20240727_526818534.shtml

No dated source we could reach names a cash machine on the mountain. Draw
cash before you come up, and break it into small notes on the way.

![A basket of orange persimmons on a weighing scale at a roadside table, a few small banknotes and coins in a tin beside it](/images/guide/paying-for-things-on-moganshan-3.webp 'Farm stalls and picking bases price fruit by the kilo, so the total is rarely a round number. Carry coins as well as notes.')

## Cards in a terminal: what the 2024 rules require here

Cards are the harder question. In April 2024 the central bank and the Ministry of Culture and Tourism
(文化和旅游部) ordered full acceptance of foreign bank cards at every 5A and 4A
scenic area, every national and provincial tourist resort and every hotel of
three stars or more. The same notice told those places to keep a staffed
ticket window and to take cash.

> Foreign card acceptance at scenic areas and resorts, People's Bank of
> China and Ministry of Culture and Tourism, 银发〔2024〕76号, 1 April 2024,
> tier 1.
> https://policy.mofcom.gov.cn/claw/clawContent.shtml?id=99793

Moganshan falls inside that rule twice. The ministry named Deqing's
Moganshan International Tourism Resort (莫干山国际旅游度假区) a national
tourist resort in December 2020. The Yucun (庾村) scenic area at the foot of
the mountain is rated 4A. (The rating is Yucun's, not the whole
mountain's.)

> National tourist resort list, Ministry of Culture and Tourism
> (文化和旅游部), 15 December 2020, tier 1.
> https://zwgk.mct.gov.cn/zfxxgkml/zykf/202012/t20201228_920220.html

> Yucun scenic area rated 4A, Zhejiang Online (浙江在线), 9 June 2023, tier 2.
> https://zjnews.zjol.com.cn/zjnews/202306/t20230609_25838009.shtml

No Deqing source we could find says how far the resort has got. On paper, the ticket window should take both your card and
your cash. A village guesthouse is neither a scenic area nor a star rated
hotel, so the notice doesn't reach its desk. Don't count on a card there.

![A small staffed ticket hatch in a stone wall with a card terminal on the ledge, wet paving and a folded umbrella in the foreground](/images/guide/paying-for-things-on-moganshan-4.webp 'If the app fails at the gate, go to the staffed window: the 2024 rule keeps it open for cash.')

## At the guesthouse: what to ask before you pay

Xinhua counted nearly 900 licensed guesthouses, or minsu (民宿), around
Moganshan on 29 September 2026. The card rule above names none of them, and
each decides how it wants to be paid.

> Nearly 900 licensed guesthouses around Moganshan, Xinhua (新华网),
> 29 September 2026, tier 2.
> http://www.zj.xinhuanet.com/20260929/2bb0c09f3dc547deb5beb818d6b25693/c.html

Ask in the message that books the room. Ask how they want to be paid: app,
card or cash. Ask whether the stay is paid in one go, because over RMB 200
the 3 percent applies. Then there's the passport: find out whether the house
reads it by machine. Huzhou police said in
March 2025 that about 120 hotels and guesthouses across Huzhou had a reader
called e境通 (e Jingtong) that takes a check in from six or seven minutes to
under one.

> Passport readers at about 120 hotels and guesthouses in Huzhou, Zhejiang
> Daily (浙江日报) on Hangzhou Net (杭州网), 25 March 2025, tier 2.
> https://news.hangzhou.com.cn/zjnews/content/2025-03/25/content_8959193.htm

If your bank won't let the card bind, or you'd rather not give an app your
passport, cash still covers the whole trip. It takes planning: enough for
the room, plus small notes for the rest.
[What a minsu is](/where-to-stay/minsu-explained/) explains why the answers
differ from one house to the next.
