# A weekend in Moganshan from Shanghai

Two nights on a mountain 200 to 240 km from Shanghai. Moganshan is in Deqing County, Huzhou prefecture, Zhejiang Province, about 60 km north of Hangzhou. The Moganshan in Putuo, Shanghai, is a road and an art district, and it is the one the search results keep offering you.

It works as a weekend getaway from Shanghai, and the reason is the train. Two nights in Moganshan, Friday evening out and Sunday afternoon back, is the trip most people take.

It is also the most expensive and most crowded way to do it.

## The shape of a Moganshan weekend

You leave Hongqiao on a Friday evening train, reach Deqing inside 80 minutes, and take a taxi up the hill in the dark. Saturday is your full day. Sunday morning is yours, then you come back after lunch.

| You start at | Door to door, one way | What that means for Friday |
|---|---|---|
| Central Shanghai | 2h45 to 3h30 | An evening train gets you in for a late check-in |
| Shanghai Pudong | 4h00 to 4h45 | Do not try it Friday. Sleep in Shanghai, travel Saturday morning |

Both are estimates, and both include getting to Hongqiao, the train and the transfer. Double them for the round trip: 5 hours 30 minutes to 7 hours of moving, spread over three days instead of one. That spread is the argument for two nights. One night works, but it rushes both travel days. [Shanghai to Moganshan](/getting-here/from-shanghai/) has the full breakdown.

## Friday: the two clocks that decide your evening

The train is the easy part. Thirteen direct services a day run from Hongqiao to Deqing in 63 to 80 minutes, second class fares roughly RMB 61 to 110. Pick your Friday departure off the [live Hongqiao to Deqing timetable](/go/trains-shanghai-deqing/) once you know the answer to the next two paragraphs, because the hard part comes after you get off.

![A crowded evening station concourse with motion blurred commuters](/images/guide/itineraries-weekend-from-shanghai-2.webp 'The Friday train is the easy clock. The transfer at the other end is the one that decides your evening.')

Clock one is the Y1 tourist shuttle from the Deqing Coach Center. Seven departures a day, and the last one leaves at 16:00.

Clock two is the transfer centers. No private vehicle goes inside the scenic area, so everyone changes at Yucun, Fatou or Houwu, and those three run 08:00 to 18:00.

Your Friday comes down to which of the two you can still catch.

| Leave Hongqiao | Reach Deqing | What is still running |
|---|---|---|
| By about 14:30 | About 15:45 | The 16:00 Y1 shuttle, RMB 10 |
| By about 16:00 | About 17:15 | Taxi only, about RMB 100, but you clear the 18:00 transfer window |
| 17:39 (G1419) | 18:42 | Taxi only. Transfer centers closed |

The itinerary below uses the third row, because that is the train people leaving an office catch. It works, so long as your bed is not inside the scenic area, and very few are. A taxi reaches the door in 30 minutes for RMB 100.

> Train times, shuttle departures and transfer-center hours, BeyondBorder Group Ltd primary research, 1 to 5 August 2026.

A second Friday warning, and it is about food. Chinese domestic reporting records no evening economy in these villages: no night market, little late-night food. Arrive at 19:30 and the village is shut. Send the guesthouse your train number and ask them to hold dinner.

## The weekend, hour by hour

The times assume the G1419 out and a Sunday afternoon return. Treat it as a shape, because your Saturday will drift.

| When | What you are doing |
|---|---|
| 17:00 | Hongqiao. Security queues build on Friday evenings |
| 17:39 | G1419 leaves for Deqing |
| 18:42 | Arrive Deqing. Taxi to the village, 30 minutes, RMB 100 |
| 19:30 | Check in. Dinner at the guesthouse, arranged in advance |

### Saturday

| When | What you are doing |
|---|---|
| 07:30 | A bamboo path near the village, before the day buses |
| 09:00 | Breakfast |
| 09:45 | Your nearest transfer center, most likely Yucun. They open at 08:00 |
| 10:15 | The villa streets and Sword Pond |
| 12:30 | Lunch in Yucun, at the square or the 1932 culture park |
| 14:00 | Luhuadang Park, or the Bai and Yun Art Museum |

| 19:00 | Dinner at your guesthouse |

### Sunday

| When | What you are doing |
|---|---|
| 08:00 | Breakfast, check out, leave your bags |
| 09:00 | Whatever you missed: a second bamboo path, or the villas at walking pace |
| 12:30 | Lunch, then collect the bags |
| 13:30 | Taxi or Y1 shuttle to Deqing. Y1 returns run 08:40 to 17:00 |
| 15:00 | Train to Hongqiao |
| 16:30 | Home |

The Saturday 17:00 line is not padding. The transfer centers stop at 18:00 wherever you are standing. Check the Sunday evening timetable out of Deqing on a booking app before you go, and hold a seat on it.

## What to do with Saturday

Tables can hold the timing. They cannot tell you what the day is for. Two things here can, and the first is the bamboo. Forest cover is over 92 percent, mostly moso, and it is a working crop rather than landscaping. The paths are best early, and that alone is the reason to sleep up here. The walks are on the [bamboo forest](/moganshan/bamboo-forest/) page.

![Layered bamboo covered ridges in clear morning light with a road on one flank](/images/guide/itineraries-weekend-from-shanghai-3.webp 'Saturday is the only full day a weekend gives you. It is worth deciding in advance what it holds.')

Second, the architecture. About 250 historic villas survive in local mountain stone, built from the 1890s by American and British missionary families and later by Chinese owners. Our [villa walking tour](/moganshan/hill-station/walking-tour/) suits a slow morning.

Sword Pond, or Jianchi, is the sight most visitors list. In legend the mountain is named for the swordsmiths Mo Ye and Gan Jiang, and the pond is where their blades were quenched. This page does not dress that up as history.

None of these sights keeps published opening hours in English, and the walking times between them depend on where the transfer puts you down. Your guesthouse is the fastest check on both.

Worth knowing before you buy a ticket. The villages and guesthouses sit outside the ticketed core, so a weekend here does not have to include one. Sword Pond and the villa streets are inside it.

## What a weekend costs

Two people sharing a room, in RMB.

| Item | Cheap end, off-season | Dear end, peak |
|---|---|---|
| Rail, Hongqiao to Deqing, round trip, two people | 244 | 440 |
| Taxi up Friday, Y1 shuttle down Sunday | 120 | 120 |
| Two nights, mid-range room | 600 | 2,000 and up |
| **Total, before tickets and food** | **about 964** | **about 2,560 and up** |

Those totals are our arithmetic on the published bands. Nobody sells this as a package. And note what the columns really compare, because the bands split by season and never by day of the week. The right-hand column is a peak-season room, and calling it a measured Saturday premium would be a stretch.

Ticket prices do not agree across sources. One gives RMB 120 online and RMB 130 at the gate. Another gives RMB 50 off-season and RMB 80 in peak. Winter is half price. Check at booking rather than trusting any single number, ours included. Prices checked 5 August 2026.

> Both published scenic-area ticket ranges, BeyondBorder Group Ltd primary research, 1 to 5 August 2026.

## The weekend problem

None of those numbers are accidents.

Saturday is the worst night of the week to sleep in Moganshan, and the whole county knows it. The weekday and weekend split is severe: weekends and holidays drive the volume, and Mondays are notably quiet. Sector occupancy was about 55 percent in 2024, so beds sit empty midweek while your Saturday costs triple.

Holidays are worse again. High-end occupancy hit 94 percent over the May 2024 holiday, and footfall in Yucun rose 30 to 40 percent above normal during National Day 2025.

> Guesthouse occupancy, room-rate bands and holiday footfall, BeyondBorder Group Ltd primary research, 1 to 5 August 2026.

## The midweek version of the same trip

Same trains, same shuttle. Two nights, shifted.

![An empty village lane with closed shutters and wet stone paving](/images/guide/itineraries-weekend-from-shanghai-4.webp 'The same mountain on a Tuesday. Cheaper, quieter, and a genuinely different place.')

| Two nights, starting | Friday to Sunday | Saturday to Monday | Sunday to Tuesday |
|---|---|---|---|
| Peak-rate nights | Two, including Saturday | One | Neither is a Saturday |
| Your full day | Saturday, the busiest | Sunday | Monday, the quietest |
| Days off needed | None | One | Two |
| Trains and shuttle | 13 services, 7 departures | Same | Same |
| Who it suits | People with jobs | Anyone with one flexible day | Remote workers, anyone mid-trip |

The Saturday-to-Monday version is the one most people overlook. One day off, one fewer peak night, and the busiest day on the mountain traded for the quietest. If you can move one thing about this trip, move that. Sunday to Tuesday beats both of them.

## Where to base yourself

On a two-night trip the village matters more than the guesthouse.

| Village | What it gives you over two nights |
|---|---|
| Yucun | The hub at the foot of the mountain. Square, market, main gateway, shortest transfers |
| Xiantan | The densest guesthouse village, on the north side: about 135 minsu among under 2,000 residents |
| Houwu | Quieter, about 30 minutes out. On a short trip that is time spent twice |

A minsu is a small, owner-run guesthouse. Staying near Yucun saves transfer time on both days. On a short trip that beats quiet. See [where to stay](/where-to-stay/) and [getting around Moganshan](/getting-here/getting-around/).

## Which weekend, and which season

| Season | What you get | For a weekend |
|---|---|---|
| July to August | Mean 24.1 °C, 6 to 7 °C cooler than Shanghai and Hangzhou | The historic season and still the busiest |
| Spring and clear late autumn | Bamboo shoots, green meadows, then clear air | The promoted shoulder seasons, which is another way of saying fewer people |
| Winter | Cold. January mean 1.4 °C | Ticket is half price |
| Golden Weeks | May Day, National Day | Skip them |

The place exists because of that summer gap. More in [seasons](/seasons/).
