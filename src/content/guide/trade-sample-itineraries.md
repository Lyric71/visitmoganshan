# Operator-ready itinerary modules

These are blocks, not tours. Each drops into a Yangtze Delta program your team already sells. All of it was checked on 5 August 2026.

Moganshan is a mountain in Deqing County, Huzhou prefecture, Zhejiang Province, 60 km north of Hangzhou and roughly 200 to 240 km from Shanghai. It sits between the two cities most of your clients already have on file, so you add nights to a corridor your coach drives.

Our consumer pages tell a traveler what to look at. This one gives you nights, legs, cut-offs and client-documentation lines.

## How to read these

Every time here is marked **measured** or **estimate**. Measured means a published timetable or posted operating hour. Estimate means our arithmetic on those, buffered where stated.

The modules exclude guiding, meals, tickets and the vehicle, which you still price. We publish no rates, room blocks, commission terms or lead times. Nobody has quoted us any, and inventing them would be worse than a blank.

No property here is named as a recommendation. [Trade and media](/trade/) sets out why.

That first row barely surfaces in English-language sources, which is how it surprises people at the boundary instead of appearing in your file.

The Y1 leaves Deqing Coach Center at 07:30, 08:40, 09:40, 10:40, 13:00, 15:00 and 16:00, returning between 08:40 and 17:00. Nothing moves between 10:40 and 13:00, exactly when a mid-morning train from Hongqiao puts your client on the platform. It also stops in Moganshan town, so anyone going into the scenic area faces another change.

A taxi or van covers the same ground in about 30 minutes for about RMB 100, both measured. Party size decides which you book.

| Party | Last-mile call | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| FIT couple on a rail package | Y1 at RMB 10 a head, if arrival misses the midday hole | Measured |
| Group | Private vehicle. Seven departures will not carry a party | Measured timetable, our call |
| Either | The vehicle still stops at a transfer center | Measured |

### Back-timing the arrival day

Arrival day is where these programs fail, and 18:00 is why. Build it backwards.

| Step | Clock | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| Transfer center closes | 18:00 | Measured |
| Client standing at the center by | 17:30 | Estimate, our 30-minute buffer |
| Off the train at Deqing by | 16:50 | Estimate, buffered, slow 40-minute road leg |
| Depart Shanghai Hongqiao by | 15:30 | Estimate, buffered, slow 80-minute rail leg |
| Depart Hangzhou East by | 16:30 | Estimate, buffered, slow 17-minute rail leg |
| Long-haul arrival at Pudong | Not viable same day | Estimate, add 50 to 70 min by car to Hongqiao |

Program order changes your product. Hongqiao to Deqing runs 63 to 80 minutes, with 13 direct services a day. From Hangzhou East it is 13 to 17 minutes, and there are 16 of them. Both measured. Running Hangzhou first buys the arrival day an extra hour, and on a tight day that hour is an entry rather than a lobby.

We would not sell that Pudong row. [Getting here](/getting-here/) has the leg detail; [getting around](/getting-here/getting-around/) covers on-mountain transport.

Those are the constraints, and the modules fit inside them.

## Choosing between the two modules

Deqing station sits between your two city segments, so the mountain belongs second. Slot it third and the client crosses the same ground twice. Either way it replaces a city night rather than extending the trip, and clients take that better when you say so early.

| | Module A | Module B |
|---|---|---|
| Nights | 2 | 3 |
| Full days on the mountain | 1 | 2 |
| Costed movements | 6 | 6, plus the free day |
| Sells to | General inbound | Walkers, August families |

Availability is rarely the constraint. Occupancy in the area sat near 55 percent in 2024, over roughly 1,000 guesthouses in September 2025, so midweek space is real. Golden Weeks invert it.

## Module A: two nights, three days

The standard block, and the one we quote first.

![A small group with day packs walking up a village lane towards guesthouses](/images/guide/trade-sample-itineraries-3.webp 'The two night module is the one that works commercially. Arrival day is written off on purpose.')

| Day | Movement | Timing | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| D1 | Rail to Deqing, road leg to the village, check in | 63 to 80 min, then 30 to 40 min | Measured |
| D1 | Scenic-area entry only if you clear a center before 18:00 | Arrival day only | Estimate |
| D2 | Village to transfer center, into the area, back out | Inside 08:00 to 18:00 | Measured |
| D3 | Road leg to Deqing, rail on to your next city | 30 to 40 min, then the onward leg | Measured |

Six costed movements: the two rail legs, the two road legs, and a vehicle change at each end of the scenic-area day. That is your transfer line and it does not shrink. Departure day has its own ceiling: the last Y1 to Deqing goes at 17:00.

The client gets one full scenic-area day and two mornings. Mornings sell best, landing before the day visitors.

Put the dinner warning in writing. Chinese domestic reporting keeps making the same point: no night market, little sold to eat after dark. Book both dinners at the property ahead.

## Module B: three nights, four days

Still six costed movements. What changes is the day you gain in the middle.

| The added day | What it holds | Why it works |
|---|---|---|
| No ticketed entry needed | A second village, or walking | Keeps the day off the 08:00 to 18:00 clock |
| Summer positioning | July and August average 24.1 °C, 6 to 7 °C under both cities | Measured. Why the hill station exists |
| Heritage depth | About 250 stone villas from the 1890s on | Enough for a guided morning |
| The cost | A third quiet evening | No evening economy, in any season |

> Summer mean temperature, the differential and surviving villa count, BeyondBorder Group Ltd primary research, 1 to 5 August 2026.

Sell the third night to walkers and August families, not a first-timer on five days. [Why Moganshan](/trade/why-moganshan/) argues the case; [seasons](/seasons/) has the month by month.

## Why the one-night module does not hold

Which leaves the version most catalogs carry. Operators reach for one night because it prices cleanly. The clock defeats it.

![An empty hotel corridor with doors along one side and dusk in the far window](/images/guide/trade-sample-itineraries-4.webp 'One night means arriving after the light and leaving before the morning. The client sees a corridor.')

One night gives the client an arrival afternoon and a departure morning. The afternoon only exists if the train lands early enough to clear a center, which from Hongqiao means leaving by about 15:30. The morning ends when the road leg back begins. Between them, no full day at all.

The transfer count does not move either: two rail legs, two road legs. A single night halves what the client gets and saves nothing on movements, the opposite of what a costing sheet suggests.

If the program cannot hold two nights, quote the day trip with its real cost: 5 hours 30 minutes to 7 hours of travel, on our arithmetic, for one afternoon. Otherwise leave the mountain out.

## Lines for your client documentation

- The station is **Deqing**. The one named Moganshan serves Wukang town, not the mountain.
- Private vehicles do not enter the scenic area. Flag limited-mobility clients at booking. Not confirmed: what accessible transport the centers provide.
- Ticket prices do not agree. One set runs RMB 120 online, RMB 130 at the gate. The other runs RMB 50 off-season, RMB 80 peak, half in winter. Do not fix a per-head line without checking.
- Avoid both Golden Weeks. The May 2024 holiday pushed high-end occupancy to 94 percent, and National Day 2025 lifted Yucun footfall 30 to 40 percent above normal.
- Not confirmed: what closes at 18:00. Last entry, last transfer up and gate close are three deadlines, each moving your pickup.

> Occupancy, guesthouse count, ticket price ranges and holiday footfall, BeyondBorder Group Ltd primary research, 1 to 5 August 2026.
