# Where to stay in Moganshan

About 1,000 places to sleep sit around the foot of one hill in Deqing County, Huzhou prefecture, Zhejiang Province. The hill is [Moganshan](/moganshan/), about 60 km north of Hangzhou and 200 to 240 km from Shanghai. It is not Moganshan Road, the Shanghai art district that takes over the search results whenever you type the name.

Almost none of those 1,000 are hotels. Photographs will not sort them, because they all look alike and the map means nothing to you yet. What sorts them is the village you sleep in, then the price band, then the kind of property you are booking.

First, the disclosure. This site holds no featured placements and no sponsored listings, and covers every property on the same terms. Nobody has bought a position here and nobody has been offered one.

## Which village suits you

The villages sit around the foot of the mountain, not on top of it, and the distances between them are real. Houwu is about 30 minutes from Yucun, a long way to go for dinner.

| Village | Chinese | What it is | Book it if |
|---|---|---|---|
| Yucun | 庾村 | Main hub at the foot of the mountain. Square, market, restaurants, the "1932" culture park, main scenic-area gateway. | First visit, no car, one night |
| Xiantan | 仙潭村 | Northern foot. Densest guesthouse village, with about 135 minsu among under 2,000 residents, roughly 90 percent locally run. | You want choice and price competition |
| Laoling | 劳岭村 | South side. Reservoir, kayaking, cycling. One of the earliest farmstay clusters. | You want the water and bikes |
| Sanjiuwu | 三九坞 | Where naked Home Village opened in 2007, the first yangjiale here. | You are shopping the foreign-founded end |
| Houwu | 后坞 | Quiet, west side, about 30 minutes from Yucun. Also one of the three transfer centers. | You want quiet and have transport |
| Dazaowu | 大造坞 | The most remote. Rice paddies and the Dadouwu reservoir. | You want the most remote option |
| Wukang | 武康 | Deqing county seat, about 20 km away. The rail and services town, not the mountain. | You arrive late, or want a normal hotel |

> Village profiles and guesthouse counts, BeyondBorder Group Ltd primary research, 1 to 5 August 2026.

Yucun is the safe first choice, because the main gateway and most of the restaurants are there. It is also the busiest. Over National Day 2025, daily footfall ran 30 to 40 percent above normal.

Xiantan is where it gets interesting. About 135 guesthouses compete inside a village of under 2,000 residents, and that density produces the price range you see online. It is mostly guesthouses rather than a village with guesthouses in it.

The others are quieter, and quiet costs something. Without a car, a village 30 minutes from Yucun is one you will not leave after 6 pm, which makes your guesthouse your restaurant.

### The rule that decides your arrival day

This matters more than the train timetable does.

| The rule | What it means for you |
|---|---|
| Private vehicles cannot drive inside the scenic area | You change vehicles on the way in. Your own car gets you between villages, not past that line |
| Three transfer centers: Yucun, Fatou and Houwu | The village you booked decides which you use |
| Open 08:00 to 18:00, all three | Reach Deqing at seven in the evening and your first night works around closed centers |

## What each price band buys

Village settled, the next question is money, and here the mountain behaves oddly. Rates move with the calendar more than with the property. The same room can cost RMB 400 on a March Tuesday and over RMB 1,000 on an August Saturday.

| Band | Rate a night | What you are getting |
|---|---|---|
| Mid-range, off season | RMB 300 to 500 | A converted village house, small and usually clean. The normal Moganshan stay. |
| Mid-range, peak weekend or holiday | RMB 1,000+ | The same room at a holiday price. You pay for the date. |
| High end | RMB 1,500+ | The threshold the local trade uses. Design-led builds on larger sites, fuller service. |
| Resort brands | about USD 291 to 524 | Where naked Stables and naked Castle sit. |

> Rate bands, BeyondBorder Group Ltd primary research, 1 to 5 August 2026.

Those bands were last checked on 5 August 2026 and move faster than anything else here. Rooms above RMB 1,500 grew 42 percent year on year, so owners think the money is at the top.

That does not make the RMB 400 room bad. Off season and midweek it is the best value on the mountain. It does mean the middle attracts less investment than the top, so read the property, not the band.

The calendar sits on the [seasons page](/seasons/). July and August are the core season, the May and October Golden Weeks spike hard, and Mondays are quiet.

That calendar also decides how early to book. For a Golden Week, book early: high-end occupancy hit 94 percent over the May 2024 holiday. Midweek there is usually room, since sector occupancy ran about 55 percent in 2024. Typical lead times are not in our research, and a guessed number is worse than none.

## The four kinds of property here

Moganshan accommodation comes in four kinds, and price does not tell you which you are walking into.

| Type | What you are booking |
|---|---|
| Minsu (民宿) | A small guesthouse, usually run by the family that owns it, and the default here. About 10,000 beds across roughly 1,000 properties, around 80 percent individually owned. A household with rooms, not a company with a building. |
| Yangjiale (洋家乐) | Roughly "foreigner's farmstay". Guesthouses opened here by foreign owners, starting with naked Home Village at Sanjiuwu in 2007. Investors now come from 18 countries. Still a minsu, with better odds of English. |
| Resort property | A few larger builds on the mountain, the oldest dating to 2011. Hotel service and hotel rates. |
| Off-mountain hotel | A conventional hotel down in Wukang town, off the mountain. Fine for a late arrival, poor if you came for the bamboo. |

[What a minsu is](/where-to-stay/minsu-explained/) covers what a guesthouse stay involves, including whether foreign guests are accepted.

## The Four Seasons, announced for 2030

On 12 September 2025, Four Seasons announced a Moganshan resort: 90 keys on a 23-hectare site in Yu Village, opening in 2030. The developer is Zhejiang Shangyin Cultural Tourism Development Co., a subsidiary of the Deqing County Cultural and Tourism Development Group. Its press release calls the project "Moganshan's first international luxury resort with direct access to natural hot springs".

## The market picture nobody puts in a listing

This last part is about the business rather than your trip, and it decides what you walk into. One guesthouse in 2002, about a thousand by September 2025. That climb left a mixed inheritance.

| Measure | Figure |
|---|---|
| Properties, 2002 to September 2025 | 1 to about 1,000 |
| Occupancy, before 2021 | about 70 percent |
| Occupancy, 2024 | about 55 percent, some 15 points down |
| High-end occupancy, 2025 | 68 percent |
| Share individually owned | about 80 percent |
| Bed cap set in late 2017 | about 10,000, since drifted past |
| Payback period | stretched from 3 to 5 years out to 5 to 8 |
| Labor, as a share of revenue | 30 to 40 percent |
| Consolidation, March 2025 | Trip.com took 67.04 percent of Dalezhiye: 28 properties, 556 rooms, average rate about RMB 1,300 |

> Occupancy and ownership figures, BeyondBorder Group Ltd primary research, 1 to 5 August 2026.

Occupancy fell while beds kept being added, so owners are chasing a smaller share of a bigger market. The effect on your booking is direct. An owner under that pressure either reinvests or quietly stops spending, and no booking page tells you which. Nothing holds the middle of this market to a standard, so read recent reviews and study photographs of the room, not the terrace.

## Dinner is the decision people forget

Chinese reporting on the sector puts it plainly. These villages have no evening economy: no night market, very little late food.

Yucun has a square, a market and restaurants. The smaller villages mostly do not. Drop your bags in Houwu at seven, go looking for dinner, and you may find none.

Book half board for most stays. Half board means the room rate includes breakfast and dinner. Use whichever booking channel you like, then message the property before you pay. Ask whether they cook dinner and book it for your arrival night, ask whether they can register a foreign passport, and say how you are arriving. Meal pricing is set property by property with no published standard, so get the number in writing.

Set up Alipay or WeChat Pay before you land. Foreign cards have been linkable to both since 2023, and a village guesthouse may not take one. [Money and payments](/plan/money-and-payments/) walks through the setup.
