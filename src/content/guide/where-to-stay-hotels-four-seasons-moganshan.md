# Four Seasons Resort Moganshan

If you are searching for Four Seasons Moganshan, you are about four years early. There is no hotel. What exists is an announcement, made on 12 September 2025, giving an opening year of 2030. Moganshan is a mountain in Deqing County, Huzhou prefecture, Zhejiang Province, with Hangzhou 60 km away and Shanghai 200 to 240 km. Today it holds no Four Seasons room and no date more exact than a year.

If a Four Seasons China search sent you here, Moganshan is a project, not a property. So book something else: a few resort properties at that level are already open on this mountain, named lower down.

## What was announced, and what was not

| Item | The announcement |
|---|---|
| Brand | Four Seasons Hotels and Resorts |
| Date announced | 12 September 2025 |
| Rooms | 90 keys |
| Site | 23 hectares, given as Yu Village |
| Opening | 2030 |
| Developer | Zhejiang Shangyin Cultural Tourism Development Co. |
| Developer's parent | Deqing County Cultural and Tourism Development Group |

> Moganshan resort announcement of 12 September 2025, BeyondBorder Group Ltd primary research, 1 to 5 August 2026.

That is the whole of it. The rest is missing because it was never announced:

- room types, rates and the restaurant or spa plan
- the architect, a construction start or a month within 2030

A page describing the bathrooms of a building nobody has poured concrete for would be fiction.

Ninety keys is small. The other names show how small:

| Property | Keys |
|---|---|
| Le Passage Mohkan Shan | about 28 |
| **Four Seasons, announced for 2030** | **90** |
| naked Castle | about 95 |
| naked Stables | about 121 |
| Crowne Plaza Deqing Moganshan, in Wukang town | about 340 |

> Key counts, BeyondBorder Group Ltd primary research, 1 to 5 August 2026.

Only Le Passage is smaller, and the Crowne Plaza is not on the mountain. The site itself is about one percent of the core scenic area, which covers about 20 km². Beyond size, the announcement made one claim about the place.

## The claim in the announcement, and the qualifier people drop

The announcement describes the project as "Moganshan's first international luxury resort with direct access to natural hot springs".

Read the end of that sentence. It does not claim to be Moganshan's first international luxury resort. Foreign founders have run resort properties here since 2007, and guesthouse investors come from 18 countries, so that claim would be argued with here. What the wording claims is the hot springs.

That one sentence is the whole of the public evidence that hot water exists on this mountain. No source is named for it, no temperature is given, and nothing is open to visitors today. Do not book a trip for the water. The [hot springs page](/moganshan/hot-springs/) goes through it.

## Yu Village, and which village that actually is

Then there is the address. The site is given as Yu Village. The main village at the foot of the mountain is Yucun (庾村), often written the same way in English, and the announcement does not say whether it means that one.

![A broad green valley with terraced fields and scattered buildings below forested ridges](/images/guide/where-to-stay-hotels-four-seasons-moganshan-2.webp 'The site sits in a valley on the mountain’s flank, not up among the old stone villas.')

There is a second trap in that name: Anji County has its own Yucun (余村), different characters and county, and the two get mixed up constantly. Treat all three names as separate. The [villages guide](/moganshan/villages/) covers each one.

## Why the announcement matters more than the building will

Names aside, take the brand off and look at who signed. A global luxury operator picked this mountain. The other side of the deal is not a private developer: Zhejiang Shangyin Cultural Tourism Development Co. sits under the Deqing County Cultural and Tourism Development Group, the county government's own development group. The county put its own company behind a Four Seasons.

![A plain unmarked construction hoarding around an empty rural site below bamboo hills](/images/guide/where-to-stay-hotels-four-seasons-moganshan-3.webp 'Nothing has been built. What the announcement did was tell the rest of the market this mountain is worth 2030 money.')

Nothing else says as much about where this mountain is heading. The Ministry of Culture and Tourism named the area a national-level tourism resort on 15 December 2020, and private money had already been moving the same way.

| What the market shows | Figure |
|---|---|
| Guesthouse rooms priced above RMB 1,500 | up 42 percent year on year |
| High-end occupancy, 2025 | 68 percent |
| Sector occupancy, 2024 | about 55 percent, down from about 70 percent before 2021 |

> Occupancy and rate figures, BeyondBorder Group Ltd primary research, 1 to 5 August 2026.

The top of this market is fuller than the middle, and that is where the money has been going.

A signal is not a guarantee. Announced hotels slip, and some are never built. Five years is a long time. Our research shows no construction start and no record of permits or money committed. Until that changes, 2030 is an intention with a serious sponsor behind it, not a date to plan around.

None of which helps you book a room. It tells you what kind of mountain you will come back to.

The local trade puts the high-end threshold at RMB 1,500 a night. naked Stables and naked Castle band roughly USD 291 to 524. Checked 5 August 2026.

Booking one is mostly a calendar problem, plus one rule about arrival time.

| The decision | What it turns on |
|---|---|
| Golden Weeks, the holidays around 1 May and 1 October | They fill. High-end occupancy hit 94 percent over the May 2024 holiday |
| Midweek, Mondays above all | The quiet end of the calendar |
| Half board, meaning a rate that includes breakfast and dinner | These villages have no evening economy, so your property is your restaurant |
| Getting in before 18:00 | Private cars cannot enter the scenic area, and the transfer centers at Yucun, Fatou and Houwu work 08:00 to 18:00 |
