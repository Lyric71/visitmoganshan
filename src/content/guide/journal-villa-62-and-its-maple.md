# Villa 62 and its maple: how old is the tree?

The maple in the garden of Villa 62 is said to be more than 500 years old. A
travel writer reported that in 2021, and the guesthouse tells its guests the
same now. In 2025 Tide News, Zhejiang's provincial news service, called it a
hundred year tree. Unless they mean different trees, both can't be right, and
nobody has published a measurement that settles it.

Moganshan (莫干山) is a mountain and former hill station in Deqing County (德清县),
Huzhou (湖州), Zhejiang, about 60 km north of Hangzhou and 200 to 240 km from
Shanghai. Its old stone summer houses, the Moganshan villas, go by numbers more
often than names. None of this is Moganshan Road in Shanghai, the M50 art
district.

Both 500 year accounts come from the garden itself: a writer who sat in it, and
the people who run it. The scenic area's oldest maple is over 500, but it has
never said where it stands. The one news report to describe the Villa 62 tree in
its own words calls it a hundred, and gives the grander title to a tree at
No. 274. Even that tree was billed, in the same report, as "a century of maple
charm" (百年枫韵). Not five.

The guesthouse speaks of two trees, the writer and Tide News of one. The record
doesn't say which one the photographs show.

## What 500 years would mean on paper

In Zhejiang a tree isn't ancient because someone says so. The province's rules
on ancient and famous trees (古树名木), in force since 1 October 2017, count a tree
as ancient (古树) only once it has been assessed at 100 years or more, and then
grade it.

| Grade | Age | Confirmed by | Protected zone beyond the crown |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 500 years or more | the province, after the county assesses it | at least 5 m |
| 2 | 300 to 499 years | the city, after the county assesses it | at least 3 m |
| 3 | 100 to 299 years | the county | at least 2 m |

> Ancient tree grades and protected zones, Zhejiang Provincial People's
> Government (浙江省人民政府), decree 356, 7 July 2017, on the central government
> portal (中国政府网), tier 1.
> https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/2021-12/27/content_5719221.htm

So "more than 500" is not a loose compliment. It is the line for the top grade.
A tree above it should have a provincial confirmation and a published catalogue
entry, with a protection sign beside it.

We found no published catalogue entry for any Moganshan maple, though the
Zhejiang forestry bureau's page on the scenic area would not load when we tried.
Either way, the 500 years has not been shown to readers in a form they can
check.

A measured age still has a margin. In January 2024 Liu Lili (刘丽莉), a deputy head
of ecology at the National Forestry and Grassland Administration (国家林业和草原局),
listed the methods in use, from asking people and reading documents to ring
counts, cores and carbon 14. All of them leave error, she said. The target for a
grade 1 tree was an error under 25 percent.

> Tree age methods, their error, and targets of under 25, 20 and 15 percent for
> grades 1 to 3, National Forestry and Grassland Administration (国家林业和草原局), from
> Beijing News (新京报), 5 March 2024, tier 1.
> https://www.forestry.gov.cn/c/www/zhzs/548184.jhtml

A tree put at 500 and measured to that target could be anything from about 375
to 625. "The owner says" is on that list too. It counts as an interview.

![A thick, moss streaked maple trunk rising beside a low stone garden wall, its lower branches cut back long ago, red leaves overhead and a wet path below](/images/guide/villa-62-and-its-maple-2.webp 'The 2017 rules also order a county census of old trees and a file for each one, recording where it stands, its age and its condition.')

## Villa 62 among the Moganshan villas

The house is barely easier to date than the tree. The guesthouse says it was
built in the 1930s; we found no dated source we could check that gives a year.
That would put it in the second wave of building, the Chinese boom of 1928 to
1949 that [the stone villas](/moganshan/hill-station/the-villas/) page sets out.

It is on a national list. In 2006 the State Council named the Moganshan villa
group (莫干山别墅群) among 1,080 new national key protected sites, and China News
Service, captioning the Villa 62 maples in 2024, counted the house part of it.

> The Moganshan villa group, No. 949 of 1,080 in the sixth batch, State Council
> (国务院), notice of 25 May 2006, on the Ministry of Culture and Tourism site
> (文化和旅游部), tier 1.
> https://zwgk.mct.gov.cn/zfxxgkml/qt/202012/t20201206_918486.html

> Villa 62 captioned as part of the protected villa group, China News Service
> (中国新闻网), 13 November 2024, tier 2.
> https://www.chinanews.com.cn/tp/2024/11-13/10318612.shtml

Set the two ages against the house. A tree that was a hundred in 2025 dates from
about the 1920s, close to when the villa went up, and could have been planted
with the garden. A tree of 500 stood for four centuries before the hill station
existed. Either is possible, and neither is on record.

The house trades now as Maple and Azalea Valley (枫鹃谷), the name the scenic area
also gives the viewing point. By its own account it serves afternoon tea in the
garden.

![A stone villa veranda with peeling green shutters and a few mismatched wooden chairs, wet maple leaves stuck to the flagstones, a mop bucket by the door](/images/guide/villa-62-and-its-maple-3.webp 'The guesthouse says its two buildings take more than 30 guests at a time, and it serves local dishes as well as the tea.')

## The little Kyoto shot

The 2021 writer, standing under the tree, wrote that this garden was probably
what people meant when they called autumn Moganshan a "little Kyoto" (小京都). For
2025 the scenic area named 62 Fengjuan Valley among its chosen viewing points,
and ran a maple shuttle line past it. Tide News found visitors in Republican era
dress, and wedding couples, photographing among the old houses.

> The viewing point, the shuttle line and the period dress, Tide News (潮新闻), via
> China News Service (中新网), 18 November 2025, tier 2.
> https://www.chinanews.com.cn/sh/2025/11-18/9636841.shtml

The nickname oversells it. Most of the mountain is bamboo and stays green, and
the red comes in pockets. Close up, one pocket fills a phone screen, and that is
the shot.

Timing matters more than the tree's age. The maples turn late, and no 2026
festival date had been announced by 19 October 2026. [Moganshan red
leaves](/seasons/moganshan-red-leaves/) has every date on record, and the other
old trees.

![Visitors seen from behind on a narrow wet lane under red maples, a woman in a long dark skirt holding up a phone, a shuttle bus half out of frame and a bin by the verge](/images/guide/villa-62-and-its-maple-4.webp 'In 2025 a provincial promotion post called the colour at its best by 28 November. A week later Tide News put the best in December.')

## Seeing the tree

Villa 62 is not in Wuling village (武陵村), where Songyue Lodge stands. It is on
the Sword Pond (剑池) side of the mountain: the guesthouse places itself by the
Huating car park, five minutes above the pond. The 2025 maple walk that reaches
it, route 4 from Yinshan Street (荫山街), passes Huating on its way to Nos. 62
and 92. Nobody from this site has walked it. In 2025 the last bus down left Yinshan
Street at about 17:00.

> Route 4 via Huating to Nos. 62 and 92, and about 17:00 for the last bus down,
> a Zhejiang tourism promotion account (诗画浙江游浙里) on Tencent News (腾讯新闻), 28
> November 2025, tier 4, as announced.
> https://view.inews.qq.com/a/20251128A06OQU00

[The shuttle system](/plan/moganshan-shuttle-system/) has the timetable, and
[the hill station walking tour](/moganshan/hill-station/walking-tour/) the lanes
around it.

The garden belongs to a working guesthouse, and nothing published says whether
passers by may go in. Look from the lane unless the gate is open, or book a
room. Then look for a protection sign by either trunk. One would mean the tree
has been through the assessment the rules describe. None has been reported.
