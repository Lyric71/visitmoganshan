# Xiantan: the guesthouse village, and whether to stay there rather than Yucun

Across this site we have said that Xiantan holds about 135 guesthouses in a
village of under 2,000 people. That figure is seven years old. It comes from a
Shanghai newspaper's visit in spring 2019, and the three dated counts since
are all higher.

The other claim to test is an American writer's. In 2023 David Fishman skipped
Yucun (庾村), "the lively town seat", for "sleepy Xiantan Village". With a car
he still has a point. Without one, his case mostly falls apart.

Moganshan (莫干山) is a bamboo mountain and former hill station in Deqing County
(德清县), Huzhou (湖州), Zhejiang, about 60 km north of Hangzhou and 200 to 240 km
from Shanghai. It is not Moganshan Road in Shanghai, the M50 art district in
Putuo. Xiantan (仙潭村) is one of the villages around its foot.

## Xiantan village, Moganshan: what it is and where

Xiantan lies on the northern foot of the mountain, with Tianquanshan (天泉山)
behind it. The village covered 11.8 km² in 2019. Until the guesthouses came it
lived on bamboo shoots and cane. Fishman put the mountain itself around 10 km
away.

> Northern foot, 11.8 km², under 2,000 people, 135 guesthouses, Shanghai
> Observer (上观新闻), 3 April 2019, tier 2.
> https://www.shobserver.cn/wx/detail.do?id=142561

> Quiet over Yu Village, about 2,000 people, about 10 km off, David Fishman,
> Crossing the River by Feeling the Stones,
> 5 July 2023, tier 3.
> https://www.feelingthestones.com/p/weekend-getaways-perfected-escaping

Da Xiantan (大仙潭) is the name Xiantan shares with its neighbours Nanlu (南路村)
and Sihe (四合村), so the 2024 figure may count more than one village. Read it as
above 150 and rising slowly.

Who opened first depends on what you count. Fishman's host said he took paying
guests in 2003. The 2019 report dates the first guesthouse "in the real sense"
to 2013.

## What costs nothing in Xiantan

Less than it used to.

The village costs nothing. The Paper reported a footpath laid along the stream
in 2021, so that guests could walk after dinner. People's Daily lists a
thousand year yew (红豆杉) among the things the village wants to make more of.
The Xiantan art museum (仙潭美术馆) opened in 2023 on three floors and 850 m², and
over National Day 2024 its resident artist was showing more than 30 of his own
works. We found no admission price for it in any source.

> The art museum's 850 m² over three floors, Xinmin Evening News (新民晚报),
> 8 October 2024, tier 2.
> https://view.inews.qq.com/a/20241008A046CM00

The old village school is now a reading room, 甲野书房·文杏里 (Wenxingli). Since the
village put up a sign calling itself Moganshan's screenwriters' village
(莫干山编剧村), more than 30 screenwriters have moved in. Neither source prints a
price for the reading room.

> The bookroom and the screenwriters, China News Service
> (中国新闻网), 3 March 2026, tier 2.
> https://www.chinanews.com.cn/sh/2026/03-03/10580234.shtml

What is no longer free is the walk Fishman took. Biwu Dragon Pool (碧坞龙潭), a
chain of waterfalls and pools up the slope behind the old Biwu end of the
village, had "no ticket gate, and no entrance fee" when he walked it in June
2023. Since the second half of that year a company under the county supply
cooperative has spent more than RMB 8 million on it, and it sells tickets
through Douyin, Kuaishou, Meituan and Ctrip. By the operator's own figure it
drew about 85,000 visitors in 2024.

> Biwu Dragon Pool's investment, tickets and 2024 visitors, Deqing News (德清新闻网), 13 March 2025, tier 2.
> http://dqnews.zjol.com.cn/dqnews/system/2025/03/13/034904107.shtml

No dated source we can cite prints the price; the platforms show it.

## Eating in Xiantan

No source we found names a dish of Xiantan's own. You'll mostly eat at your
guesthouse. Of the 129 Xiantan listings we hold, 98 list a restaurant among
their facilities.

> Facilities on 129 captured Trip.com listings, BeyondBorder Group Ltd
> primary research, captured 8 August 2026, compiled 4 October 2026.

That is how it started. Before anyone offered a bed, the 2019 report says,
villagers set out round tables at home and cooked for passing visitors.

The village has filled in since. The Paper counted ten restaurants, five farms
and two cafés in 2021. People's Daily counted ten restaurants, seven cafés and
three tent camps in 2023. By March 2026 China News Service found a café, Beiye
(北野), serving breakfast to the first visitors of the day.

> Ten restaurants and seven cafés, People's Daily (人民日报), 12 June 2023,
> tier 2.
> https://www.crnews.net/zt/srxxzjqwgcjy/zqbwjsyy/955610_20230612122458.html

That's still not a high street: ten restaurants across 11.8 km². Book half
board, meaning breakfast and dinner, or ask your host what is open that
evening.

## The walk from Xiantan

This is a desk route: nobody on this desk has walked it. In 2019 the county's
spring programme printed a 15 km walk that starts at Houwu (后坞) or Xiantan and
climbs over Tianquanshan. It crosses the high ground by Guaishijiao (怪石角),
Dakeng (大坑) and Zhonghuashan (中华山), then drops through Luhuadang park (芦花荡公园)
and Sword Pond (剑池) on the Chiang Kai shek road (蒋公道) to Yucun square.

> The 15 km route to Yucun square, Zhejiang Online (浙江在线), 5 April 2019,
> tier 2.
> https://huzhou.zjol.com.cn/qy/dqx18223/201904/t20190405_9837182.shtml

A full day; from Dakeng to Sword Pond it is inside the scenic area. [Hiking on
Moganshan](/things-to-do/hiking/) sets out the ridge.

Getting back is the hard part. In 2025 a weekend shuttle, the Kailas (凯乐石号),
ran from Biwu Dragon Pool through Yucun to Wusi (五四村), 08:00 to 17:40 on
Saturdays, Sundays and holidays from May to October, at RMB 5. No source we
can reach confirms it ran in 2026.

> The Kailas shuttle's route, hours and fare in 2025, Deqing News (德清新闻网),
> 24 June 2025, tier 2.
> https://dqnews.zjol.com.cn/dqnews/system/2025/06/24/034991600.shtml

![A walker seen from behind on a stone path climbing through dense bamboo, a thin waterfall to one side, the frame tilted and the top of the slope lost in mist](/images/guide/xiantan-4.webp 'The county published the route as an azalea walk for its April 2019 flower festival.')

## Who should base in Xiantan

Fishman's reason was quiet.

On that, the village agrees with him. In 2019 Shen Jiangrong (沈蒋荣), who opened
Mofan and later led the village, argued against a glass walkway. If 30 coaches
came, he asked, what would be left of the quiet, which was "our biggest
selling point"?

> The glass walkway argument, Shanghai Observer (上观新闻), 3 April 2019, tier 2.
> https://www.shobserver.cn/wx/detail.do?id=142561

So base here if you have a car and two nights or more, outside a holiday. You
get the widest choice of guesthouses on the mountain, and the quiet.

Without a car, the case reverses. Xiantan has no transfer centre; the three
are at Yucun, Fatou (筏头) and Houwu, and every trip into the scenic area starts
at one. Sleep in Yucun and the coach is a short walk away; Xiantan means
driving.

> The three transfer centres, Deqing News (德清新闻网),
> 30 September 2019, tier 2.
> https://dqnews.zjol.com.cn/dqnews/system/2019/09/30/031939856.shtml

[Yucun on its own](/moganshan/villages/yucun/) makes the case for the town,
and [the shuttle system](/plan/moganshan-shuttle-system/) has the coach times.

## The honest downside

Many of those guesthouses are alike. Mofan's 26 year old manager told Xinhua
in 2025 that his family's guesthouse built the first pool here "and now it's
standard". Xinhua found the houses alike in architecture, decoration and
activities.

And the market has turned. Xinhua put Moganshan's average guesthouse occupancy
at about 70 percent before 2021 and about 55 percent in 2024, without naming
its source, and reported owners cutting prices to keep up. The habit is not
new: in 2019 some owners were already selling RMB 1,000 rooms at RMB 500.
Midweek bookers gain. One owner told Xinhua the thinner margins leave less for
upkeep.

> The pool, the occupancy figures and price cutting, Xinhua Daily Telegraph
> (新华每日电讯), 19 March 2025, tier 2.
> http://www.news.cn/local/20250319/6f3d6119568c45df9f68e7585d6a97c8/c.html

> Rooms discounted from RMB 1,000 to 500, Shanghai Observer (上观新闻), 3 April
> 2019, tier 2.
> https://www.shobserver.cn/wx/detail.do?id=142561

It isn't empty, either. People's Daily gave 150,000 visitors a year. That is
the village's own figure, not a statistics bureau count.

> About 150,000 visitors a year, People's Daily (人民日报), 12 June 2023, tier 2.
> https://www.crnews.net/zt/srxxzjqwgcjy/zqbwjsyy/955610_20230612122458.html

And the odds of English at the door are lower than at the design led end of
the market. In 2019, 90 percent of the guesthouses were run by local families.
Message before you pay; you'll know inside a day.

> 90 percent of guesthouses run by locals, Shanghai Observer (上观新闻), 3 April
> 2019, tier 2.
> https://www.shobserver.cn/wx/detail.do?id=142561

alternatives.
