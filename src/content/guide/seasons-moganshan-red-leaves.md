# Moganshan red leaves: where the maples are and when they turn

Moganshan is a bamboo mountain, and bamboo does not turn. It is easy to
conclude, as our own autumn guide once did, that colour is not what this
season is for. That misses about ten thousand trees. The scenic area counts
more than 10,000 red maples, and for three years running it has held a
festival for them. The catch is timing. The maples turn in the second half of
November and hold into December, well after the October holiday. Plan for the
last week of November.

Moganshan (莫干山) is a mountain and former hill station in Deqing County (德清县),
Huzhou (湖州), Zhejiang, about 60 km north of Hangzhou and 200 to 240 km from
Shanghai. It is not Moganshan Road in Shanghai, the M50 art district in Putuo
(普陀), which owns most of the search results.

## Why the date moves

China Weather (中国天气网), the national weather portal, gives the thresholds. Most
red leaf species start to turn once the night minimum falls below 10 °C, and
turn fully between 0 and 5 °C. A day to night swing of more than 10 °C helps.
A wet growing season pushes the turn later.

> Turning starts below a 10 °C minimum and is complete at 0 to 5 °C, China
> Weather (中国天气网), on CCTV News (央视网), 23 October 2025, tier 2.
> https://news.cctv.com/2025/10/23/ARTIofIm3U44p18MbloKnzE6251023.shtml

In November, then, watch the overnight low rather than the calendar. When
China Weather's town forecast for Moganshan (in Chinese, though the numbers
need no translation) shows nights under 10 °C, the turn has started. When the
lows reach 0 to 5 °C, the trees are close. We found no published November
average for the mountain.

The Moganshan reports say 红枫, red maple, and never name a species. China
Weather files sweetgum and Japanese maple, which turn weeks apart, under the
same 红叶, red leaves. Expect the colour over weeks, not on one day.

![Red and orange maple leaves on a wet stone stair beside a low stone wall, bamboo stems behind and a mist hanging in the valley](/images/guide/moganshan-red-leaves-2.webp 'One cold north wind can scatter the leaves, China Weather warns. Go soon after the colour is reported, not a week later.')

## The 500 year tree: which one?

A 2021 trip account on Tencent puts a maple "more than 500 years old" in the
garden of Villa 62, with tea tables underneath. The record does not settle it.

> A maple over 500 years old in Villa 62, a named travel writer on Tencent
> News (腾讯新闻), 5 December 2021, tier 3.
> https://news.qq.com/rain/a/20211205A08VZZ00

The scenic area's own figure, given in 2024 and repeated in 2025, is more than
10,000 red maples, more than 100 of them over a century old, and the oldest
over 500. Which tree, it doesn't say.

> More than 10,000 red maples, more than 100 over a century old, the oldest
> over 500 years, Moganshan Management Bureau (莫干山管理局), quoted by
> Deqing News (德清新闻网), 13 November 2024, tier 2.
> https://dqnews.zjol.com.cn/dqnews/system/2024/11/13/034801734.shtml

In 2025 Tide News called the Villa 62 tree a "hundred year" maple and gave the
title "king of maples" (枫树王) to a tree in the yard of No. 274.

> A hundred year maple in front of Villa 62 and the "king of maples" in the
> yard of No. 274, Tide News (潮新闻), relayed by China News Service

## Walking the maple routes

Seeing the old trees means walking. For 2025 the scenic area announced four
walks, each a loop from Yinshan Street (荫山街), where the shuttle from the
transfer centre stops. Nobody here has walked them. Only route 3 came with
walking times: about 40 minutes between stops, plus the walk back.

## Moganshan red leaves are not the only colour

During the 2024 festival Deqing News reported golden larch (金钱松), ginkgo and
plane trees at their best alongside the maples. The 2025 route list starts at
a century old ginkgo over the wall of No. 95, five minutes from Yinshan
Street.

> Golden larch, ginkgo and plane trees reaching their best during the 2024
> festival, Deqing News (德清新闻网), 13 November 2024, tier 2.
> https://dqnews.zjol.com.cn/dqnews/system/2024/11/13/034801734.shtml

Now the honest part. The 2021 writer called autumn Moganshan a "little Kyoto"
(小京都). It isn't. Most of the mountain stays green, and the red comes in
pockets. Two of the 2024 festival photographs were taken from a drone, which
is how a pocket of red fills a frame. Anyone hoping for a whole hillside on
fire has picked the wrong mountain. What's on offer is ten thousand maples
scattered among stone villas, with bamboo behind them.

![Walkers seen from behind on a narrow road under yellow ginkgo and red maples, bamboo slopes rising on one side and a parked van at the verge](/images/guide/moganshan-red-leaves-4.webp 'Shuttles and walkers share these narrow roads in November. Keep to the verge on the bends.')

## Tickets, cars and rooms in late November

The scenic area charges for entry. We don't print a price here because no tier
1 source we can reach has published one; [Moganshan tickets in
2026](/plan/moganshan-tickets-in-2026/) sets out every published figure with
its date.

Driving up is where a red leaf trip can go wrong. On 1 December 2025 a visitor
heading for the maples was stopped five or six kilometres below the scenic
area and sent back. From 08:00 to 17:30 every car changes to the shuttle at a
transfer centre, unless its driver is booked into a property inside and has
reserved parking.

> Shuttle only from 08:00 to 17:30 without a booking inside and a parking
> reservation, Tide News (潮新闻), 4 December 2025, tier 2.
> https://tidenews.com.cn/news.html?id=3315585

The rule and its exceptions are set out under [the private car
ban](/plan/private-car-ban-on-moganshan/).

For rooms, the calendar matters more than the trees. Moganshan is a weekend
trip from Shanghai and Hangzhou, so a late November Saturday is busy whether
the maples have turned or not, and midweek is quieter. Nobody has published a
visitor count for the festival weeks that we can find. [Autumn on
Moganshan](/seasons/autumn/) sets November against the rest of the season.

Rooms near the villas and in the villages below are listed [on
Trip.com](/go/stays-all/), and [where to stay](/where-to-stay/) sets the
villages side by side.
