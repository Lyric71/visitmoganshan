# Is Moganshan worth visiting?

Yes, if you want two slow days in cool forest with a good room, a long dinner and an early night. No, if you want sights, restaurants or anything happening after 21:00. Both halves of that answer are real.

Moganshan is a mountain in Deqing County, part of Huzhou prefecture, in Zhejiang Province. It sits about 60 km north of Hangzhou and 200 to 240 km from Shanghai. One warning: Moganshan Road is an art district in Shanghai, about 200 km away. This page is about the mountain.

## The answer in one table

| Worth it if you want | Not worth it if you want |
|---|---|
| Cool air in a hot Chinese summer | Big-name sights and monuments |
| Bamboo forest and walking | A food scene, or dinner after 20:00 |
| A design-led guesthouse and a quiet room | Bars, music, anything at night |
| Old stone villas and hill-station history | A short, simple trip with no transfers |
| A break from Shanghai or Hangzhou crowds | A single day squeezed out of a Shanghai itinerary |

## What Moganshan genuinely does well

Four things hold up.

![A quiet bamboo forest interior with no path and no people](/images/guide/plan-is-moganshan-worth-visiting-2.webp 'What it does better than almost anywhere within reach of Shanghai: nothing, at scale, for two days.')

**The temperature.** The mountain runs 6 to 7 °C cooler than Shanghai and Hangzhou in summer, with a July and August mean of 24.1 °C. This is why the place exists. American Protestant missionaries came here in the 1890s to escape Shanghai's heat, and the proposition has not changed.

**The forest.** Cover is over 92 percent, mostly moso bamboo. It is a working commercial crop, not landscaping, so you walk through farmed hillside rather than a manicured park. The [bamboo forest](/moganshan/bamboo-forest/) page covers where to walk and when.

**The buildings.** About 250 historic villas survive, built from local mountain stone in British, American, French, German and Chinese-eclectic styles. Chiang Kai-shek honeymooned at Songyue Lodge in December 1927, and Mao Zedong stayed in a 1934 villa in 1954. The [hill station history](/moganshan/hill-station/) page tells that story properly.

**The access from Hangzhou.** Trains from Hangzhou East reach Deqing in 13 to 17 minutes, with 16 services a day. From Hangzhou, the barrier to trying it is very low.

> Hayley Keon, "A Peaceful Place: Raising Imperial Children at China's Moganshan Hill Station," Journal of Imperial and Commonwealth History, published online 9 January 2026.

## Who will like it

People who are tired. That is the honest core of it. The mountain suits travelers halfway through a China trip, worn down by cities and queues, better than it suits anyone arriving fresh and hungry for things to see.

Couples do well, especially in shoulder season. So do families, and the villages feel unhurried in a way a city park does not. Summer refugees from Shanghai are the original market and still the best served.

Design and architecture people get more from Moganshan than most visitors. The guesthouse scene has been a testing ground for small Chinese hospitality design for twenty years, since Grant Horsfield opened naked Home Village at Sanjiuwu in 2007. The local word for it, "yangjiale" (洋家乐), means foreigner's farmstay.

Walkers with two full days will be fine. Walkers with four hours will not.

## Who should not come

Read this before you book.

![A closed shopfront and single street lamp on a wet village street after dark](/images/guide/plan-is-moganshan-worth-visiting-3.webp 'Evenings end early. If that reads as a problem rather than the point, this is the wrong mountain.')

**Anyone who needs nightlife.** There is none. Not a small version, not a quiet version. None.

**Anyone who eats late.** Chinese reporting is blunt about the missing evening economy: no night market, very little late food. If your guesthouse dinner is not booked, you may be eating instant noodles at 21:00.

**Anyone traveling on a Golden Week.** High-end guesthouse occupancy reached 94 percent over the May 2024 holiday, and daily footfall in Yucun during National Day 2025 ran 30 to 40 percent above normal. Weekends are milder but still busy. Mondays are quiet, a gap severe enough to decide your trip.

> Occupancy and guesthouse data, BeyondBorder Group Ltd research, 1 to 5 August 2026.

**Anyone who hates uncertainty in hotels.** There are about 1,000 guesthouses as of September 2025, up from one property in 2002. Roughly 80 percent are individually owned, and quality varies a lot. [Where to stay](/where-to-stay/) is worth reading carefully rather than skimming.

**Anyone with one spare day in Shanghai.** Door to door from central Shanghai is 2 hours 45 minutes to 3 hours 30 minutes by rail, on our estimate. From Pudong it is 4 hours to 4 hours 45 minutes. Double either for the return. Our [day trip](/getting-here/day-trip/) page does the arithmetic.

**Anyone who wants to drive to the door.** Private vehicles cannot enter the scenic area. Everyone changes vehicles at one of three transfer centers: Yucun, Fatou or Houwu, open 08:00 to 18:00. Add the 30 to 40 minute road transfer from Deqing station and you have two changes before you reach a room.

## What Moganshan is not

It is not a national park. The core scenic area covers about 20 km² and the main peak, Tashan, is 719 to 720 meters. A wooded ridge with roads and villages through it, not dramatic mountain country.

It is not a sightseeing destination. Sword Pond, Luhuadang Park, Dakeng, the Xuguang sunrise platform, Chiang Kai-shek's villa and the Bai and Yun Art Museum are the named sights. You can see most of them in a day, and none is the reason to come.

It is not cheap in peak season. Mid-range rooms run RMB 300 to 500 off-season and RMB 1,000 and up at peak. Anything above RMB 1,500 counts as high-end locally, and that band grew 42 percent year on year.

It is not undiscovered. Moganshan Town recorded 2.60 million visitors in 2018 and 2.52 million in 2021. Tatler Asia calls it "the Hamptons of China", and The New York Times listed it in "45 Places to Go in 2012" at number 18, on a numbered rather than ranked list.

## How it compares with your other options

Most readers are choosing between Moganshan and more nights in the city they are in.

![Visitors on a covered walkway in a busy classical Chinese garden](/images/guide/plan-is-moganshan-worth-visiting-4.webp 'The honest comparison is not with a wilderness. It is with another day in Hangzhou.')

| | Moganshan | Stay in Hangzhou | Stay in Shanghai |
|---|---|---|---|
| Travel to get there | 1h20 to 1h45 from Hangzhou, 2h45 to 3h30 from Shanghai, our estimates | None, you are there | None, you are there |
| What you get | Forest, cool air, quiet, villas | Lake, temples, restaurants, museums | Everything, at city volume |
| Evenings | Effectively nothing after dinner | Full | Full |
| Summer heat | 6 to 7 °C cooler | Hot | Hot |
| Crowd risk | High on weekends and Golden Weeks | High year round | High year round |
| Minimum useful stay | Two nights from Shanghai, one from Hangzhou | One night | One night |

The water towns and bamboo counties nearby are a different trip, and this site covers Deqing County rather than ranking the region.

The trade-off is simple. Hangzhou gives you more to do. Moganshan gives you fewer people, cooler air and better sleep. In July and August that is an easy call. In February it is harder, though winter tickets are half price and January averages 1.4 °C.
