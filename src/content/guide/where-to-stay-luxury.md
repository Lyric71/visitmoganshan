# Luxury hotels in Moganshan

Luxury is a loose word here. Village guesthouses use it and so do booking platforms. The local trade at least means something specific by it, a price band. This page uses figures too. What the top of this market charges and how many rooms it holds, then what the same money buys 60 km away.

That 60 km leads to Hangzhou, the nearest big city to Moganshan. The mountain itself is in Deqing County, Huzhou prefecture, Zhejiang Province, with Shanghai 200 to 240 km off. Search the name and Shanghai's Moganshan Road usually answers first. That is an art district, 200 km from this hill.

Nobody from this site has stayed at any property below. No stars, no scores, no ranking, no pick.

## Where luxury starts: RMB 1,500

Ask in Deqing and you get a number instead of an adjective. Anything above RMB 1,500 a night is treated as the high end.

| What you are booking | A night |
|---|---|
| Village room, off season | RMB 300 to 500 |
| The same room, peak weekend or holiday | RMB 1,000+ |
| Where the local trade puts high end | RMB 1,500+ |
| naked Stables and naked Castle, as one range | about USD 291 to 524 |

> Moganshan rate ranges, BeyondBorder Group Ltd primary research, 1 to 5 August 2026.

Two warnings go with that table.

| The warning | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| RMB 1,500 is a trade convention, not an enforced standard | A property can price above the line and owe you nothing in particular for it |
| The dollar and yuan figures come from separate sources | Each stays in its own currency here rather than being converted at a rate that will be stale by the time you book |

Rooms priced above RMB 1,500 grew 42 percent in a year. Owners have decided the money sits at the top, and new supply follows it. What it buys, measured against somewhere you already know, is the next question.

## The 60 km that should reset your expectations

Amanfayun sits in Hangzhou, about 60 km from this mountain. Its rooms run roughly USD 987 to 1,196 a night. Carry that figure into your booking.

![A city hotel room high above dense towers with floor to ceiling glass](/images/guide/where-to-stay-luxury-2.webp 'Sixty kilometres away in Hangzhou the same money buys a different category of hotel entirely.')

| Property | Where | Rough price a night |
|---|---|---|
| naked Stables and naked Castle | Moganshan | USD 291 to 524 |
| Amanfayun | Hangzhou, about 60 km away | USD 987 to 1,196 |

> naked Stables and naked Castle price range, BeyondBorder Group Ltd primary research, 1 to 5 August 2026.

> Amanfayun price range, BeyondBorder Group Ltd desk check, August 2026.

Do the subtraction. The cheapest Amanfayun night costs almost double the dearest naked night. Moganshan's ceiling sits under Hangzhou's floor, and the two are about an hour and a half apart by road.

Arrive with Aman expectations and the price says no before you get here. The mountain is not the problem. The word is, and it cuts both ways. Travelers who want that level can have it in Hangzhou and still reach the bamboo on a day trip.

A note for anyone who searched luxury hotels near Shanghai. The city is 200 to 240 km off, 2 to 3 hours by car, and these tables are what waits at the end of that drive.

## The listing nobody here holds

No Moganshan property belongs to any of the international curated hotel collections, the membership groups that vet properties and market them abroad. Checked in August 2026.

Those collections are how many travelers shortlist at this price. Without one, a Moganshan hotel asks you to book on its own description, or on platform reviews you cannot audit.

## 2030, when the ceiling moves

On 12 September 2025, Four Seasons announced a Moganshan resort: 90 keys on a 23-hectare site at Yu Village, opening in 2030. The developer, Zhejiang Shangyin Cultural Tourism Development Co., sits inside the Deqing County Cultural and Tourism Development Group. That group belongs to the county government. The press release calls the project "Moganshan's first international luxury resort with direct access to natural hot springs".

> Moganshan resort announcement, Four Seasons Hotels and Resorts, 12 September 2025.

Ninety keys would put it third by size among the four, just under naked Castle. It would also be the first international luxury brand here, and it would move the ceiling: a Four Seasons does not price into a USD 291 to 524 range.

None of which helps you this year. There is no room and no published rate, and 2030 is an intention rather than a booking. [The Four Seasons page](/where-to-stay/hotels/four-seasons-moganshan/) has what was announced and what was left out.

## Booking at this end of the market

Three things decide a booking at this end. The brand on the gate is the least of them.

![An empty resort reception lounge with low seating and tall windows onto forest](/images/guide/where-to-stay-luxury-4.webp 'Book direct or through a platform that holds a real reservation. Rates here move a long way by date.')

| The choice | What actually decides it |
|---|---|
| When you go | The high-end segment ran 68 percent occupancy in 2025, and hit 94 percent over the May 2024 holiday. Golden Weeks sell out. Mondays do not |
| Dinner | Chinese reporting is consistent that these villages carry no night market and little late food. Book half board, meaning a rate with breakfast and dinner in it |
| Your arrival time | Private cars stop at the scenic-area boundary. The transfer centers at Yucun, Fatou and Houwu run 08:00 to 18:00, so an evening train into Deqing is a problem |

> Occupancy figures and transfer center hours, BeyondBorder Group Ltd primary research, 1 to 5 August 2026.

Ask one question before you pay: which side of that boundary the hotel stands on, and which transfer center serves it. Send it as a message, so the answer arrives in writing.
