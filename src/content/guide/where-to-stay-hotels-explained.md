# Every hotel in Moganshan

This list holds twelve hotel and resort names associated with Moganshan. One is off the mountain, one is announced for 2030, and naked Home Village has been part of naked Castle since 2017. They are not twelve separate hotels you can book today.

The mountain itself sits in Deqing County, Huzhou prefecture, Zhejiang Province, China, roughly 60 km north of Hangzhou, with Shanghai 200 to 240 km further off. None of that should be confused with Moganshan Road, the Shanghai art district known as M50, out in Putuo District some 200 km from here.

The names are complete. No ratings here, because nobody on this site has stayed in them.

## What this list is, and what it deliberately is not

We have not spent one night in one room of any property named on this page.

We do not rate them. No stars, no scores, no ranking, no "best of", no "our pick". Nothing here describes service, comfort, food or design. We have not experienced any of it. Each property's own site says more, in its own words. A ranked list of Moganshan hotels could be assembled in an afternoon from other people's marketing copy. It would carry nothing we could stand behind.

Per property we publish five things and nothing else: the name, where it is, the year it opened, the key count, and the price band. Most of these properties publish nothing in English, so where a figure has never been put out publicly the cell reads Not published rather than carrying a guess.

LEED is the only certification here, a third party building standard scored on construction and energy use. It says nothing about the bed.

## Two entries to read carefully

The Crowne Plaza Deqing Moganshan runs about 340 rooms from Wukang, the county seat of Deqing, 20 km off. The name says Moganshan and the booking sites file it with the mountain properties. Wukang is the rail and services town. No private vehicle may drive inside the scenic area, and its three transfer centres close at 18:00. That matters if your train gets in after six. [Getting here](/getting-here/) sets out the route.

The Four Seasons is the other. Its announcement came on 12 September 2025 and gives 90 keys on a 23 hectare site at Yu Village, with 2030 as the year. Behind the project is Zhejiang Shangyin Cultural Tourism Development Co., which belongs to the Deqing County Cultural and Tourism Development Group.

> Moganshan resort announcement, Four Seasons Hotels and Resorts, 12 September 2025.

The wording used is "Moganshan's first international luxury resort with direct access to natural hot springs". Nobody can book a Four Seasons room on this mountain today. Announced dates slip, and a 2030 opening is irrelevant to any trip you take this year.

## Twelve hotels, a thousand guesthouses

That ratio is what no booking site shows you.

![A dense village of small guesthouses packed tightly along a mountain contour](/images/guide/where-to-stay-hotels-3.webp 'For every hotel on the list there are roughly eighty guesthouses, and no list covers those.')

| Measure | Figure |
|---|---|
| Guesthouses, September 2025 | about 1,000 |
| Beds across them | roughly 10,000 |
| Government bed cap, set late 2017 | about 10,000. Stock drifted above it, and the overshoot is not published |
| Named hotels and resorts, this page | 12 |

> Guesthouse counts, bed capacity and the local bed cap, BeyondBorder Group Ltd primary research, 1 to 5 August 2026.

The cap came with tighter approvals in late 2017, and new supply still lands among the guesthouses. That is why this directory runs to twelve rows.

A search for "Moganshan hotels" returns hundreds of results, almost none of them hotels. They are minsu, small owner run guesthouses, about 80 percent individually owned. [What a minsu is](/where-to-stay/minsu-explained/) explains what booking one involves.

Rates here answer to the calendar more than to the property, which is why the table below gives ranges rather than single numbers.

## The guesthouses this page cannot cover

Those thousand guesthouses sit in villages ringing the mountain's base. No honest English directory of those exists yet, and no search engine will produce one. Building one takes stays and photography over months.

![A small family run guesthouse frontage with plants in tins and a bicycle against the wall](/images/guide/where-to-stay-hotels-4.webp 'Most of them have no English presence at all, and no booking site lists them.')

Until then, [where to stay](/where-to-stay/) starts with which village to sleep in, and the [villages guide](/moganshan/villages/) says what each has after dark.

Two narrower pages follow the same no rating rule. [Luxury stays](/where-to-stay/luxury/) handles the top end, [villas](/where-to-stay/villas-explained/) the historic stone houses, of which about 250 survive.

Everything filed as a hotel by the booking system, which is a much longer list than the twelve above and includes places we have written nothing about, is on the [hotels listing](/where-to-stay/hotels/) with live rates.

*Original property research was compiled in August 2026. The village locations and farmstay history above draw on the dated Chinese reports cited here. Rates move sharply between weekday and weekend, and the Four Seasons date is an announcement, not a booking.*
