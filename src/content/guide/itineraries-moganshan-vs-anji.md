# Moganshan vs Anji: one bamboo mountain, one bamboo county

Frommer's puts Moganshan and Anji on one destination page, as if they were two names for the same bamboo hills west of Shanghai. They are not the same kind of place. Moganshan is one mountain with a former hill station on top. Anji is a whole county, and its bamboo runs across most of it.

So Moganshan vs Anji comes down to how you travel. It decides which station you get off at and whether you need a car, and on the reported prices it moves the cost of a day by close to RMB 300 a person.

Moganshan (莫干山) is in Deqing County (德清县), Huzhou (湖州), Zhejiang, about 60 km north of Hangzhou and 200 to 240 km from Shanghai. Anji County (安吉县) is its neighbour to the west, in the same prefecture. Neither has anything to do with Moganshan Road in Shanghai, the M50 art district, which owns the search results for the name.

## Moganshan vs Anji at a glance

| | Moganshan | Anji |
|---|---|---|
| What it is | One mountain and its villages; a ticketed scenic area of about 20 km² | A county; its sights are scattered across the south of it |
| The bamboo | Forest covers over 92 percent of the mountain, mostly moso | About 1 million mu (亩) of bamboo, roughly 670 km² |
| What people go for | About 250 stone villas of the old hill station, walks, guesthouses | The Bamboo Sea (中国大竹海), the Cloud Grassland (云上草原) at 1,168 m |
| Nearest station | Deqing, 18 km by road from Yucun | Anji, 47 km by road from the Bamboo Sea |
| Gate price reported | RMB 80 (2023) | RMB 58 at the Bamboo Sea (2021), RMB 180 at the Cloud Grassland plus RMB 180 for its cableway (2026) |

> Moganshan's forest cover, villas and scenic area, BeyondBorder Group Ltd primary research, 1 to 5 August 2026; Anji's bamboo area, Xinhua (新华网), 15 August 2024, tier 2. https://www.news.cn/digital/20240815/6c532d66e1914ebaa1abfd9f48432e99/c.html; road distances measured on OpenStreetMap, BeyondBorder Group Ltd desk research, 18 October 2026.

## Why the two get mixed up

Start with the guidebook. Frommer's files both under a single destination, "Anji and Moganshan". Start there and you'd assume one base covers both.

> One destination page for both, Frommer's, as listed in an English web search, BeyondBorder Group Ltd desk research, 18 October 2026. https://www.frommers.com/destinations/anji-and-moganshan/

Then the film. Crouching Tiger, Hidden Dragon (《卧虎藏龙》) shot its bamboo scenes at Anji's Bamboo Sea, not on Moganshan. Anji calls itself the China bamboo county (中国竹乡), and the national press runs the two together: a 2013 People's Daily Digest piece was headlined "Anji: the Crouching Tiger bamboo county".

> The Bamboo Sea as the film's location, Anji as the bamboo county, and the headline, People's Daily Digest (人民文摘, of 人民日报), 1 August 2013, tier 2. http://paper.people.com.cn/rmwz/html/2013-08/01/content_1295217.htm

The names trip people as well. Moganshan's market village is Yucun, written 庾村. Anji has a Yucun too, written 余村, which the UN tourism body named one of its first Best Tourism Villages in December 2021. Different characters, different county. Show a driver the characters, not the English spelling.

> Anji's 余村 among the first Best Tourism Villages, Ministry of Culture and Tourism (文化和旅游部), 2 December 2021, tier 1. https://www.mct.gov.cn/whzx/whyw/202112/t20211202_929544.htm

Searching in Chinese is no better. Of the first nine results for a two day Moganshan and Anji guide, five were template pages on Sina and Weibo that call themselves tested (实测) and end in a FAQ. The two we opened each finish with a line saying the content was generated by AI.

> Search results and the AI notices on two pages, BeyondBorder Group Ltd desk research, 18 October 2026.

What all of that hides is size. Anji's bamboo runs to about a million mu, roughly 670 km², and 840,000 mu of its moso has been put under land use transfer contracts (流转). Moganshan's whole ticketed core is about 20 km². Even Anji's own figure moves: a 2025 report put the bamboo at 1.08 million mu.

> About 1 million mu, and 840,000 mu of moso under contract, Xinhua (新华网), 15 August 2024, tier 2. https://www.news.cn/digital/20240815/6c532d66e1914ebaa1abfd9f48432e99/c.html; 1.08 million mu, People's Daily Overseas Edition (人民日报海外版), 20 August 2025, tier 2. https://www.forestry.gov.cn/lyj/1/gglcy/20250820/638999.html

In practice you walk through Moganshan's bamboo, and drive through most of Anji's on the way from one ticket gate to the next.

Weather changes the picture more than the season does.')

## Getting to each

Both have a high speed station on lines from Shanghai and Hangzhou. They are not equally useful.

| | Moganshan | Anji |
|---|---|---|
| Station | Deqing (德清站) | Anji (安吉站), opened 28 June 2020 |
| From Shanghai Hongqiao | 13 direct trains a day, 63 to 80 min | 92 min on the first train, December 2024 |
| From Hangzhou | 13 to 17 min from Hangzhou East, 16 trains a day | 40 min on the opening day, 2020 |
| Station to the sights | 18 km to Yucun; Y1 shuttle RMB 10 or a taxi about RMB 100 | 24 km to the county town, 47 km to the Bamboo Sea |

> Deqing trains, shuttle and taxi, BeyondBorder Group Ltd primary research, 1 to 5 August 2026; Anji's opening, People's Daily Online Zhejiang (人民网), 28 June 2020, tier 2. http://zj.people.com.cn/n2/2020/0628/c186327-34116471.html; Hangzhou in 40 minutes and the station's distance from the county town, Hangzhou Net (杭州网), 29 June 2020, tier 2. https://hznews.hangzhou.com.cn/chengshi/content/2020-06/29/content_7764060.htm

Anji's Shanghai link is new. The line from Hongqiao through Suzhou to Huzhou opened on 26 December 2024, and its first train left Hongqiao at 10:00 and reached Anji at 11:32. Anji had been told the trip to Shanghai, over two hours before, would shrink to about an hour. That first run took 92 minutes.

> The first train and the promised hour, China Daily (中国日报网), 27 December 2024, tier 2. https://zj.chinadaily.com.cn/a/202412/27/WS676e1112a310b59111dab20f.html

We couldn't find a published count of daily trains between Shanghai and Anji, so check the timetable before you build a plan around one.

The bigger catch is where Anji's station sits. It stands outside Tianzihu (天子湖镇), in the north of the county, 24 km from the county town, while the Bamboo Sea and the Cloud Grassland are down in the south. Without a car or a hired driver, a good part of your day goes on getting from one to the other.

Moganshan has the opposite problem. The station is close, but from 08:00 to 17:30 cars may not drive into the scenic area. Day visitors change at a transfer centre at Yucun, Fatou or Houwu and ride a coach up. Our [shuttle guide](/plan/moganshan-shuttle-system/) has the routes, and says why to plan on the last bus down at 17:00. Pick your train from the [live Hongqiao to Deqing timetable](/go/trains-shanghai-deqing/) with that clock in mind.

> The car rule and its hours, Tide News (潮新闻), 4 December 2025, tier 2. https://tidenews.com.cn/news.html?id=3315585; the three transfer centres, Deqing News (德清新闻网), 30 September 2019, tier 2. https://dqnews.zjol.com.cn/dqnews/system/2019/09/30/031939856.shtml

## Who should choose which

Money aside, the choice is mostly about how you like to spend a day.

Choose Moganshan if you're coming by train and want to stay put. Deqing station is 18 km from Yucun. The transfer coach does the climbing, and the walks start at the guesthouse door. It suits people who want old stone houses, quiet paths through bamboo and an early night.

There are drawbacks. The ticketed core is small and weekends fill. The coaches stop in the late afternoon too. Our [bamboo forest page](/moganshan/bamboo-forest/) says when the paths are worth walking.

Anji is for a different trip. You have a car, or don't mind paying for a driver, and you want things to do more than places to walk. The Cloud Grassland sits at 1,168 m, with a cableway, a cliff park of more than 30 high rides and a ski season. In summer the top usually feels 5 to 6 °C cooler than Hangzhou, Eastday reported. Anji also has more than 300 village cafés, according to the deputy head of its tourism bureau.

> The Cloud Grassland's altitude and summer temperatures, Eastday (东方网) on China Daily, 25 June 2026, tier 2. https://caijing.chinadaily.com.cn/a/202606/25/WS6a3cbecfa310d709c2fba050.html; more than 300 village cafés, Xinhua (新华网), 8 October 2026, tier 2. https://www.news.cn/politics/20261008/8c9efc40e56f43b98be9df334cc7aa4d/c.html

Anji asks more of you in return. The distances are long and the big attractions cost more. Nor is there one place that feels like the centre. Families who want rides will find more of them there; anyone hoping for a weekend without a driver will not.

![A wet stone lane between grey stone villas under bamboo, fallen leaves on the paving and a walker with an umbrella seen from behind far up the lane](/images/guide/moganshan-vs-anji-4.webp 'About 250 stone villas survive from the hill station years, most of them built from the 1890s on, and the lanes between them are the walk.')

## Doing both in one weekend

It can be done, but only with wheels. By road, Yucun is 29 km from Anji's county town and 49 km from the Bamboo Sea, and no bus between the two turns up in anything we read. Ask your guesthouse to book a car and driver for the day. None of the reports gives a day rate, so agree the price and the hours before you set off.

> Road distances measured on OpenStreetMap, BeyondBorder Group Ltd desk research, 18 October 2026.

Pick one base and treat the other as a day out. Sleep on Moganshan if you want the evenings quiet and the walks on the doorstep; the [rooms in the Moganshan resort zone](/go/stays-all/) are listed together on Trip.com. If most of your plan is the Cloud Grassland, sleep in Anji. That's the long drive.

The two stations also give you an open route. Come in to Anji from Hongqiao, drive across and leave from Deqing for the train home. Anji station to Yucun is 49 km by road. That saves doubling back, though it does mean a car for the middle of the trip.

Give it two nights. Each crossing to Anji's southern sights is about 50 km by road, and with one night you get two of those and a rushed sight in between.
