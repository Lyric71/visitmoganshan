# Image and video library

Start with what you came for. This library has no files in it yet, and no license text, request form or contact behind it.

Editors get the geography wrong more than anything else. Moganshan is a mountain in Zhejiang Province, China, inside Huzhou prefecture, in a county named Deqing. Hangzhou sits about 60 km down the road, Shanghai 200 to 240 km beyond it. Search for Moganshan images and the results fill with Moganshan Road, the M50 gallery quarter in Shanghai. After that, a plywood brand. So the first job of any picture here is to prove which Moganshan it is.

## Why the stock photography here is so thin

The English-language record on Moganshan is short and old: a New York Times feature in June 2011, a listing among 45 places the year after, TIME in January 2010, CNN Travel on one resort around 2011. Nothing in Travel + Leisure, Monocle, the Financial Times, National Geographic Traveller, Condé Nast Traveler or BBC Travel.

## What the library will hold

The subjects below are what the destination is.

![Photographic prints laid out in a grid on a pale table seen from above](/images/guide/trade-image-library-3.webp 'Landscape, architecture, transport and the practical shots nobody takes. That is the gap this is meant to fill.')

| Subject | What the Moganshan photos have to show |
|---|---|
| The bamboo forest | Over 92 percent of the mountain, nearly all moso, cut and dug as a crop. Stumps, thinned stands, working tracks |
| The stone villas | Chinese-eclectic, German, French, American and British styles, all in local mountain stone |
| The villages | Yucun, Laoling, Sanjiuwu, Houwu, Dazaowu and Xiantan, which is under 2,000 strong and carries roughly 135 guesthouses |
| The journey | Deqing station, the Y1 shuttle, styled as a Republican-era tram, the three transfer centers (Yucun, Fatou, Houwu) |
| The seasons | Four pictures of one slope: 24.1 °C in July and August against 1.4 °C in January, spring shoots, clear late autumn |

> Location, forest cover, villa count, village and guesthouse counts, shuttle route, transfer centers and temperature means, BeyondBorder Group Ltd primary research, 1 to 5 August 2026.

Traps come with it.

| Trap | What is actually true |
|---|---|
| Yucun | Caption it Yucun, Moganshan. Anji's 余村, the UN Tourism award village of 2021, is another county entirely |

| The villas | About 250 survive, most of them private homes. Nothing shot through a gate or window goes in |

[The stone villas](/moganshan/hill-station/the-villas/) has the etiquette. [The bamboo forest](/moganshan/bamboo-forest/) explains why the slopes look worked rather than groomed.
