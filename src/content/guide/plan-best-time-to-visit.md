# The best time to visit Moganshan

The mountain in question stands in Deqing County, Huzhou prefecture, Zhejiang Province, China, about 60 km north of Hangzhou and 200 to 240 km from Shanghai. Search results will also hand you Moganshan Road, an art district in Shanghai. Wrong Moganshan.

The best time to visit Moganshan is late April to May, or late September to early November. Avoid the first week of May and the first week of October, which are national holidays. If you act on one line here, make it the day of the week ahead of the month.

| Question | Our answer |
|---|---|
| Best overall | Late April to May, or late September to early November |
| Best for the cool air | July and August, booked months ahead |
| Dates to avoid | The first week of May and the first week of October |
| The choice that matters most | Monday to Thursday, in any month |

## Why the answer is not simply summer

Ask anywhere else and the answer comes back in one word: summer. That is historically correct. Missionaries built this place in the 1890s to escape Shanghai's heat, and it still works. July and August average 24.1 °C, some 6 to 7 °C below Shanghai and Hangzhou.

![A queue of visitors packed onto a narrow stone mountain path in harsh summer light](/images/guide/plan-best-time-to-visit-2.webp 'Summer is when the mountain is coolest and when it is least pleasant. Both things are true at once.')

Everybody else knows this, so you bid against the whole Yangtze delta for a bed. The shoulder months hand you most of the cool air and a fraction of the crowd. Both are promoted locally as shoulder seasons, which tells you the operators know summer is oversubscribed.

The published means cover January, July and August, against an annual mean of 13.3 °C, and the shoulder months sit between them. [Moganshan weather](/moganshan/weather/) has the figures.

> Temperature means and the summer gap, BeyondBorder Group Ltd primary research, 1 to 5 August 2026.

## What actually goes wrong, and how often

Weather and Moganshan crowds both spoil trips. They do not cost the same.

| What goes wrong | What it costs you | Fixable on the day |
|---|---|---|
| A wet or misty afternoon | A view, and slippery footing | Yes. Walk anyway, or swap days |
| No room at any price | The trip | No |
| A rate that has tripled | The budget | No, though avoidable in advance |
| A queue at the transfer center | An hour of your day | No. Capacity is fixed |

Bad weather is a bad afternoon. A bad date is a bad trip.

The occupancy record backs that up. Guesthouse occupancy across the area averaged about 55 percent in 2024, and the high-end segment 68 percent in 2025. Over the May 2024 holiday it hit 94 percent. An average near 55 against peaks of 94 means many empty beds somewhere. Set that beside the weekday split, where Mondays are notably quiet, and you know where.

> Occupancy, average and peak, BeyondBorder Group Ltd primary research, 1 to 5 August 2026.

## Month by month, with a verdict

The year itself, month by month. The verdict column answers one question: would we book it?

![Fresh yellow green spring growth against darker mature bamboo on a hillside path](/images/guide/plan-best-time-to-visit-3.webp 'Late April and May: new growth, long visibility, and none of the crowd that arrives in July.')

| Month | Why you would come | The catch | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| January | Half-price tickets, empty paths | Mean 1.4 °C, near freezing | Solitude, not a first visit |
| February | Half-price tickets, easing late | Cold, and quiet with it | Fine if you came to walk |
| March | Shoulder season opens, bamboo shoots | Unsettled | Worth it midweek |
| April | Mild walking, low volume | Fills up late in the month | In our window. Book it |
| May | Spring at its strongest | Week one is a national holiday | In our window, from week two |
| June | Green and empty, the heat not yet full | Humid | Better than its reputation |
| July | The 24.1 °C gap, the founding reason | Peak rates, peak crowds | Go, if booked months out |
| August | As July | As July, only harder | Only with a room already held |
| September | Heat falling, volume falling faster | Still warm early on | In our window, from mid-month |
| October | Clear air and good light | Week one is a national holiday | In our window, from week two |
| November | Crisp and cheap, few people | Short days against an 18:00 cutoff | Window closes early in the month |
| December | Cheapest and emptiest | Cold, villages close early | Go for the quiet |

Crowd levels are our estimate, not a forecast for your Tuesday. One row is a minority view: we rate June highly and almost nobody else does. The heat has arrived by then. The core season has not. The [seasons guide](/seasons/) covers what each one looks like on the ground.

## Midweek beats any month

The strongest lever you have has nothing to do with the season.

Moganshan sits 2 to 3 hours from Shanghai by road and about 1.5 hours from Hangzhou. That makes it a weekend market, and the split holds all year.

| Your dates | What it feels like | What it costs |
|---|---|---|
| Monday to Thursday | Paths and transfer centers move freely | Off-season and shoulder rates |
| Friday to Sunday | Full guesthouses, queues to transfer | Peak rates, in any month |

Put the two swings side by side. The year moves about 23 degrees, from 1.4 °C in January to 24.1 °C in summer, and you can dress for all of it. Take the same week and arrive Monday, not Saturday, and the mountain goes from full at RMB 1,000 a night to half empty nearer RMB 400. Our source gives those bands by season rather than by weekday, so read the size of the gap and not the two numbers. The day of the week swings your trip harder, and it is the one thing you control.

We know what we are asking. Traveling Monday to Thursday means spending leave days, or pulling children out of school. It is still the cheapest upgrade here.

You cannot take your own car into the scenic area. Everyone changes at Yucun, Fatou or Houwu, and those centers run 08:00 to 18:00. Capacity is fixed and weekends fill it. Read [getting here](/getting-here/) before you fix dates, since the shuttle timetable decides what your arrival day can hold.

## The two weeks to avoid

A Golden Week is a weekend that runs for seven days. The May Day and National Day holidays, China's two Golden Weeks, sit directly on top of the two best periods of the year. That overlap is no accident. Those are the weeks the whole country is free to travel. The exact dates shift each year, so check yours before counting seven days from the 1st.

![A completely full mountain car park with cars parked along both verges of the road](/images/guide/plan-best-time-to-visit-4.webp 'Golden Week is not a busy version of the mountain. It is a different and much worse trip.')

What a Golden Week does to the mountain:

| What changes | Over the holiday |
|---|---|
| Rooms | Gone, or past RMB 1,000 where March cost RMB 400 |
| Daily footfall in Yucun | 30 to 40 percent above normal |
| Transfer centers | A weekend's load, seven days running |

You will still get the mountain. The quiet is the part that disappears.

> Holiday footfall in Yucun, BeyondBorder Group Ltd primary research, 1 to 5 August 2026.

If your dates are locked to a holiday, book the room first. [Where to stay](/where-to-stay/) explains why the village matters more than the property when everything is full.

## Where our own answer is weak

Our answer has two soft spots.

The windows are narrow. Both together come to perhaps ten good weeks, and the guesthouses know it. Book four to eight weeks out, not two.

Then daylight. By November the 18:00 cutoff bites well before you feel like stopping. An October afternoon holds a walk and a late lunch. A November afternoon holds one of them.

## Winter, honestly

If that rules our window out, there is one more answer. Winter is the contrarian pick, with the caveats attached.

| What winter costs you | What it gives back |
|---|---|
| A January mean of 1.4 °C, close to freezing | Scenic-area tickets at half price |
| A bare mountain, not a lush one | Mid-range rooms at RMB 300 to 500, against RMB 1,000+ at peak |
| Villages that go quiet after dark | An hour's walk without meeting anyone |

The last of those is the one people underrate. No money buys an empty path in October.

> Room rate bands and the half-price winter ticket, BeyondBorder Group Ltd primary research, 1 to 5 August 2026.

Ticket prices conflict between sources, and so does the discount window. Confirm both at booking. [Tickets and entry](/plan/tickets-and-entry/) sets out the ranges instead of picking one.

## The order to decide in

| Decision | When |
|---|---|
| Weekday or weekend, and no Golden Week | Before anything else |
| The month, from the table above | 6 to 8 weeks out |
| The room, then the train. Beds are scarcer than seats | 4 to 8 weeks out |

If your dates cannot move, stop optimizing the month. Book the room the day you know the dates, and if anything can still move, move the day of the week. The rest of the [planning guide](/plan/) runs through entry rules, trains and costs.
