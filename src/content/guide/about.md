# About this site

visitmoganshan.com is an English-language guide to Moganshan, a mountain in Zhejiang Province, China, inside Deqing County in Huzhou prefecture. Hangzhou is roughly 60 km south of it. Shanghai is 200 to 240 km off. In Chinese the mountain is written 莫干山.

It is for travelers heading to China, or already there, weighing a few days outside Shanghai and Hangzhou. Tour operators, DMCs and editors want [the trade page](/trade/) instead.

This page is here so you can judge how much of the rest of the site to believe.

## Who publishes it

The missing field visit is why we describe hotels without reviewing them.

## How we handle facts

The method is more settled than the business model.

![Annotated research pages spread across a wooden table under a lamp](/images/guide/about-3.webp 'Every figure on the site is traced to a source and dated, or it is marked unverified and left that way.')

Every figure on this site traces back to a dated fact base. If a number is not in it, it does not reach a page. The same document underpins the destination fact sheet on [the trade page](/trade/).

That shows up as three habits.

### Where sources conflict, we print both

Scenic-area ticket prices are the clearest case. Two published sets circulate and they disagree.

| Published set | Booked online | At the gate | Season rule |
|---|---|---|---|
| First set | RMB 120 | RMB 130 | half price in winter |
| Second set | RMB 50 off-season | RMB 80 at peak | not stated |

> Both published scenic-area ticket ranges, BeyondBorder Group Ltd primary research, 1 to 5 August 2026.

We cannot reconcile them. So [the planning pages](/plan/) print both and name the conflict. Check the price when you book. Printing one set alone would make us look more certain than we are.

### Where a date is uncertain, we publish the range

Take the founding. E. N. Fletcher, an American Northern Baptist missionary, picked the mountain out as a summer escape from Shanghai. Published sources put that at any year from 1891 to 1894, and disagree again over who built the first Western villa. So [the Moganshan pages](/moganshan/) print the range instead of a founding year, and date the first villas to 1896 to 1898.

> Founding date range, first-villa attribution and the 1896 to 1898 villa dates, BeyondBorder Group Ltd primary research, 1 to 5 August 2026.

### When the figure does not exist, we say so

Neither Huzhou nor Deqing releases an inbound or foreign-visitor figure. It is the first number a DMC or an editor asks for, and we do not have it. We will not estimate it and pass that off as data.

> Absence of any published inbound or foreign-visitor figure for Deqing and Huzhou, BeyondBorder Group Ltd primary research, 1 to 5 August 2026.

You will also see lines that say, in plain words, that something is unconfirmed. They are not oversights, but flags on what the research could not confirm.

The same instinct covers the naming traps. Shanghai's Putuo District has a Moganshan Road, the M50 art quarter, 200 km from here. A station named for the mountain does not go near it either; you want Deqing. English results mix all three, which is much of why this guide exists.

## Why we describe hotels but do not rate them

Moganshan had about 1,000 guesthouses in September 2025, plus resorts. [Where to stay](/where-to-stay/) names them and explains what a minsu is, with published rate bands.

It does not rank them or name a best. We have not stayed in any of them. A rating written from a desk in Hong Kong is a guess with a number attached, and the number is what makes it look like research.

Every property gets the same treatment, whether it is naked Stables, the state-owned Moganshan Hotel or the Four Seasons announced for 2030.

That decision costs the site its most commercial pages. There is no best-hotel list, and no page built to sell one property, because we cannot write them honestly yet.

## Four things we do not do

Four commitments, and none of them has an exception clause.

- We do not sell rooms. No booking engine, no cart.
- We do not take a commission on a booking we cannot see. No affiliate link is live today, and the final affiliate policy is not settled.
- We do not accept payment for placement or for a link.
- We do not trade coverage for a press trip or a hosted stay. Nothing here has come from either, to date.

## Dates, because things change

Anything that can go stale carries a date. Ticket prices, train times, shuttle timetables, room rates and entry rules all move. China's unilateral visa-free scheme runs only to 31 December 2026 with no extension announced, the kind of fact an undated page gets badly wrong.

![A stone villa under scaffolding on a misty bamboo hillside](/images/guide/about-4.webp 'Prices, shuttle times and opening dates on this mountain move. Each page carries the date it was last checked.')

Every page ends with a last-checked line, and gets re-dated when a figure moves. [Plan your trip](/plan/) collects the time-sensitive items in one place.
