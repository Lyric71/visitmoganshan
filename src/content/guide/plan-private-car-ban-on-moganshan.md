# The private car ban on Moganshan: what 08:00 to 17:30 actually means

Can you drive up Moganshan? Between 08:00 and 17:30, no. Your car stops at
a transfer centre at the foot of the mountain and you go on by shuttle. The
Moganshan private car ban has been in force since 30 November 2018. No
English guide we could find explains it, so most foreign visitors meet it at
a barrier on a bend.

| Question | Answer, as of December 2025 |
|---|---|
| Can a private car drive into the scenic area 08:00 to 17:30 | No |
| Before 08:00 or after 17:30 | Yes, no permit needed |
| The exception during the day | A confirmed booking at a property inside the gate, plus a parking reservation made through it |
| Where the car stops otherwise | Yucun, Fatou or Houwu transfer centre |

The mountain is Moganshan (莫干山), in Deqing County (德清县), Huzhou (湖州),
Zhejiang Province (浙江省), about 60 km north of Hangzhou and 200 to 240 km
from Shanghai. Moganshan Road in Shanghai, home of the M50 art district, is
a street 200 km away with no barrier and no shuttle.

## What the rule actually says

The wording, as the provincial outlet Tide News (潮新闻) printed it in
December 2025, is short. From 30 November 2018, between 08:00 and 17:30,
vehicles entering the Moganshan scenic area must change onto scenic area
buses at a transfer station (换乘站). The reason, a staff member told the
same reporters, is flat ground: the mountain has little of it for car parks,
and the buses need room to turn.

> Vehicle transfer rule, 08:00 to 17:30 daily, in force since 30 November
> 2018, and the staff explanation, Tide News (潮新闻), 4 December 2025,
> tier 2. https://tidenews.com.cn/news.html?id=3315585

Two things follow. It applies to the scenic area (风景名胜区), the protected
core, not to the villages below it, where most of the guesthouses are. And it is enforced on the road, well below the ticket gate,
by staff with a sign that says turn around.

## The exception: a bed inside the gate and a parking reservation

Here is the part that trips people up, because it looks like a sales trick.
It is the rule. A guest with a confirmed booking at a minsu (民宿,
a guesthouse) or hotel inside the scenic area may drive in at any time of
day, on two conditions: the booking itself, and a parking reservation
(停车预约) made in advance. Staff put it down to geography: the properties
inside the gate are scattered, some far from any shuttle stop, and guests
arrive with luggage.

> Guests holding an in park accommodation booking and a parking reservation
> may drive in at any time, Tide News (潮新闻), 4 December 2025, tier 2.
> https://tidenews.com.cn/news.html?id=3315585

The reports don't say how the reservation is made. Every Chinese account we
read has the guesthouse handling it: you book, you send the owner your
number plate, the owner registers the car. No public booking page or
official channel name could be confirmed, so ask the property in writing.
Forum posts mention a per car charge on entry. We could not source that
figure at any tier, so it is not printed here.

![A parked car with a suitcase beside it on a narrow lane between stone walls and bamboo, a guesthouse gate half open](/images/guide/private-car-ban-on-moganshan-2.webp 'Only a property inside the gate can register your car. Ask which side of the line the bed is on before you book.')

## What happened in December 2025

The rule made the news because a visitor called Zhou Jia drove up for the
autumn colour on 1 December 2025 and was stopped on a switchback short of
the Sword Pond (剑池) area. Told she needed a guesthouse booking and a
parking reservation to continue, she called it tied selling. The story ran.

Two days later Tide News reporters drove the same road. Near the Linhai
Pavilion (林海亭), still 5.8 km from the entrance, staff stopped them too,
with the same message.

> Reporters stopped near the Linhai Pavilion, 5.8 km from the entrance, on
> 3 December 2025, Tide News (潮新闻), 4 December 2025, tier 2.
> https://tidenews.com.cn/news.html?id=3315585

The signs were there, telling vehicles bound for the scenic area to turn
round and change at Houwu. They are in Chinese, which is why the rule is
news to foreign drivers and not to locals.

## Where the car stops

There are three transfer points, one for each approach. The main one, the
Moganshan tourist transfer centre (莫干山旅游集散换乘中心), is in Yucun (庾村)
at the foot of the mountain. The other two are at Fatou (筏头) on the
provincial road S304 and at Houwu (后坞).

| Transfer point | Where | Route up, as reported in 2019 |
|---|---|---|
| Moganshan tourist transfer centre | Yucun, off Huangfu East Road | The back of the mountain, to Yinshan Street |
| Fatou transfer centre | Fatou, on the S304 | The front of the mountain, via Houwu, to Huating |
| Houwu transfer point | Houwu village, off the S304 | Joins the Fatou route |

> Three transfer points, their locations and routes, Sohu Travel (搜狐旅游,
> 权哥探店), 2 July 2019, tier 3.
> https://www.sohu.com/a/324235488_357973

The Yucun centre opened with the rule, with 500 parking spaces and a shuttle
at RMB 35 return or RMB 18 single down, the county's tourism account said in
December 2018. Whether that launch fare still holds is for a separate piece
on the shuttle system; until then the
[getting around page](/getting-here/getting-around/) has the board.

> Transfer centre opened with 500 parking spaces and a RMB 35 return shuttle
> fare, as announced by Deqing Culture and Tourism (德清文旅), 7 December
> 2018, tier 4, reproduced on 163.com.
> https://m.163.com/dy/article/E2EU8TUV0518SHA7.html

Parking at the two main centres is charged by the hour. The only tariff in
print is a 2019 travel report, repeated unchanged in a September 2025 forum
answer. No county price notice could be reached from outside China, so this
is what has been reported, not what has been confirmed.

| 30 minutes to 5 hours | RMB 10 |
| 5 to 10 hours | RMB 15 |
| 10 to 15 hours | RMB 20 |
| Over 15 hours | RMB 25 |

## Before 08:00, after 17:30, and the quiet weekday

The rule has edges, and the edges are useful. Before 08:00 and after 17:30
the road is open to anyone, no booking, no reservation. Leave Hangzhou at
06:30 and you are up before the barrier. The catch is that the ticketed
sights keep their own hours, so an early car buys a walk, not the Sword
Pond.

A softer edge exists too. Staff told Tide News that on quiet weekdays the
cut off for driving in can be relaxed to 15:00, while weekends and holidays
run the full 08:00 to 17:30. That is discretion, not policy. Don't plan
around it. The [news page](/journal/news/) carries each Golden Week
programme when it drops.

> Weekday relaxation to 15:00 at staff discretion; weekends and holidays
> strictly 08:00 to 17:30, Tide News (潮新闻), 4 December 2025, tier 2.
> https://tidenews.com.cn/news.html?id=3315585

## The hours that disagree

Three sets of times circulate and they are not the same thing.

| What | Hours | Source and date |
|---|---|---|
| The car ban | 08:00 to 17:30 | Tide News, December 2025 |
| The shuttle at Houwu | 08:30 to 17:00, last up 16:30, last down 17:00 | Tide News, December 2025 |
| The car ban, one forum answer | 07:00 to 17:00 | Autohome, September 2025 |
| The transfer centres, our own check | 08:00 to 18:00 | BeyondBorder Group Ltd desk research, August 2026 |

> Shuttle hours at Houwu, departing when full or every 30 minutes, Tide News
> (潮新闻), 4 December 2025, tier 2, and the 07:00 to 17:00 forum answer,
> Autohome (汽车之家), 22 September 2025, tier 3.
> https://tidenews.com.cn/news.html?id=3315585
> https://www.autohome.com.cn/ask/18981801.html

The 08:00 to 17:30 window was printed by a newsroom that had just been
stopped on the road, so it is the one this page uses. The shuttle window
inside it decides your day: a last bus down at 17:00 puts a day tripper off
the mountain before the road reopens to cars. The 18:00 figure on our own
getting around page came from a desk check of listings and will be
reconciled when the shuttle piece runs. Nobody here has walked the road or rung the ticket
line, 0572 8412345, which answers in Chinese.

## Who should drive, and where they should sleep

For a first visit staying in a village, leave the car. The train to
[Deqing station](/getting-here/deqing-station/) and a taxi or guesthouse
pickup covers the last leg. Inside the scenic area a car earns nothing.
A day trip by car does not add up either. The
[tickets and entry page](/plan/tickets-and-entry/) has the hours inside the
gate, and the [planning FAQ](/plan/faq/) covers what follows.

A car pays in three cases. You are staying inside the gate and the property
will register the plate. You are moving between outlying villages over
several days, a road job [the villages page](/moganshan/villages/) explains.
Or someone in the party cannot manage the shuttle and the walk at the top,
in which case a bed inside the gate is the answer and the car follows.

One question decides it: is the property inside the scenic area boundary or
outside it? Inside means the old hill station, the
few hotels at the top and a small number of minsu on the upper slopes.
Outside means Yucun, Xiantan, Laoling, Houwu and the rest, where most
foreign visitors sleep. Listings rarely say which. Ask the property
whether it can register a car for daytime entry. A yes settles it. A vague
answer means outside.
[Where to stay](/where-to-stay/) sorts the properties by type, and the
[hotels explained page](/where-to-stay/hotels-explained/) covers the named
hotels.
