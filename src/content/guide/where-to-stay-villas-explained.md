# Private villas and whole-house stays

The villa question here is an expensive mix-up. People book against the wrong meaning. Search "Moganshan private villa" and half the results are museum pieces. Nobody rents those out. The villa you can book is a modern guesthouse, taken whole.

Moganshan itself sits in Zhejiang Province, China, inside Deqing County of Huzhou prefecture. Reckon it at 60 km north of Hangzhou, 200 to 240 km out from Shanghai. Search the name and you also get Moganshan Road, an art district in Putuo, Shanghai, which is the other mix-up.

## One phrase, two completely different bookings

| When people say "Moganshan villa" | What it is | Can you sleep in it |
|---|---|---|
| The historic villas | Stone houses left from the 1890s hill station | Almost never. Most are private homes or leases |
| A whole-house stay | A guesthouse booked as a building, not room by room | Yes. This is the bookable one |

Tripadvisor adds a third. As of August 2026 it lists an attraction called "Moganshan Villas", and that is not accommodation.

> "Moganshan Villas" attraction listing, Tripadvisor, checked 5 August 2026.

No building carries that name. It stands in for the scattered stock of old houses, so two reviewers may describe different places. For the architecture, [the stone villas of Moganshan](/moganshan/hill-station/the-villas/) is the page. This one is about beds.

## Why the old houses are barely a place to stay

American Protestant missionaries started the hill station in the 1890s, and about 250 stone houses still stand. Then 1949 emptied them. Residents left, the buildings went to work units or were requisitioned, and they became housing instead of hospitality. The view from the road is the whole visit.

![A boarded up historic stone house with weeds through the terrace paving](/images/guide/where-to-stay-villas-2.webp 'Most of the famous stone houses are in this condition. Almost none of them are rooms you can book.')

> Villa counts and post-1949 use, BeyondBorder Group Ltd primary research, 1 to 5 August 2026.

naked Castle is the exception people run into: a house of 1910 by a Scottish doctor and missionary called Maitland, reopened as a hotel in 2017 with about 95 keys. It is close to the only historic villa on this mountain you can actually sleep in. [The hotel list](/where-to-stay/hotels/) has the rest.

## The whole-house stay, which is what most of these searches want

So much for the houses you cannot have. Ten beds is not a hotel. It is a converted village house with a family in it, which is why the building can be yours.

![The living room of a whole house rental with a long table, a wood stove and glazing onto bamboo](/images/guide/where-to-stay-villas-3.webp 'This is what a bookable villa here usually is: a whole modern guesthouse, taken by one group.')

| The structure | The figure | What it does for you |
|---|---|---|
| Guesthouses around the mountain | about 1,000, September 2025 | Plenty to choose from |
| Beds across them | roughly 10,000 | About ten beds a property |
| Individually owned | about 80 percent | An owner, not a revenue manager |
| Xiantan, the densest village | about 135 guesthouses, under 2,000 residents, 90 percent locally run | Where the price competition is |

> Property counts, bed capacity, ownership share and Xiantan figures, BeyondBorder Group Ltd primary research, 1 to 5 August 2026.

There is a catch. Whole-house is rarely a listed inventory type, so channels show rooms priced by the room, and no button says building. Pick the property, then write to the owner and ask what it costs with nobody else in it. [What a minsu is](/where-to-stay/minsu-explained/) covers how they run.

### Who this suits, and who it does not

1. **A family of eight to twelve.** What the ten-bed average was built for. One kitchen, one terrace, nobody on a sofa.
2. **A birthday, a reunion, a wedding party.** The evening is the point, and taking the building is the only way to get one you control.
3. **A small offsite.** Twelve or so is what one house absorbs. Past that you book a cluster, and [groups](/groups/) has that math.
4. **Two or three couples on one trip.** Often cheaper whole than split into peak-weekend rooms, though below six people that stops holding.
5. One couple is where it falls apart. You pay for eight empty beds when a thousand properties here will sell you a room.

The stock grew out of family houses and still behaves like them. Groups do well out of that. Couples mostly do not, and that is a fact about the buildings.

## Three things that make it harder than it sounds

None of the following will stop you. All three have stopped somebody.

### You cannot drive to the door

Private cars are not allowed inside the scenic area. Everybody swaps vehicles first. The three transfer centers are Yucun, Fatou and Houwu, working an 08:00 to 18:00 day.

> Scenic-area vehicle rule and transfer center hours, BeyondBorder Group Ltd primary research, 1 to 5 August 2026.

Work back from it. The run up from Deqing takes 30 to 40 minutes:

| You reach Deqing station | Against the 18:00 close |
|---|---|
| Before about 16:00 | Comfortable, with time to shop |
| After about 17:30 | Assume night one starts off the mountain |

Arithmetic on the published shuttle time, not a schedule. Not every village sits inside the boundary, so check the [villages guide](/moganshan/villages/).

### Dinner is on you

Chinese trade reporting keeps circling the same gap. These villages have nothing you could call an evening economy. No night market, and few kitchens open late.

Yucun, the hub village at the bottom of the mountain, has restaurants around its square and market. The rest hold close to nothing after dark, so self-catering stops being optional. Settle food in writing before you pay.

### Nobody publishes a price

This is the question that brings most people here, and no property on this mountain publishes a whole-house rate. Every one of them is quoted on request, against your dates.

What is published is the per-room band, so start there. Checked 5 August 2026:

| Per room, per night | Rate |
|---|---|
| Mid-range, off season, midweek | RMB 300 to 500 |
| Mid-range, peak weekend or Golden Week | RMB 1,000+ |
| The local trade's high-end threshold | RMB 1,500+ |

> Per-room rate bands, BeyondBorder Group Ltd primary research, 1 to 5 August 2026.

Say five rooms, though nothing in the record fixes that number, and off season the sum is RMB 1,500 to 2,500. An owner giving up the chance to sell singly may want more. Over the May 2024 holiday high-end occupancy hit 94 percent, so a Golden Week is not the week to push.

## What to ask before you send money

You would not ask any of these about a room.

![A smartphone showing a blank messaging screen beside a notepad on a wooden table](/images/guide/where-to-stay-villas-4.webp 'Whole house deals are agreed in chat and paid in advance. Get the bed count and the dinner terms in writing.')

| Ask | Why it changes for a building |
|---|---|
| Exclusive use, or the building minus the family's floor | Owners often live in, which is normal here and not what people picture |
| Bedrooms against actual beds | The two rarely match, and your headcount rides on the second |
| Kitchen access, and what time dinner happens | No restaurant to fall back on |
| Can they register a foreign passport | A directive of 24 May 2024 bars refusing foreign guests for want of a license, though refusals still get reported |

> Joint directive on hotel accommodation for foreign guests, Ministry of Public Security, Ministry of Commerce and National Immigration Administration, 24 May 2024.
