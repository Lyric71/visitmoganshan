# News and updates

This page logs what is changing in Moganshan, newest first. Every entry carries its source. The wider [journal](/journal/) holds the longer pieces.

Several things are moving at once on this mountain in Deqing County, Huzhou prefecture, Zhejiang Province: who can enter China without a visa, how the trains run, who owns the guesthouses, which brands are buying in. Hangzhou is 60 km from it, Shanghai 200 to 240 km.

Search for Moganshan news and Shanghai results come first. Those belong to Moganshan Road, the gallery street in Putuo District, 200 km east of here. None of it appears below.

## 17 February 2026: Canada and the United Kingdom go visa-free

On 17 February 2026 China added Canada and the United Kingdom to its unilateral visa-free list. That took it to 50 countries. Holders of both passports now enter for 30 days. No application, no fee.

Published advice lags. Some still routes British and Canadian readers into a visa application nobody needs.

> Visa-free country list and dates of addition, BeyondBorder Group Ltd primary research, 1 to 5 August 2026.

**What this means for a visitor:** with a British or Canadian passport, the trip needs a ticket and nothing else. American passports are not on it at any date, and take the transit route below. The [visa-free entry page](/plan/china-visa-free-entry/) has every country and date.

## 12 September 2025: Four Seasons announces a resort for 2030

Four Seasons announced a 90-key resort at Moganshan on 12 September 2025. The site is 23 hectares, given as Yu Village, and the opening year is 2030.

Then read the developer's name twice. Zhejiang Shangyin Cultural Tourism Development Co. sits under the Deqing County Cultural and Tourism Development Group, so the county government's own development arm is on the other side of the deal.

> Four Seasons Moganshan announcement of 12 September 2025, BeyondBorder Group Ltd primary research, 1 to 5 August 2026.

**What this means for a visitor:** nothing bookable. 2030 is four years out, no construction has started, and dates that far ahead move. Our [Four Seasons page](/where-to-stay/hotels/four-seasons-moganshan/) covers what was announced and what was left out.

## March 2025: Trip.com takes control of a 28-property group

Trip.com acquired 67.04 percent of Dalezhiye in March 2025. The platform that sells rooms here now owns some.

| The deal | Figure |
|---|---|
| Stake acquired | 67.04 percent |
| Properties | 28 |
| Rooms | 556 |
| Average rate | about RMB 1,300 |

Moganshan's beds are otherwise small and local. About 80 percent of the area's guesthouses have a single owner, and Moganshan Town counted about 1,000 of them by September 2025. Twenty-eight under one platform is a different animal.

> Dalezhiye transaction, ownership split and property counts, BeyondBorder Group Ltd primary research, 1 to 5 August 2026.

**What this means for a visitor:** more of what you book may belong to one company. At about RMB 1,300 a night those rooms are near the top of this market, where the high-end line is RMB 1,500. [Where to stay](/where-to-stay/) has the bands.

## 26 December 2024: a new high-speed line, though not to your station

The Shanghai to Suzhou to Huzhou high-speed line opened on 26 December 2024. At its fastest it puts Huzhou 55 minutes from Shanghai Hongqiao. The northern approach into Zhejiang got better.

![New railway viaduct piers crossing flat farmland towards a completed elevated track](/images/guide/journal-news-2.webp 'New line, new station, wrong mountain. The stop that carries the name is nowhere near it.')

It did not change the trip most visitors take.

| Station | What it is to you |
|---|---|
| Deqing, on the Nanjing to Hangzhou line since 2013 | The station to book, 63 to 80 minutes from Hongqiao |
| Huzhou, on the new 2024 line | Farther from Moganshan than Deqing |

> Line opening, journey times and station geography, BeyondBorder Group Ltd primary research, 1 to 5 August 2026.

**What this means for a visitor:** probably not your train. [Shanghai to Moganshan](/getting-here/from-shanghai/) has the timings and the last mile.

## 17 December 2024: 240-hour transit replaces the 72 and 144-hour rules

On 17 December 2024 China folded its 72-hour and 144-hour transit schemes into one 240-hour rule. Ten days, counted in hours, for 55 nationalities including the United States. It covers 24 provinces and municipalities, Shanghai and Zhejiang among them, with free movement across those lines.

![A quiet border control desk with a closed lane barrier](/images/guide/journal-news-3.webp 'The transit rule changed twice in thirteen months. It is the item on this page most likely to move again.')

All of it rests on one condition, a confirmed ticket onward to a third country. A round trip home does not qualify.

> Transit scheme, eligible nationalities and covered provinces, BeyondBorder Group Ltd primary research, 1 to 5 August 2026.

**What this means for a visitor:** on a US passport this is the route in, short of a visa. Shanghai and Zhejiang are both covered, so Shanghai to Hangzhou to Moganshan stays inside the scheme.

## November 2024: the visa-free stay doubles to 30 days

In November 2024 the stay allowed under the unilateral scheme doubled, from 15 days to 30, for every country on the list.

> Change in permitted stay, BeyondBorder Group Ltd primary research, 1 to 5 August 2026.

**What this means for a visitor:** two nights out here used to cost you two nights in Shanghai. On a 30-day stay that trade is gone.

## 24 May 2024: hotels may not turn foreign guests away

A joint directive of 24 May 2024 bars hotels from refusing foreign guests for lack of a license. Three bodies signed it:

- Ministry of Public Security
- Ministry of Commerce
- National Immigration Administration

Refusals are still reported. Two years on, the directive has not settled it on the ground.

> Joint directive of 24 May 2024 and reported refusals, BeyondBorder Group Ltd primary research, 1 to 5 August 2026.

**What this means for a visitor:** ask before you pay, and get it in writing. This mountain runs on minsu (民宿), small family-run guesthouses, so put the question to the property.

## The standing watch item: the scheme runs out at the end of 2026

Two entries above rest on one law with an expiry date. The unilateral visa-free regime is written only to 31 December 2026, and as of August 2026 no extension had been announced.

![A wall calendar in a plain room with one date circled in pencil](/images/guide/journal-news-4.webp 'The visa-free scheme has an end date on it. Nobody has moved it yet, and that is the whole watch item.')

| If you travel | What to do |
|---|---|
| Before 31 December 2026 | No action. The rules above apply as written |
| In 2027 or later | Check again before booking anything non-refundable |

> Legislated end date of the unilateral scheme, BeyondBorder Group Ltd primary research, 1 to 5 August 2026.

Nothing has been legislated past that date, so this page says nothing about 2027.
