# Moganshan noodles: the four shops the Chinese record names

The English guides to Moganshan we checked send you to a hotel dining room and
stop there. Few name a noodle shop. The Chinese record does. In 2024 the
provincial tourism department's media account and the county's promotion account
printed noodle shops at the foot of the mountain, with street numbers, and a
restaurant listing adds a fourth. If you are asking where to eat on Moganshan
without a booking, this is the list the written record supports.

Moganshan (莫干山) is a bamboo mountain and former hill station in Deqing County
(德清县), Huzhou (湖州), Zhejiang, about 60 km north of Hangzhou and 200 to 240 km
from Shanghai. It is not Moganshan Road in Shanghai, the M50 art district, which
owns the search results. Three of the four shops are in [Yucun
(庾村)](/moganshan/villages/yucun/), the market town below the scenic area gate
where the road up the mountain and the [Chiang Kai shek
path](/things-to-do/chiang-kai-shek-path/) both begin.

Nobody from this site has eaten at any of them. What follows is what dated
sources say. Most are promotion accounts run by government, and they get that
weight: they tell you a shop exists and what it is known for, not that it is
good.

| Shop | Where | Known for, in the record | Last dated mention |
|---|---|---|---|
| Xingxian Noodle Shop (杏仙面店) | 15 Huangfu East Road (黄郛东路), Yucun | pig liver tossed noodles | September 2024 |
| Aqingsao Noodle House (阿庆嫂面馆) | 9 Huangfu East Road, Yucun | stir fried pig liver noodles in a heavier sauce | September 2024 |
| Xiaohuozi Dry Tossed Noodles (梅溪·小伙子干挑面), Moganshan branch | 42 Yuxin North Street (庾信北街), Yucun | dry tossed noodles with a counter of toppings | May 2026 |
| Huiyi Noodle House (回忆面馆) | 27 Duihekou Street (对河口街), on the road in | pig liver, three delicacies and pork chop noodles | May 2024 |

> Three of the four addresses, the Zhejiang culture and tourism department's
> media account (诗画浙江游浙里) on Tencent News (腾讯新闻), 20 May 2024, tier 4, as
> published. https://news.qq.com/rain/a/20240520A00KC500

## Why the answer is noodles, and why before noon

Start with the bowl the county eats. Dry tossed noodles (干挑面), also called lard
noodles (猪油拌面), come with no soup. You lift and turn them with your chopsticks
until the seasoning at the bottom coats every strand. In 2011 Deqing News called
it the most ordered breakfast in Wukang, the county seat, and quoted a resident
who reckoned eight breakfast shops in ten could make it properly.

> Dry tossed noodles as Wukang's most ordered breakfast, Deqing News (德清新闻网), 29
> June 2011, tier 2.
> http://dqnews.zjol.com.cn/dqnews/system/2011/06/29/013927478.shtml

Yucun had its own claim. A 2018 account said that in 2011, before the street was
rebuilt in a Republic era style, pig liver noodles were one of the town's three
treasures, with sesame cakes and cloth shoes. The same writer found Yucun silent
by 21:00. Treat noodles as a morning and lunchtime meal, and settle dinner with
your host.

> Pig liver noodles among Yucun's three treasures in 2011, and the town quiet by
> 21:00, The Paper (澎湃新闻), 湃客 contributor column, 9 October 2018, tier 3.
> https://www.thepaper.cn/newsDetail_forward_2502086

## Where to eat on Moganshan for noodles: Huangfu East Road

**Xingxian Noodle Shop**, at No. 15, is described first in both accounts, and
Aqingsao against it. The county account calls it an old name everyone locally
knows; the provincial one says it has been handed down for decades. Its
signature, in both, is pig liver tossed noodles (猪肝拌面): liver sliced and
blanched, laid on thin noodles with spring onion and celery, a house chilli
sauce and broth. Beef and green pepper noodles are on the list too, the toppings
fried to order.

**Aqingsao Noodle House**, at No. 9, is described by comparison. Same liver,
stir fried (爆炒猪肝面), but in a heavier sauce, on alkaline noodles (碱面) with more
bite. "For those who like it strong," the county account says, "we recommend
this one." In May the provincial account reported spring bamboo shoot in the
bowl. Beef noodles cover anyone who will not eat liver.

Both accounts place Aqingsao "opposite Moganshan station" (莫干山车站). Go by the
street number. Moganshan railway station is in Wukang, about 20 km away, as [the
Deqing station guide](/getting-here/deqing-station/) explains, and a shop on
Huangfu East Road is not opposite it.

The county account came out of a vote. Its news app, 我德清 (My Deqing), asked
readers for their favourite noodle shop in 2024, and the write up listed eight
shops across Deqing, both of these among them, in no order. We don't rank them
either.

> Eight shops from the app's vote, in no order, with both Huangfu East Road
> addresses, Ai Deqing (爱德清), the county promotion account, on NetEase (网易), 2
> September 2024, tier 4, as published.
> https://www.163.com/dy/article/JB3GJODL0514AIVC.html

## The Anji chain on Yuxin North Street

Meixi (梅溪) is not a Moganshan name. It is a town in Anji County, next door,
where the brand's main branch is listed. The Moganshan branch is at 42 Yuxin
North Street, in Yucun. Its listing names pig liver in sauce, braised spring
bamboo shoot, dry tossed noodles and pork intestine. One review, dated 12 May
2026, calls it well known, with a queue at mealtimes.

That review is the most recent dated sign that any of the four is trading.

> The Moganshan branch at 42 Yuxin North Street and its queue at mealtimes,
> Ctrip restaurant listing (携程美食林), review of 12 May 2026, tier 4, cited only as
> a listing, never for a price or hours.
> https://you.ctrip.com/food/1011/154234561.html

## The one on the road in

**Huiyi Noodle House** is the outlier, and not in a village. The provincial
account puts it beside the S304, the road to the mountain, opposite the Duihekou
reservoir (对河口水库), "a little out of the way". It gives no distance from Yucun.
It recommends pig liver and pork chop noodles, three delicacies noodles (三鲜面) of
shrimp, fish, greens, shredded pork and dried bamboo shoot, and dry tossed
noodles with a fried egg, in portions it calls big enough for two.

Only that one account, of May 2024, names it, and nothing later confirms it is
still trading.

On a map app, search the Chinese name, 回忆面馆, rather than the street.')

## What the record will not tell you

Three things a visitor wants to know, and the sources leave out.

**Price.** No source we can cite prints the price of a bowl at any of the four.
The figures that circulate come from booking listings and unsigned guides, which
we never quote for a price. Read the board on the wall, and see [paying for
things on Moganshan](/plan/paying-for-things-on-moganshan/) for how to pay a
small sum.

Opening hours appear only on listings, so we print none; the dated record says
breakfast and lunch, which means going before noon. Nor can we promise a shop is
still open. The table gives each one's last dated mention, and on the morning a
recent review on a map app or on Dianping (大众点评), China's restaurant review
platform, settles it.

**A name that misleads.** A shop called Yucun Noodles may not be in Yucun. A
Hangzhou subdistrict account reported that the son of the woman who opened what
it calls the town's first noodle shop, on Yucun square, now runs Yu Village
Noodles (于村面馆) in Wukang, and since 1 April 2026 a Hangzhou branch, 庾村面馆·山河
(Yucun Noodles, Mountains and Rivers).

> The Wukang shop, and the Hangzhou branch opened on 1 April 2026, the Shangtang
> subdistrict account (寻"糖"记) on Sohu (搜狐), 10 April 2026, tier 4, as published.
> https://www.sohu.com/a/1007541664_121123792

## If you pass through Wukang

The county's noodle street isn't on the mountain. Baile Street (百乐街) in Wukang
runs under 500 m and holds 10 to 20 noodle shops. Over May Day 2026 one, a fish
noodle shop, sold more than 200 bowls a day, and a visitor from Shanghai told
the reporter he had spent half a day in Yucun, then come down for Wukang's
mutton noodles. Arriving late, or leaving by Wukang? Eat there.

> Baile Street under 500 m with 10 to 20 noodle shops, and more than 200 bowls a
> day at one over May Day, China News Service (中国新闻网), 6 May 2026, tier 2.
> https://www.zj.chinanews.com.cn/jzkzj/2026-05-06/detail-ihfefuir8009492.shtml
